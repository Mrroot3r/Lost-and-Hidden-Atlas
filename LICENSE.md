## What this covers

- The places: their names, pins, kinds, write-ups, access notes and the other recorded facts (the files under `source/tools/`
  that begin `data_`, `bricks_`, `hours_` and `legos_`, and what is built from them, including the spreadsheet and map-file exports).
- The research notes, the guides and the leads list.
- The Atlas's own code: the app and the tools.

Each place keeps a permanent `id`. If you match your records to it, links between the two keep working.

## What it does not cover

These are in or beside the Atlas under their owners' terms, which this licence does not change:

- **Photographs.** Each belongs to its photographer and is used from Wikimedia Commons under the licence shown with it.
  The picture lists (`pics_` files, `photos.json`) carry the credit and licence for each one.
- **Historic markers and old town centres** (the "historic pockets" layer): titles and positions from HMdb.org, for
  non-commercial use, credited to HMdb.org.
- **Old rail, trolley and canal lines, and the unchecked leads layer:** OpenStreetMap contributors and OpenHistoricalMap,
  under the Open Database License (ODbL).
- **Map tiles, aerial photographs, LiDAR shading and old survey maps:** the agencies and services named under "Data credits"
  in `README.md`.
- **Leaflet** (`leaflet.js`): its own BSD 2-Clause licence.
- **Vanished places, old mines, public land and the National Register lists:** United States government records, in the
  public domain.
