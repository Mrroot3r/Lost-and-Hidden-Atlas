## What this batch is

Thirteen historic state hospitals, schools and asylum sites in Georgia, Alabama, Mississippi, Louisiana, Arkansas and Florida, a batch of the themed pass on state hospitals: Central State Hospital's Powell Building and Cedar Lane Cemetery (Milledgeville), Gracewood (Augusta), Bryce Main and Bryce Hospital Cemetery No. 2 (Tuscaloosa), the Mississippi State Lunatic Asylum site and Asylum Hill (Jackson), Mississippi State Hospital (Whitfield), East Mississippi State Hospital (Meridian), Ellisville State School, the Center Building of East Louisiana State Hospital (Jackson, La.), Central Louisiana State Hospital (Pineville), the Arkansas State Hospital site (Little Rock, gone) and the Apalachicola Arsenal officers' quarters at Florida State Hospital (Chattahoochee). Searcy Hospital was already on the map with the Mount Vernon Arsenal. Sources: Wikipedia, Park Service Register records and forms, New Georgia Encyclopedia, Georgia Trust, SAH Archipedia, Encyclopedia of Alabama, Encyclopedia of Arkansas, 64 Parishes, the hospitals' and states' own pages, HMdb marker transcriptions, Clio, Historic Augusta and news reports. Where institutions kept Black patients apart, the entries say so from the sources (Milledgeville, Bryce and its Northport farm colony, Whitfield's two campuses, Jackson La., Pineville founded for Black patients, the Arkansas farms). Not checked: whether the Walker, Green and Jones buildings at Milledgeville have been demolished since early 2024; what survives of East Mississippi's 1885 building, of Gracewood's pre-1921 buildings and of Ellisville's early buildings; the fate of the old Pineville campus after June 2024; the exact positions of Rose Cottage, the Chattahoochee officers' quarters and Whitfield's Administration Building on the aerial; and whether Florida State Hospital kept Black patients separately.

## Tips

- form.py returns only photo captions for several Southern Register forms (Bryce, the Louisiana Center Building, Chattahoochee); the Park Service's other asset for the same listing, found by a web search for the name plus "npgallery", often holds the full text.
- osmnear.py found no named buildings on these campuses; the Register list pages on Wikipedia give usable points for each listed building.
- Several Register points for buildings on Southern hospital campuses sit under trees; a look.py tile at half 90 m helps but did not settle Rose Cottage or the Chattahoochee officers' quarters.
