# pics_kjzq: pictures job

Pictures only: no places, pins or text changed. About 40 places were looked at in five rounds of `photo_find.py next 5` (Commons logged in for most rounds; one round ran without the login and was slow).

- Picture chosen: 9 (Guthrie Santa Fe Depot, Baker City Carnegie library, Rutland Prison Camp, Old Dowlin Mill, Mogollon, Port Townsend City Hall, Old Cahawba, Old Scott County Jail, Builders' House site in Old Salem).
- Said none by the session: 16 (candidates were interiors, plaques, signs, maps, lichen close-ups, or other towns' buildings).
- Recorded none by the tool itself: about 20 (nothing on Commons, or nothing new since an earlier look).

Hesitations:
- rutland-prison-camp: only 1934 survey photographs of the camp when it stood (the place is ruins now); chose the general view.
- mogollon-nm: a street view of the historic district, not one building.
- old-cahawba: the Crocheron columns, the best-known ruin at the site.
- builders-house-site-salem: the glass-roofed Lot 81 archaeological site, 32 m from the pin.
- greenwich-village-historic-district: only a war memorial and a cyclist were offered; none.

Tips: a `next 5` round took 12 minutes when the Commons login failed (JSONDecodeError) and about 2 minutes when it worked.
