## What this batch is

51 Carnegie library buildings in Wisconsin, public and college, standing, moved and gone, as part of the owner's pass to put every building Andrew Carnegie paid for on the map. Superior's main library (1204 Hammond Avenue) was already on the map and was left alone. Sources: Wikipedia's state list and articles, Wisconsin Historical Society property and National Register records, the Wisconsin Library Heritage Center, Clio, county historical societies, local papers and radio, and the libraries' own pages for hours.

## What was not checked

- Hours for most working libraries and every museum: not on the pages opened, so `hours='?'`. Hours given only for Baraboo, Cumberland, Edgerton and Washburn, from their own sites.
- Wall material for most: `brick='?'` unless a source named it. Tomah red brick; Durand and Reedsburg part red brick; Beloit College, Columbus, Eau Claire, Medford, Sheboygan, Sturgeon Bay, Two Rivers (cream brick) and Washburn not red brick. Racine's main library: one page says stone, another red brick.
- Present use not found: Madison Sixth Ward branch, Reedsburg's old library. Hudson: still the library (Wikipedia, state record) or not (an older survey). Kaukauna: still the library (state record) or apartments (a 2026 radio story).
- Reedsburg, Sheboygan, Two Rivers, Rice Lake, Madison, Richland Center, Stevens Point and Lawrence College pins are near, not on a building.
- Ladysmith and Sturgeon Bay: sources name the private owners; left out. Superior East End is a private home: look from the street only.
