## What this batch is
Eighteen places in Watertown and southern Jefferson County, drawn at random by `choose.py --state ny` (region `nny`, which offered Adams); the Atlas had only the Paddock Arcade and the roundhouse ruins in Watertown and nothing within 15 km of Adams. Watertown: the Flower Memorial Library, the courthouse complex, the Paddock Mansion (Jefferson County Historical Society museum), Trinity and St. Paul's Episcopal churches, the Masonic Temple, the Emma Flower Taylor Mansion, Thomas Memorial AME Zion Church, the Public Square district and the Olmsted-designed Thompson Park. South of the city: the Adams commercial district and the Smith-Ripley House in Adams, the Pierrepont Manor complex with Zion Church, the 1818 Norton-Burnham House (Daniel Burnham's birthplace) and the Oliver/Cyrus Bates stone house in Henderson, the Dr. Samuel Guthrie House (chloroform, 1831) and the 1825 Bedford Creek stone arch bridge in Hounsfield, and the Battle of Big Sandy site in the Lakeview Wildlife Management Area. Sources: Wikipedia through `wiki_raw.py`, HMdb markers, the Norton-Burnham and Hounsfield Multiple Resource Area Register forms (NPGallery text), historypointer.com summaries for addresses, the Jefferson County Historical Society, the City of Watertown's library and parks pages and its 2023 Thompson Park Master Plan, the National Park Service and TCLF on Thompson Park, the Preservation League of NYS and TAUNY on Thomas Memorial, 6sqft on the Taylor Mansion. Not checked: nothing was seen on the ground; the library's own hours (its hours page refused; only the genealogy department's hours are given); service times for Trinity, St. Paul's and Zion churches (no parish pages found); whether the Masonic Temple became an arts centre; whether the Ripley House museum still opens; which building at Pierrepont Manor is which (pin is on a large house that may be the manor); which house is Guthrie's (pin is on NY 75 between houses, prec area); DEC rules at the Big Sandy boat launch; Thompson Park hours. Most Watertown and Adams Register forms were NPGallery placeholders.
