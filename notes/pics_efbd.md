# Pictures batch efbd (2026-11-22, scheduled pictures run)

What the batch is: 35 places already on the map with no picture were taken in turn by `tools/photo_find.py next 5`. 24 of them had candidates and were looked at by eye; 11 had nothing on Wikimedia Commons and were recorded as none by the tool. No place, pin or text was added. The pictures file is `tools/pics_efbd.py`.

Counts:
- Pictures chosen: 18.
- Said none after looking: 6 (Canonchet stable ruins, Center Furnace, Laurel House site, Eagle Tavern site, Bloomington (Kansas), Bowness House).
- Recorded as none by the tool itself: 11 (Throckmorton County Jail, Pine Top ski area, Livermore Falls, LeQuille Mill, Brudenell Island cemetery, Kingstone Lisle, Upper Wakarusa crossing, Fort Wooster, St. Paul's Lutheran Berne, Lidar Huckleberry Kingdom Road cellar, Van Eps cement mill).

Where I hesitated (a likely picture was chosen, but a person should look):
- Robertsdale coal town (PA): a small building beside the East Broad Top line, not a company house; the mine buildings are not in the picture.
- Nassau village (NY): a Victorian house in the Church Street historic district, not the Academy or the Chatham Street row.
- Glenn Springs (Big Bend, TX): an NPS view of the spring site, with no ruins in frame.
- Grafton (IL): a limestone house on East Main Street about 800 m from the pin, chosen for the stone; not the ice house or the wharf.
- Hoosac Tunnel (MA): a c.1900s postcard photograph of the west portal, not a recent view.
- Fort Andrews (Peddocks Island): an aerial view of the island with the fort's remains on the east end; the fort itself is hard to see.
- Gran Quivira (NM): an old slide of a cross in front of mission church walls; it is not certain which ruin it is.
- Trinity Court House (NL): a three-storey building with cupola, named exactly in the file; its roof was not checked against the description.
- Lunenburg old town (NS): a street of coloured buildings, not one building.
- Hunt's Mills (RI), Joliet Iron Works (IL), Boise Basin Museum (ID): chosen from the place's own name and the picture's address; the Boise choice is an outside wall, not the interior photograph that was also on the sheet.
- Christ Church, Rugby (TN): the candidate was named by the file, 119 m from the pin.

Not checked: the aerial and street pictures were not compared with a second source for the place's exact building.
