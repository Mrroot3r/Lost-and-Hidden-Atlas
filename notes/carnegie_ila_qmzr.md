## What this batch is

Illinois's Carnegie libraries for towns A to L, plus three college buildings: Monmouth (Poling Hall), North Central (Carnegie Hall) and Shurtleff (the dental school's biomedical library, Alton). The University of Chicago's Harper Library, which Carnegie helped pay for, was already on the map from the Rockefeller pass. These are 38 places from Wikipedia's state list that could be placed from pages read in this session.

Eleven of them are gone or hidden, and each pin is on the site:
- Brookfield
- Chicago Heights
- Decatur
- Des Plaines
- Downers Grove
- Evanston
- Grayville
- Highland Park
- Aurora: hidden inside a 1960s building

Hours come only from the operator's own page. Where none was read, the place says hours='?'.
