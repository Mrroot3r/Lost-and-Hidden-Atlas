# Pictures batch rrhz

30 places looked at: 10 got a picture, 20 got none.

Pictures chosen: Notre-Dame-du-Mont-Carmel, Licking County Jail, Fort Amherst, Warner House, Manresa Castle, Maison Laberge, Leicester DL&W station, St. Mary's Anglican (Elliston), Vieux couvent de Château-Richer, Ancienne église de Saint-Pierre (Île d'Orléans).

Hesitations:
- fort-amherst-stjohns: chose 4 (ruined emplacements with the headland); lighthouse views 3 and 6 also fit.
- manresa-castle: chosen by name only (Joe Mabel, whose description names Port Townsend); no position.
- ancienne-eglise-saint-pierre-io: chosen by name only, a wide exterior from the research group; file name names the church.
- vieux-couvent-chateau-richer: the description says brick; the picture looks like stone and cream trim, but the caption names the building and it is 17 m from the pin.
- tannery-prattsville: none; the only candidate was Pratt Rock carvings 196 m away, not the tannery.
- copeland-carriage-shop-beecher-hollow: none; only the covered bridge at 12 m.
- roxbury-depot and monument-lefebvre: none because the single candidate's thumbnail came through blank on the sheet and could not be seen. Worth another try next time.
- coleman-main-street: none; only single buildings and cars.

Tip: many places have no Commons photo within 250 m; those are skipped by the tool itself.
