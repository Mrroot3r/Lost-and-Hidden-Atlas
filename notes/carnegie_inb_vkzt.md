## What this batch is

Indiana's Carnegie libraries for towns L to P, part of the owner's pass to put every Carnegie- and Rockefeller-funded building on the map. 34 places from Wikipedia's state list that could be placed from pages read in this session. Logansport's burned in 1941 and the present library stands on its site; part of Noblesville's survives inside City Hall. Hours come only from the operator's own page; where none was found the place says hours='?'.

- Mooresville, Paoli and Pendleton: the address points did not fall plainly on one building on the aerial, so the pins are 'area'. - North Vernon: county offices (library) or city hall (list).
- Lowell: OpenStreetMap shows a martial arts studio at 512 East Commercial Avenue, matching the list.
- Princeton: the facts come from the library's own page, which was read on a temporary web host; the link may move.
