## What this batch is
Sixteen places in central and eastern Suffolk County, drawn at random by `choose.py --state ny` (region `nyc`, which offered Riverhead); `start.py` showed no Atlas place within 25 km of Riverhead and none in eastern Brookhaven. All standing. Riverhead town: the Big Duck (Flanders), the Vail-Leavitt Music Hall (sold to The Jazz Loft in March 2026 for restoration), the Suffolk County Historical Society museum, Hallockville Museum Farm (Hallock Homestead, 1765) and the Jamesport Meeting House (1731). Southampton town: the Shinnecock Canal and its lock, and the Quogue Life-Saving Station (private house). Brookhaven town: the Marconi station building at Rocky Point with the story of RCA's Radio Central, Wardenclyffe (Tesla's lab, burned November 2023, new visitor centre July 2026), the Longwood Estate at Ridge, the William Floyd Estate (NPS), the Terry-Ketcham Inn (1693), the Davis Town Meeting House at Coram, and in Yaphank the Homan-Gerard house and mill site, the 1871 almshouse barn at the Suffolk County Farm and St. Andrew's church (1853). Sources: Wikipedia and the three towns' Register lists (`wiki_raw.py`), and with `page.py` the pages of Suffolk County Parks, NPS, NYS DEC, Cornell Cooperative Extension, the historical society, Hallockville, the Jamesport trust, the Ketcham Inn Foundation and the Yaphank Historical Society, HMdb and Pomeroy marker pages, and local news. Hours were read from the managing body's page for the Big Duck (a 2024 monthly notice), the Floyd Estate and the County Farm; the historical society's page gave only a Wednesday 10 a.m. reopening; Hallockville gave events only (`hours='?'`); the Tesla Science Center's tours page answered 403, so Wardenclyffe is view only. Not checked: anything on the ground, interiors, parking except where a mapped lot was seen on the aerial, St. Andrew's service times, Longwood's opening times (no Town of Brookhaven page found). Pins that mark the grounds or a nearby point and not the building: Longwood (park point in trees), Homan-Gerard (Register point by the pond), St. Andrew's (geocoder point near the church, which is hidden by trees), Shinnecock Canal (on the railroad bridge, not the lock), the almshouse barn (one of several barns).
