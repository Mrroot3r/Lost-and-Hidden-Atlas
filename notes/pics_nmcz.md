Job 8 (pictures): 30 places already on the map with no photograph, taken five at a time from `photo_find.py list --n 30`. No new places, no pins, no text about places. Every picture below was chosen by looking at its sheet; the address, credit and licence come from Wikimedia Commons through the tool.

Result: 13 places got a picture, 17 got none.

- lock-33: the Commons file is named "Old Erie Canal Lock 33" while the place is the enlarged double lock. Taken as the same lock at 131 m from the pin.
- runaway-pond-glover: only a by-name view. Chosen the wide view of the former pond bed with its sign; the two stereo views show the valley, not the pond.
- raymond-depot: by-name view (Raymond Depot NH) with a small cart in front. The 43 m candidate shows a locomotive, not the depot.
- fairbank-az: the townsite has no single building; chosen the parking-area view of the townsite buildings at 50 m.
- lac-la-biche-mission: the mission sign fills the foreground; the church and buildings behind it are the subject. Chosen over the 1930 archive photo.
- fayette-county-courthouse-la-grange: the 0 m candidate is the old jail next door, not the courthouse; chosen the courthouse at 11 m.
- robbins-house-concord: the house is small and in the background, with interpretive signs in the foreground.
- narbonne-house-salem: two by-name candidates show the same house; chosen the one captioned Narbonne House.
- Places with no picture because Commons had nothing within 150 to 400 m of the pin, or only a namesake or a neighbouring building: bank-of-sumpter-vault (the candidates were railway trains), grace-church-waterford (a sign close-up and the town hall), summerside-united-baptist (the former post office 128 m away), lyndhurst-bridge (wet tracks, not the bridge), white-elephant-saloon-albany (the courthouse), minor-park-swales (signs and beetles).
