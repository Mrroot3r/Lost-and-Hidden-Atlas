## What this batch is
Five standing buildings in Schenectady taken from the names in one Old World Exploration video (the record is `source/videos/20250131_IokDMDSKt0k.md`; the video is only a tip). The video named 32 buildings; 10 were already on the Atlas (Schenectady is well covered), 5 are gone, and most of the rest are not named clearly enough to look up. Added: the 1926–1928 YMCA at 13 State Street (now apartments), the 1831–1833 Greek Revival county courthouse at 108 Union Street, the 1913 county courthouse at 612 State Street, St. John the Evangelist Church on Union Street (with Mass times from the parish's own site) and the 1911 building at State Street and Erie Boulevard that became St. George's Lodge No. 6 Masonic Temple and Club. Sources: Wikipedia as raw text, the New York courts' historical society's county legal history (PDF, text read), Schenectady HistoryForge, Buildings of New England, sjechurch.com and the Metroplex authority's statement. Not checked: the Masonic temple's Register form (the state's PDF answered 404 and had no archived copy; its 1911 furniture-store origin and five-story brick description come from search-result quotes of that form, so the entry is marked mixed), when the Masons left it, the county courthouse's visiting hours, and what St. John's is built of (one source says stone; a Commons photograph of a corner tower shows red brick: marked mixed, brick 'some'). Pins are on the buildings on the leaf-off aerial. No Commons picture fitted any of the five.
