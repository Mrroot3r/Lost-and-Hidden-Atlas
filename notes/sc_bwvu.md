## What this batch is
Nineteen places spread across South Carolina outside Beaufort County (which sc_vxwi covered), so the whole state gets a first scatter: in the Upstate, Poinsett Bridge (1820), Campbell's Covered Bridge (the last in the state), Hagood Mill (working 1845 log grist mill), Oconee Station (1792 stone blockhouse and 1805 Richards House) and the Old Stone Church at Clemson (1797–1802); in the Midlands, the Star Fort at Ninety Six, the Columbia Canal at Riverfront Park, Historic Camden, Rose Hill (Gist mansion, Union) and Redcliffe (Beech Island); in the Pee Dee, Old St. David's at Cheraw (1770–73 frame church); on the coast, Prince George Winyah Church (Georgetown, 1745–47), Atalaya (Murrells Inlet), Hampton Plantation house, Pompion Hill Chapel, the Biggin Church ruins, Old Santee Canal Park, Colonial Dorchester's tabby fort and bell tower, and Fort Moultrie. Sources: Wikipedia raw text (`wiki_raw.py`), the St. David's Register nomination (NPGallery text), and the managers' pages read with `page.py`: South Carolina State Parks (`southcarolinaparks.com/<slug>`; the slugs are short: `hampton`, `redcliffe`, `rose-hill`, `oconee-station`, `colonial-dorchester`, `huntington-beach`), NPS hours and fee pages for Ninety Six and Fort Moultrie, SCDNR's June 2024 Poinsett Bridge news release, the City of Columbia regional-parks page, oldsanteecanalpark.org, historiccamden.org/admission and pgwinyah.org's home page. NOT checked: anything on the ground; hours at Hagood Mill (hagoodmill.org is now a domain for sale), Campbell's Covered Bridge (gcrd.org page empty), Poinsett Bridge preserve, Old Stone Church, Old St. David's, Pompion Hill Chapel and Biggin (no owner's page found; all `view`); Prince George Winyah's full service times (only a 7:45 a.m. Sunday service was listed on the home page; its sub-pages answered 404); who owns Old Santee Canal Park and which canal works show there; what remains of Campbell's mill. Pins: Hagood Mill, Hampton Plantation and Pompion Hill moved to OpenStreetMap points after the Wikipedia points showed woods or lawn on the aerial (checked again after moving); Colonial Dorchester is site precision in woods (the tabby fort was not picked out; the LiDAR shows an uncertain square earthwork about 90 m south-east); Old Santee Canal Park is the park address, not a canal feature (area). Red brick: 'yes' for Prince George Winyah only (Wikipedia: English red brick); 'no' where the material is stone, wood, log or earth; '?' for the rest, including Fort Moultrie, Biggin, Pompion Hill, Redcliffe and Hampton, which are brick or partly brick but whose colour no source or picture opened here settled. Walnut Grove Plantation (Roebuck) was dropped: the Spartanburg County Historical Association's page for it answers 404.

## Tips
- `southcarolinaparks.com` uses short slugs (`/hampton`, `/redcliffe`, `/rose-hill`), not the full park names; `page.py --all` then shows the HOURS and ADMISSION blocks, which the default `-g` filter splits into fragments.
- `nom.py` finds SC historic buildings well where the Wikipedia coordinate is off (Hampton Plantation House, Pompion Hill Chapel, Hagood Mill were all 80 to 450 m off).
- A full `pincheck.py --lidar` run of 19 pins takes longer than the two-minute shell limit: run it in the background and read its log.
