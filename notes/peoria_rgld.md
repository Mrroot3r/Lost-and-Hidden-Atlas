## What this batch is
Thirteen standing buildings in Peoria, Illinois, and the old Peoria State Hospital grounds in Bartonville next to it, the first Atlas places within 15 km of Peoria. The names came from one walk-through video of the city's old buildings (the record is source/videos/20221109_ZjRVL4sJlkM.md; the video was a list of names only, never a source). Seven video names were added (the Madison Theatre, the YWCA Building, the Peoria Life (Commerce Bank) Building, Bradley Hall and Westlake Hall, the U.S. Post Office and Courthouse, the state hospital), and six more from the county's National Register list were added beside them: City Hall, the Hotel Pere Marquette, the Central National Bank Building, the GAR Memorial Hall, the 1856 Cumberland Presbyterian Church, and the Flanagan and Pettengill-Morron house museums. Eight video names were already gone and are listed in the record so nobody looks for them again. Sources: Wikipedia read as raw text, Clio, Cinema Treasures, Peoria Magazine, Peoria Public Radio and the City of Peoria's City Clerk page (City Hall hours). Not checked: hours of the two Peoria Historical Society house museums (its site would not answer), the Pere Marquette's own page, the state hospital museum's own page (news reports only), who owns the Madison Theatre now, and which downtown parking lot is nearest each building. Red brick was left '?' for most places; pictures settled it only for City Hall (red sandstone, so no), the Madison (some) and the Bradley halls (no). Nothing was checked on the ground.
