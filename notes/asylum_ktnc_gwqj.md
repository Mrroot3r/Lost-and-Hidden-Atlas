## What this batch is

Fourteen state hospitals and schools for the "feeble-minded" in Kentucky (Eastern State at Lexington, the Lakeland asylum site, Western State at Hopkinsville, the Frankfort school's cemetery, the 1937 Kentucky State Hospital now a prison), Tennessee (the Nashville asylum site, Lyons View in Lakeshore Park, Western State at Bolivar), North Carolina (Dix Hill, Broughton's Avery Building, the old Cherry Hospital campus built for Black patients, Caswell Training School) and South Carolina (the Mills Building and the Babcock Building). Sources: Wikipedia, the Park Service's Register records and forms, the North Carolina office's copies of the Broughton and Dix Hill forms, SAH Archipedia, Kentucky Historic Institutions, state and HMdb marker texts, city, park and college pages, and news. Not checked: what Western State (Kentucky), the Mills and Babcock buildings, Cherry, Caswell and Northpoint are built of (brick='?'); when the Lexington administration building was built and whether the pin is on it; which Cherry buildings still stand (from the aerial only); where the Nashville stone gatehouse is; which Caswell building is the 1912 Hardy Building; park and museum hours.

## Tips

- kyhi.org (Kentucky Historic Institutions) has a page for every Kentucky asylum, school, poor farm and sanatorium; WebFetch reads it.
- North Carolina's Register forms are at shpo-images.dncr.nc.gov/<county code><number>.pdf and files.nc.gov/ncdcr/nr/<code>.pdf; WebFetch reads both, page.py and form.py do not (form.py has no state copy for North Carolina).
- nationalregister.sc.gov no longer answers; South Carolina's records are at schpr.sc.gov.
- Esri tiles drawn for look.py and pincheck.py can sit 15 m apart for the same spot; re-run the pin check after every move.
