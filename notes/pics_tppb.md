# pics_tppb: photograph search (job 8, pictures)
tidied: 2026-11-26

30 places that had no photograph were checked against Wikimedia Commons candidates.

Result: 6 got a picture, 24 got none.

Pictures chosen: ofarrell-cabin-boise, winedale-stagecoach-inn, pipestone-commercial-district, georgetown-canal-locks-1-4, barton-chapel-robbins, fenwick-island-lighthouse.

Hesitations:
- barton-chapel-robbins: the only candidate was marked by name only (4 m from the pin); its file name and description name the chapel and Robbins, and it matches the brick chapel.
- winedale-stagecoach-inn: the photo is titled "Winedale inn complex"; taken 25 m from the pin and shows the two-storey galleried inn.
- georgetown-canal-locks-1-4: several views of lock 1 or 2; took a wide view of a lock with its spillway, not the construction shots.
- pipestone-commercial-district: a district, so a view of downtown blocks was chosen over single buildings.
- bull-durham-sign-goliad: square views only; no painted advertisement identifiable, so none.
- valley-falls-mill-ruins: candidates showed a railway bridge, canal and other mills, not the ruin, so none.
- mcadam-railway-station: only group photos of people or a distant street view, so none.

Most of the rest had nothing on Commons near the pin, or only unrelated photographs (insects, Hall of Fame interiors, concerts).
