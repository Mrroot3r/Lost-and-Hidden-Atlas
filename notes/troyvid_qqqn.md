## What this batch is
Four standing buildings in Troy, New York, taken from the names in an Old World Exploration video about the city (the record is source/videos/20251114_f9B1UNw9XEc.md). The video named 54 buildings; 24 were already on the Atlas, 10 are gone, and the four added here are the Caldwell apartment house (1907), the Quackenbush Building (1857), RPI's Pittsburgh Building (1909–1912) and the former St. Francis de Sales Church on Congress Street. Sources: the National Register form for the Central Troy Historic District boundary increase (16000367, read in full from NPGallery), the RPI Institute Archives' building history, Wikipedia's list of RPI buildings, and a Times Union article (2009) and an Evangelist article on the St. Francis de Sales sale. Pins are OpenStreetMap building outlines or a street address point, each looked at on the aerial. Not checked: any interior, the year St. Francis de Sales was built, whether that church is still used by the fraternity, and visitor parking at RPI. No Commons picture fitted any of the four.
