## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of North Dakota (11 places): 7 standing buildings (four still libraries or nearly so, Devils Lake's lofts, Minot's arts building and two university halls) and the sites of 4 torn-down ones (Bismarck, Fargo, Grand Forks and Fargo College). The sources are Wikipedia's state list and articles, the state's National Register survey of philanthropic libraries (npgallery 64500383), single nominations, Prairie Public's Dakota Datebook and the libraries' and universities' own pages. That covers every row of the state list.

## What was not checked

- Fargo's library site: the northeast corner of Roberts Street and First Avenue North (Prairie Public), Roberts Street and Second Avenue North (the city), or 625 Second Avenue North (Wikipedia's list). The pin is the list's coordinate, as 'area'.
- What stands now on the Bismarck, Fargo, Grand Forks and Fargo College sites was not found.
- UND's Carnegie Hall: whether the president's office moved in, as planned in 2018, was not checked.
- Devils Lake: the building's 2015 buyer is named in Wikipedia and left out here.
- Walls come from the sources; pictures were not looked at for them.
