## What this batch is
Twenty places in inland Columbia County, New York, the first pass there (region `hud`, drawn at random by `choose.py --state ny`, which offered Ancram, Hurley and Philmont; the Atlas had only Copake Iron Works between the Hudson shore and the Massachusetts line). Nearly all standing. Claverack: the Shaw Bridge (1870 double-span Whipple bowstring, closed and fenced), the 1767 Reformed Dutch Church and the 1786 first county courthouse (apartments). The Columbia Turnpike's two tollhouses: West Gate (stone, about 1800, Greenport) and East Gate (1799, Hillsdale, being rebuilt by Friends of East Gate in 2026). Ancram: the iron works site marker at the paper mill (1743 or 1748, sources differ) and Simons General Store. Copake Falls: Upjohn's Church of St. John in the Wilderness and the 1892 Methodist church that is the Roeliff Jansen Historical Society museum. Kinderhook: Lindenwald (NPS) and the Luykas Van Alen House with the Ichabod Crane Schoolhouse (county historical society). Austerlitz: Spencertown Academy, St. Peter's Presbyterian (1771), Steepletop and Old Austerlitz. Ghent: the Bartlett House. Chatham: the Crandell Theatre and Union Station. Clermont: the academy and St. Luke's; Linlithgo: the Livingston Memorial Church over the family crypt. Sources were read with `wiki_raw.py` (Wikipedia raw text, including Register summaries) and `page.py` (NPS, Columbia County Historical Society, Millay Society, Austerlitz and Roeliff Jansen historical societies, Friends of East Gate, Pomeroy Foundation and HMdb marker pages, Spencertown Academy, Crandell Theatre, Bartlett House, Bridge Design & Engineering, The Gossips of Rivertown, an 1878 county history on usgennet.org). Not checked: anything on the ground; whether the Shaw Bridge restoration has happened; how far the East Gate rebuild has got; Simons General Store's present use; owners of the West Gate tollhouse, Clermont Academy and the Livingston Memorial Church; hours of the Bartlett House, Old Austerlitz tours, church interiors. Brick was set from Wikipedia lead photographs opened with `brick.py look` (Dutch church, courthouse, Van Alen House, Bartlett House and Livingston Memorial Church red brick; St. Peter's has a red brick chimney) or from materials named in sources; four are left `?` (Ancram marker, Steepletop, Old Austerlitz, Crandell, whose "pink brick" is not red). Pins: St. John in the Wilderness is `area` (the church was not picked out on the aerial); Old Austerlitz was moved to the OpenStreetMap shop point; Clermont was moved onto St. Luke's (the town hall); Union Station and the East Gate pins sit beside the building, within `site`.

## Tips
- NPGallery's Register text (`.../NRHP/<ref>_text`) returned only its placeholder for every Columbia County form tried; `page.py` says so at once. Wikipedia's Register-based articles carried most of the detail instead.
- `page.py` prints only matching sentences; on a site whose page text is one long run (cchsny.org), the hours were in the saved copy under `research/pages/` and had to be read from there with a short script.
- `brick.py look` needs the place built into the site first: write the batch with `brick='?'`, build, look, then set `brick=` in the data file.
