## What this batch is
Eight standing buildings in Duluth, Minnesota, taken from the names in one walk-through video of old Duluth photographs (the record is `source/videos/20220422_6BEf859jj4U.md`; the video gave 13 names, one already on the Atlas as Glensheen, four not named well enough to look up). Added: Historic Old Central High School (now apartments), the 1905 Duluth Masonic Temple, the Fitger's Brewery complex, the three main buildings of the Duluth Civic Center (St. Louis County Courthouse, City Hall, the Federal Building), Tower Hall at the College of St. Scholastica, and the Duluth Commercial Historic District along Superior Street. Sources: Wikipedia read as raw text, the St. Louis County Register list, and the Register forms for the Civic Center (86003097), Fitger's (84001690) and the Masonic Temple (15000215), read as text. Hours from the owners' own pages read in a headless browser: the Fitger's complex, the City of Duluth's home page, and St. Louis County's list of its offices in the courthouse. Not checked: the courts' own Duluth pages (a bot check blocks them, so the courthouse hours are the county offices' hours), any visiting arrangement for the Masonic Temple or the Federal Building, the Fitger's brewery museum's own hours (only the complex's), parking everywhere (downtown street parking; the mapped lots near the pins were a roof and a plaza on the aerial, so none is given), and every interior. Pins are on the buildings on the aerial; the courthouse and City Hall pins were moved about 40 m onto the roofs. Brick: from the forms' words, and from Commons pictures for Fitger's, Tower Hall and the commercial district; the Federal Building is '?'.
