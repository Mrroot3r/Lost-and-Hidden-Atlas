# Pictures batch yhfy (scheduled "pictures" job)
tidied: 2026-11-26

Picked 30 places waiting for a photograph from `photo_find.py list --n 30`, looked at five at a time with `photo_find.py look`, and recorded one answer for each.

Result: 11 places got a picture and 19 got none (no free Commons picture near the pin, or none that showed the place).

Pictures chosen: Port-Royal Habitation (view from Annapolis Basin), Fulton County Museum, Fort Warren (from the harbour), St. Mary's Church (Albany), Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception (Harbour Grace), Trinity United (Mahone Bay), École de fabrique de Saint-François, St. John's Lutheran (Mahone Bay), Claverack Reformed Dutch Church, Stephen and Harriet Myers House, Busch Building (Sierra City).

Places where I hesitated:
- trinity-united-mahone-bay: two candidates are named Trinity United Church; I chose the white steepled church (panoramio, 78 m). The other named file shows a church with a square tower, so a person should check this one.
- cheese-box-factory-henry-buck: a Commons file names the Cheese-Box Mill ruins on the same trail, but it shows a stone wall in snow 309 m from the pin. Not clearly the wheel pit or dam, so none.
- silver-city-historic-district-idaho: the by-name candidates are other Silver Citys (New Mexico, South Dakota, Georgia, Mississippi) and a Nevada view. None.
- gold-hill-mines-park: candidates show a village street and a metal works yard, not the mines. None.
- livermore-nh: all candidates are other Livermores or unrelated subjects. None.
- troy-new-england: candidates are Averill Park street views and signs; no trolley trace in view. None.
- fort-warren: chose the 2017 view from the harbour (by name, 650 m); the visitor centre is in the picture but the fort walls are the subject.
