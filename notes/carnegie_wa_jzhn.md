## What this batch is

Washington's Carnegie libraries from Wikipedia's list (43 rows): 41 places, 30 standing and 11 gone. Tacoma's Carnegie library (`carnegie-library-tacoma`) and Port Townsend's (`port-townsend-carnegie-library`) were already on the Atlas and were left out. The list has no college Carnegie libraries for Washington, and none were looked for beyond it.

Sources: The list and its articles; the 1982 National Register nomination for the Carnegie libraries of Washington (grant amounts, architects, use in 1981, which were razed); the Seattle Public Library's own pages (history and hours); HistoryLink (University branch, Walla Walla); the Spokesman-Review (Heath branch); the Everett Herald; the Lynnwood Times (Snohomish); Walla Walla Public Schools; the museums' and the Lower Elwha Klallam Tribe's pages.

## What was not checked

- Pins were looked at on the aerial. South Bend (address worked out along the street) and Wenatchee (a street corner) are `area`; every gone library is `area` except Seattle's, whose block the 1982 nomination says the Central Library now stands on.
- Hours not found for Anacortes, Fairhaven, Centralia, Goldendale, Hoquiam, Ritzville, South Bend and the Ballard pub (`hours='?'`). Three Seattle branches were shut when read (Fremont and West Seattle for heating work, University for renovation until late 2026): look again.
- Brick: the colour was seldom given. Only Walla Walla (deep maroon pressed brick) is `yes`; Green Lake and University are stucco (`no`); Seattle's lost Central was sandstone.
- Current use comes only from Wikipedia's list for Anacortes, Auburn, Burlington, Olympia, the four Spokane buildings (Heath from a 2014 article), and Wenatchee.

- Disagreements kept in the text: Fremont's address (the library gives 731 N 35th St; the list 1501 N 45th St); Edmonds' last year as a library (1962 or 1982); Everett's funeral-home years; Ritzville's grant ($10,500 or $10,000); Pasco's architect; the Clarkston building's school use; Snohomish's last use before restoration.

## Leads not finished

- Ellensburg, Yakima, Prosser, Puyallup, Sunnyside, Sedro-Woolley, Renton and Bellingham's main library: exact lots not found. Prosser's demolition year: the list says 1974; the 1982 nomination counts one demolition in 1972 without naming it.
