## What this batch is

The Carnegie libraries of Minnesota in towns A to L: 26 places, from Wikipedia's state list and articles, Placeography's Minnesota Carnegie Libraries Tour (from the state preservation office's files, about 2010, so its "now used as" lines may be old), MNopedia and National Register nominations, with hours from the operators' own pages. Five are gone and pinned on their sites: Anoka, Austin, Benson, Fairmont and Graceville. The Duluth main library and the Lincoln branch are in.

## What was not checked

- Brick: from Placeography's and Wikipedia's descriptions only. Albert Lea and Luverne are red brick; Duluth Lincoln is brown and dark red (`some`). Most others are buff, grey or brown (`no`); the rest `?`.
- Hours were not found for Detroit Lakes, Glenwood, Hutchinson, Crookston (the historical society's archives) and Luverne (Carnegie Cultural Center).
- Crookston, Fergus Falls and Litchfield are `area` pins: the Carnegie building could not be picked out on the aerial.
- Alexandria's present use was not found (offered for sale in 2014).

## Leads not finished

- Hamline University's Carnegie library (1536 Hewitt Ave, St. Paul): only the 1907 entrance survives, inside a 1972 modern building, so it was left out under the no-modernist rule. A person could judge it.
- Grand Rapids (21 5th St NE): the Carnegie building is almost wholly enclosed by a 1938 front and a 1971 expansion; left out.
- West Duluth branch (torn down 1992): site not found.
- Hibbing (closed 1954, torn down later): site on 3rd Avenue SW not pinned down; its murals are at the First Settlers Museum.
- Lake City (Carnegie-Tryon library, razed 1966 or 1967): exact site not settled.

## Tips for the next session

- placeography.net has a page for nearly every Minnesota Carnegie library, gone ones included ("(Razed)" in the title), with wall materials and colours.
- Look-alike sites: alexlibrary.net is Indiana, hutchpl.org is Kansas, glenwood.lib.ia.us is Iowa.
- Dawson's present occupant is a law office named after a family; it is left out.
