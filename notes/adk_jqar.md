## What this batch is
Twenty-four places in northern and western Clinton County, New York (region `adk`, drawn at random by `choose.py --state ny`, which offered Champlain); `start.py` found no Atlas place within 20 km of Champlain and only the Kent-Delord House and the Old Stone Barracks in Plattsburgh. Plattsburgh: City Hall, the Macdonough Monument, Trinity, First Presbyterian and St. John the Baptist churches, the Strand Theatre, the old county courthouse, the D&H depot, Plattsburgh Barracks round the U.S. Oval (with the county museum and the War of 1812 Museum), the Fort Brown earthworks (Fort Brown Park) and Hawkins Hall. Rouses Point and Champlain: the Fort Montgomery ruins (private), the 1889 D&H station (History and Welcome Center), the 1932 Overton Corners border station, the Pliny Moore House and the Point au Fer "White House" site (markers only). Chazy and Altona, the Miner country: the Alice T. Miner Museum, Chazy Central Rural School, Heart's Delight Farm (Miner Institute), the 1805 Trombly log home, the Million Dollar Dam (permit from the Miner Institute) and the McGregor Powerhouse. Dannemora and Lyon Mountain: the Clinton Prison wall and the Lyon Mountain station museum. Sources: Wikipedia through `wiki_raw.py`, HMdb marker pages (texts and positions), the Lake Champlain Maritime Museum's 2019 battlefield report for the City of Plattsburgh (a PDF that `page.py` reads, 97 pages), and the own pages of the Village of Rouses Point, the Diocese of Albany (Trinity), the Diocese of Ogdensburg, the Alice T. Miner Museum, the Miner Institute, the Clinton County Historical Association and the Friends of Lyon Mountain; Adirondack Explorer for the Million Dollar Dam; WAMC for the Strand's money trouble. Not checked: nothing was seen on the ground; Mass times at St. John the Baptist (holycrosspbg.org answered 403) and service times at First Presbyterian; the War of 1812 Museum's hours (battleofplattsburgh.org reset the connection); the Strand's status after autumn 2026; Lyon Mountain's current hours (the newest on its blog are for 2015); Heart's Delight exhibit hours (the institute gives none); whether the Pliny Moore House standing is Moore's own (the marker says "similar to"); whether Fort Montgomery can be seen from a public road. Pins marking an area, not the thing: Point au Fer (markers), the Trombly log home (marker), the McGregor Powerhouse (marker at Feinberg Park), the Clinton Prison (marker on NY 374), Heart's Delight (the institute's Wikipedia point), the Pliny Moore House (marker). The Million Dollar Dam has no parking point. `check.py` warns that the Overton Corners pin is just outside the coarse New York outline; on the aerial it is on the US station, south of the border strip.

## Tips
- HMdb nearby lists (3 miles round Rouses Point, Champlain, Chazy, Plattsburgh, Lyon Mountain, Mooers) gave most of the batch; the marker inscription sits between "Inscription." and "Topics" (or "Location.") in `page.py --all` text.
- The City of Plattsburgh's ABPP battlefield report (`cityofplattsburgh-ny.gov/sites/cityofplattsburgh.com/files/city-historian/ABPP2887-16-009FinalDrafte311.pdf`) reads with `page.py` and describes Fort Brown, Fort Moreau, Fort Scott, Crab Island and Valcour Island as found in 2018.
- holycrosspbg.org (Plattsburgh's Catholic parish) answers 403; battleofplattsburgh.org reset the connection with no readable archived copy.
- `photo_find.py` offered a Texas photo (palm trees) for "Fort Brown earthworks": the Brownsville fort shares the name.
