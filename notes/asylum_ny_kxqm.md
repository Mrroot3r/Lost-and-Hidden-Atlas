## What this batch is

Twelve New York places of the state hospitals and asylums pass, none on the Atlas before: the Octagon on Roosevelt Island (New York City Lunatic Asylum), Buell Hall at Columbia (the Bloomingdale Asylum's Macy Villa), Willowbrook State School and the Willowbrook Mile, the Queens County Farm Museum (Creedmoor State Hospital's farm), Matteawan State Hospital (inside Fishkill prison), Middletown State Homeopathic Hospital, Letchworth Village and its old cemetery, St. Lawrence State Hospital at Ogdensburg, Gowanda State Homeopathic Hospital, Craig Colony for Epileptics at Sonyea (inside Groveland prison), and Brigham Hall, the private asylum at Canandaigua. Sources: Wikipedia's articles; the National Register records for the Octagon and Brigham Hall (neither form is digitised); WAMC and Untapped New York on Middletown; the Rockland County Business Journal on Letchworth Village; Livingston County's historian on Craig Colony; Hudson Valley Ruins on Matteawan; Empire State Development's 2025 Gowanda study and a State Senate release; the DEC notice, DASNY's 2024 finding and an Assembly release on St. Lawrence; Michael Kleen and Asylum Projects; the College of Staten Island's Willowbrook Mile pages; Queens Farm's visit page. Every pin was looked at on the state's leaf-off aerial. Not checked: which original buildings still stand at St. Lawrence after the 2023–24 demolitions, Matteawan's main building beyond a 2007 report, whether Willowbrook's buildings are the originals and what style they are, red brick nearly everywhere (sources say "brick" without a colour), and whether the Columbia campus and the Letchworth cemetery have set hours.

## Tips

- Wikipedia's articles on New York's state hospitals are mostly short; search the news for current status (closings and demolitions after 2020 at Gowanda, Ogdensburg and Middletown).
- State demolition notices (DEC's Environmental Notice Bulletin, DASNY's negative declarations) list building numbers, years built and whether the historic preservation office counts them as contributing: useful for what is still there.
- The Register forms for the Octagon and Brigham Hall are not digitised; form.py gives only the Park Service record.
