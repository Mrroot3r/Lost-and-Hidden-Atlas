## What this batch is
Five places: the Englewood Theater (1949) and the Woodson-Sawyer House (about 1866) in Independence, the Liberty Arsenal site and the West Liberty Courthouse Square district in Liberty, and Aker Cemetery near Smithville. This is a small batch because the Atlas already holds nearly every National Register listing in Jackson, Clay, Platte and Cass counties: Wikipedia's county lists, compared by name against every place list, left only a handful, and several of those are residential districts or houses with no article and no readable nomination. Sources were Wikipedia read as raw text, Missouri State Parks, KCUR, Kansas City magazine, KSHB, Northeast News and Cinema Treasures; the Woodson-Sawyer marker text and nomination form (HMdb and mostateparks.com answered 403 and 404) are taken from web search results that quote them. Not checked on the ground: all five. The Englewood's register listing date; which three shops make up the West Liberty district and whether they are brick; Aker Cemetery's owner, access and condition (the federal map puts the pin in the Smithville Lake recreation land); the Woodson-Sawyer house was not picked out on the aerial under its trees, so its pin marks the site. The Liberty Arsenal pin falls on the edge of an active quarry on the aerial; it is on private land and marks the general area. Pictures: one found (Woodson-Sawyer House); none for the Englewood and none fitting for West Liberty.
