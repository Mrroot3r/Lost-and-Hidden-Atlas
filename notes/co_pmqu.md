## What this batch is
Sixteen places spread over Colorado, which had 34: Amache (Granada), the Limon depot, the Cokedale coal camp and coke ovens, the Como roundhouse, the Healy House in Leadville, the Marble mill site, the Ute-Ulay mine and mill at Lake City, the Lewis Mill above Telluride, the Beaumont Hotel (Ouray), the Strater Hotel (Durango), Our Lady of Guadalupe at Conejos, Hope Lutheran Church (Westcliffe), the Museum of Colorado Prisons (Canon City), Victor's downtown, the Hotel Colorado (Glenwood Springs) and the Stanley Hotel (Estes Park). Sources: Wikipedia as raw text (`wiki_raw.py`, and `--rows` on the Park, Summit, Hinsdale, Lincoln, Gunnison and Conejos county Register lists); with `page.py` the NPS Amache pages, History Colorado (Healy House, Conejos church), the South Park Rail Society, HMdb (Ute-Ulay), Colorado Preservation, the Town of Limon, Visit Telluride, SAH Archipedia and the Denver Gazette (Cokedale), the Sopris Sun (Marble), the Pikes Peak Library District (Victor museum) and the hotels' own pages. Not checked: nothing on the ground; service times for both churches and whether Hope Lutheran is still active; hours of the Museum of Colorado Prisons (prisonmuseum.org reset the connection), the Victor Lowell Thomas Museum, the Cokedale museum, the Marble mill site park and the three hotels; whether visitors may now walk among the Ute-Ulay buildings (`conf='mixed'`); who owns the ground under the Cokedale coke ovens. Pins moved on the aerial: Limon depot, Cokedale (to a street; the ovens show beside State Highway 12 to the southeast), Como roundhouse, Ute-Ulay, Hope Lutheran (address lookup). The Healy House and Lewis Mill pins could not be checked (haze and cloud). Amache is `area`: the camp is large.
