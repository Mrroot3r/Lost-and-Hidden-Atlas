Where I hesitated:
- cohoes-locks-9-10-george-street-park: the candidate named "Enlarged Erie Canal Lock 9" is a masonry wall in the park; the candidate named Lock 10 showed mostly grass and a fence. Chose Lock 9. Batch zzkv chose Lock 10.
- hibernia-iron-works: the picture is the back of the mansion, 19 m from the pin. The forge ruins are not visible; the mansion is the main standing structure of the site.
- salem-tavern-smokehouse: the candidates all showed the Salem Tavern building itself or the street; the smokehouse foundation is not visible in any of them. Said none rather than show the tavern for a foundation.
- lester-park-lime-kiln: flagged LOOK TWICE (no position), but the file name and description both name Lester Park and a lime kiln remnant. Chosen.
- franklin-square-saratoga: chose a wide view of two houses on the square (3 and 5 Franklin Square) over the fountain and single houses.
- fort-miles: the barracks (T-603) are a surviving building inside the fort; the nearest candidates were a gun and armour plate close-up and a tower. Chose the barracks as the standing fort structure.
- memorial-square-summerside: the candidate named Memorial Park shows the park with paths and a cannon, 147 m from the pin. The drill-shed site is not visible. Chosen as the nearest wide view of the square.
- brick-kingdom-barton: the candidates show King Block, houses by the falls, and a 1965 car. The falls views show a mill wall and a dam, not the factory ruins. Said none.
- fort-point-trinity: all three candidates were Charleston, South Carolina postcards, a different place. Said none without viewing the sheet beyond its candidate names; the names are clear enough.
- lehigh-canal-lock-1-jim-thorpe: the Lock 25, Lock 28, Glendon and 1970 Keck's Park lock photographs are other locks. Said none.
- st-peters-harbour-lighthouse: chose the clearest close view of the whole tower (candidate 8). Several other frames of the same tower were nearly as good.
