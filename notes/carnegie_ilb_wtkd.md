## What this batch is

Illinois's Carnegie libraries for towns M to Z. These are 40 places from Wikipedia's state list that could be placed from pages read in this session.

Four of them are gone, and each pin is on the site:
- Morris
- Pekin: a plaza keeps one of its entrance lamps
- Rockford: torn down in 2018
- Springfield's Lincoln Library

St. Charles survives inside the present library. Hours come only from the operator's own page. Where none was read, the place says hours='?'.

## Not checked

- Brick: Oregon is 'some' (buff brick with red bands, per Wikipedia). Rockford's grey brick and Sycamore's red sandstone are 'no'. The rest are '?'.
- Moline: a radio story names the private owner, and that name is left out here.
- Maywood: whether the Carnegie building is still whole inside the 1994 expansion is not settled. Its hours came from the village page, not the library's own, so hours='?'.
- Mount Carmel ("an attorney's office") and Park Ridge ("a beauty salon"): these uses are from Wikipedia's list only.
- Sterling, Sheffield and Wyoming: the addresses are from the list.
- Ridge Farm, Savanna and Waverly: hours were on town pages, not the library's own, and Waverly's page gave two schedules. All three are left at hours='?'.
- Olney: trees hide the building on the aerial. OpenStreetMap puts the museum at the same point.
- Sheffield and Vienna pins are marked as near, not exact.

## Leads not finished

- Milford, Onarga, Petersburg, Pittsfield, Robinson, Sheldon, Taylorville (the list says a law office), Toulon, Warren: nothing settled. Lookalike pages exist for Milford in Indiana, Warren in Indiana, Sheldon in Iowa, and Marion and Morris elsewhere.
- Peru: used until 1986, on Putnam Street. Whether it stands was not found.
- Rushville: the new library opened in 2014. The old building's fate was not found.
- Tuscola: the library is at the list's address, but whether that is the 1903 building was not confirmed.
- Wilmette: torn down in 1951, and the present library stands on the site. The street address was not read.
