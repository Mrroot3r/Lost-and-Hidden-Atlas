# Pictures batch rcwf (scheduled pictures run, 22 November 2026)

23 places from the waiting list were looked at, in three rounds of `photo_find.py next 5`, and the run stopped at about 40 minutes.

- 11 places got a picture (chosen by looking at each sheet, one candidate each).
- 5 places got `none` from the session: auriesville-shrine-coliseum, grafton-inn-ny, klondike-ramp-schenectady, holley-canal-falls, st-barnabas-blue-rocks.
- 7 places had nothing on Commons and were recorded as `none` by the tool: lidar-gibson-taylor-road-south-house-site, erie-lock-51-jordan, old-daley-inn-lansingburgh, crawford-depot-ga, gibsonville-ccc-camp-letchworth, mohasco-power-house, fisk-quarry-isle-la-motte.

Places where the session hesitated:

- c-o-canal-tide-lock: chose the waste weir remains at the end of the Rock Creek basin; the tide lock chamber itself is not among the candidates.
- st-peters-anglican-catalina: chose a photograph found by name only; the Gothic church fits the description, but the 1863 date could not be checked from the picture.
- st-augustine-water-works: chose the first of a series of photographs found by name only (a brick building with a hipped roof and an arched door); it fits the description of a pumping station but is not captioned as one.
- fort-griffin-town-flat: chose the restored civil jail; the lodge hall named in the same place was also among the candidates and is not shown.
- bryn-du-mansion-granville: chose Bryn Du Farm (1865), found by name only, where the house is a distant view across the lawn; the mansion photograph (2 km off, names it) was passed over as a different view of the same house.
- lynwood-baptist-church-hadley: chose a photograph found by name only; it shows the white church with its bell tower.
- auriesville-shrine-coliseum: the only candidates were the interior and a wooden cross on the grounds, so none.
- st-barnabas-blue-rocks: every candidate was a St Barnabas church in another country (Australia, Ontario) or another state, so none.
