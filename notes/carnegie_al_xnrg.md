## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Alabama (14 places): 9 standing buildings, among them three college ones (Alabama A&M's Carnegie Hall, Auburn's Mary E. Martin Hall and Tuskegee's Carnegie Hall), and the sites of 5 torn down (Anniston, Ensley, Gadsden, Huntsville, West End in Birmingham). Sources: Wikipedia's state list and articles, the Encyclopedia of Alabama, National Register nominations, and the libraries', museums' and universities' own pages. Hours only from the operators' own pages.
