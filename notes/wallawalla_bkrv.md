## What this batch is
Eleven standing places in Walla Walla, Washington, from the names in one walk-through video about the city's old buildings (Old World Exploration, August 2024; the record is source/videos/20240816_oZGim67RzLU.md). The video was used only as a list of names: 38 names, 11 places added (the courthouse and the Hall of Records are one entry, Court House Square), 2 already on the Atlas (Whitman Mission), 4 gone, 1 not found, 19 not checked. Added: the Baker-Boyer Bank Building, the Dacres Hotel, the Marcus Whitman Hotel, the Memorial Building at Whitman College, Court House Square (Hall of Records 1891, jail 1906, courthouse 1916), the Fort Walla Walla historic district at the VA medical center, the main post office, and the Green Park, Washington and Sharpstein schools and the 1907 YMCA. Sources read: National Register forms on NPGallery (text layer), Wikipedia as raw text and the county Register list, the Pacific Coast Architecture Database, SAH Archipedia, Walla Walla 2020's building reports (ww2020.net), courthouses.co, the Fort Walla Walla Museum's own page, an architects' page for Sharpstein, and a housing listing for Washington School. Not checked: interiors of all of them, the courthouse's and post office's hours, the Marcus Whitman Hotel's own page, what the YMCA building is used for now, which corner of Fourth and Main the Dacres stands on (the pin is at the crossing, `prec='site'`), visitor rules on the VA grounds, and brick on four places (Dacres, Fort Walla Walla, Green Park, Sharpstein: '?').

## Tips
- wallawallawa.gov answers HTTP 403 to `page.py`, and the archived copies could not be opened either, so the Downtown Walla Walla Historic District nomination (listed 2021) was not read.
- Walla Walla 2020 (ww2020.net) publishes a spreadsheet of all its building history reports (linked at the foot of each report, `wp-content/uploads/ww2020_database-research-<date>.xlsx`): title, address and build date for several hundred buildings. Its reports read fully with `page.py`.
- The Register list and Wikipedia point for the Dacres Hotel lies about 90 m east of Fourth and Main, where its Register form puts it.
- The Fort Walla Walla Register form (74001985) reads well and lists each of the fifteen army buildings with its date.
