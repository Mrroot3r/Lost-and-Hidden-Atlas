## What this batch is
Twenty-two places, the first pass in Chemung County, New York (region `wny`, drawn at random by `choose.py --state ny`, which offered Elmira, Fredonia and Montour Falls; the Atlas had nothing within 20 km of Elmira), plus three just north in Schuyler County. Elmira: the Chemung Canal Bank (Chemung Valley History Museum), the courthouse complex, the 1833 Arnot house (Arnot Art Museum), Trinity Church (a sports hall since 2024), the Civil War prison camp site with its restored and rebuilt buildings, Woodlawn and the national cemetery, the John W. Jones House, the Mark Twain Study, Quarry Farm (closed, view only), Eldridge Park's Looff carousel and Fire Station No. 4 (yellow brick, shut). Round it: Newtown Battlefield State Park, Mount Saviour Monastery, Harris Hill and the National Soaring Museum, Christ Church at Wellsburg, Riverside Cemetery at Lowman. Horseheads: the 1866 depot museum, Hanover Square and the Zim House. Schuyler: the Brick Tavern Stand museum, Shequaga Falls with the Montour Falls district, and the 1948–1952 Watkins Glen Grand Prix road course. Sources: Wikipedia raw text and the two county Register lists, HMdb marker pages and nearby lists (marker positions pinned several places), parks.ny.gov, cem.va.gov, arnotartmuseum.org, soaringmuseum.org, schuylerhistory.org, elmiraprisoncamp.com, johnwjonesmuseum.org, marktwainstudies.com (web fetch tool). Every Register form tried is an NPGallery placeholder. Not checked: hours for the Chemung Valley History Museum, Trinity's sports business, the Horseheads depot museum, the Zim House (Wikipedia only says by appointment) and Mount Saviour (its site answers 403); who owns Riverside Cemetery (marked view), Woodlawn Cemetery's own rules (only the national cemetery's), Shequaga Falls Park's owner, Christ Church's and the fire station's present use, Eldridge Park's ride season. Red brick was settled from the Wikipedia photographs for eight places; eleven are `?`. Nothing was seen on the ground.
