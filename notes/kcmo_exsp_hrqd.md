## What this batch is
Fifteen standing places in Excelsior Springs, Kearney, Smithville, Missouri City, Liberty and the Woodneath farmhouse, Clay County, east and north-east of Kansas City. The Atlas had one place within 25 km of Excelsior Springs and none in Liberty. Sources: the National Register list for Clay County and Wikipedia articles read as raw text (`wiki_raw.py`); the Friends of the James Farm page; web search results quoting the Jesse James Bank Museum and Liberty Jail listings. No Register form was read. Pins were checked on the Esri aerial; five were moved or downgraded (Elms, Wyman, Clinton House, Odd Fellows Home, Ligon and Jewell Hall marked as area). Not checked: any interior; owners of most private buildings; hours for the Hall of Waters, Elms, Liberty Jail, Woodneath and the Odd Fellows Home winery (hours='?'). The Bank Museum hours come from search-result listings, not its own page. Red brick is recorded from Wikipedia only for the Colonial and Ligon (and the Wyman school, "brick"); the rest are '?'. Photo: the Colonial Hotel got one from Commons; the other 14 have Wikipedia pictures.
