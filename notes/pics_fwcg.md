# Pictures batch fwcg (2026-11-21)
tidied: 2026-11-26

## What this batch is

A pictures job (PROMPTS.md job 8): photographs for 30 places already on the map that had none, chosen by looking at the candidate sheets from `tools/photo_find.py`. No new places, no pins, no text about places. The choices are in `tools/pics_fwcg.py`: 15 places got a picture and 15 got none. Every picture's address, credit and licence come from Wikimedia Commons through the tool.

## Places that got a picture

Caldwell Presbyterian Church (Lake George), Quitman Home Economics Building, Heyfield Memorial United Church, Derby House (Salem), Angie Debo House (Marshall), Boonton Ironworks ruins (the stone arches), Troy Gasholder Building, Opus 40 (the ramps), Olana (the house from the lawn), Northville historic district (a view of Main Street, not one building), Ladew manor house, Crown Point ruins, Goshen stone schoolhouse, Lebret Sacred Heart church, SS Moyie at Kaslo.

## Places that got none

Brudenell Island cemetery, Edwardsville tannery (Hunter), Cannington Manor, Fort Cobun, Old St. Michael's (Gardenton), Contrabando (Lajitas), Brainard Rural Cemetery, Rensselaerville grist mill, Sidney covered-bridge abutment, Silver City cemetery (Utah), Hirshfeldter Building (Downieville), Prudence Island magazine, The Kirk (Summerside), Charlestown naval airfield, Garden of the Gulf Museum. Most of the "none" answers are "nothing on Commons within the radius", which is what the tool printed.

## Where I hesitated

- Caldwell Presbyterian: both candidates carry the church's name and look like the same white church with a tower; I took candidate 1, which shows the rear wing too.
- Quitman: candidate 1 is 0 m from the pin and does not name the building; candidate 2 names it. Both are the same one-storey stone school. I took candidate 1.
- Boonton: the arch bridge over the Rockaway is also on Commons and close; I took the stone arches of the ironworks ruin, not the bridge.
- Troy: candidate 2 is 32 m from the pin and the same round brick building; I took candidate 3 (by name), which is clearer.
- Opus 40: candidates 1 to 5 all show the sculpture. I took candidate 4, the wide view with no person in the foreground.
- Olana: the five by-name candidates include a sunset and a painting; I took candidate 2, the house seen from the lawn. The place's own description ("views over the lost river industries") reads oddly for a house, so this is a pin-level question for the owner.
- Northville: a whole village. I took a Main Street view (candidate 2), not a single building, because one building would stand for the district. Owner may prefer none.
- Derby House: the Commons caption says Richard Derby built it; the place says Elias Hasket Derby. The picture is of the house at 168 Derby Street, so I kept it, but the caption's name is not ours to fix.
- Fort Cobun: the one candidate shows wooded grass at the earthworks; the earthworks are not plainly visible, so none.
- Hirshfeldter Building: the one candidate named for it is a white two-storey house with a wooden balcony; the place describes a fireproof stone store. None.
- Crown Point: candidates 1 and 2 are both the ruins of the same barracks; I took candidate 2 for the wider view.
- Lebret: the by-name church photo is 201 m from the pin; I took it because the church is plainly the church in the description.
- Garden of the Gulf: the candidates show the harbour and the bridge, not the post office; none.
- Charlestown: every candidate was a piping-plover photo from the refuge; none.
- Silver City: the by-name candidates are namesakes in Georgia, Montana and Idaho; none for Utah.
