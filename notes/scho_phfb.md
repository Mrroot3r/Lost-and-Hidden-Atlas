## What this batch is
Seventeen places in western and southern Schoharie County (region `scho`, drawn by `choose.py --state ny`, which offered Cobleskill; the Atlas had nothing in Cobleskill, Richmondville, Jefferson or the Breakabeen valley). Nearly all standing: the Cobleskill village district and the Bull's Head Inn (1802, a working restaurant), the Bramanville grist mill, the Richmondville Methodist church and the Bunn–Tillapaugh feed mill, the Charlotteville District 14 schoolhouse, the Maple Museum (1837 Presbyterian church) and Judd Hall in Jefferson, the Breakabeen and North Blenheim hamlets, the West Fulton Methodist church (Akron plan), the Forks in the Road schoolhouse and the South Gilboa U&D depot, the Livingstonville church, the closed Zion St. John's Lutheran church at Beekman Corners, the American Hotel in Sharon Springs (working inn), and Swarts Tavern (stone house of about 1772) in Schoharie. Four lead rows from `mohawk_wqbq` were finished and removed (Swarts Tavern, Breakabeen, North Blenheim, Bramanville Mill). Sources: Wikipedia raw text (mostly one-paragraph Register stubs), HMdb markers, the two inns' own pages (hours read November 22, 2026), the Schoharie County Historical Society's Cobleskill history, the Summit historian's schoolhouse PDF, and Pure Catskills / Mohawk Valley Museums for the Jefferson Historical Society. Not checked: anything on the ground; what the 2011 Irene flood left in Breakabeen and North Blenheim (conf mixed); whether the Bramanville mill still stands (pin is area: the aerial shows the creek bank, not the mill); the South Gilboa depot's condition and restoration; owners and parking almost everywhere; service times for the Richmondville, West Fulton and Livingstonville churches; interiors everywhere. The Maple Museum's hours come from the society's member listing on Pure Catskills, not a page of its own. Pins moved with `pix.py`: Breakabeen (Register point 2 km off, in woods), North Blenheim (500 m off, in woods), Forks in the Road (onto the building in the road fork, not confirmed as the school), South Gilboa depot (35 m). Brick: only the Cobleskill district is `some` (the red-brick Hotel Augustan in a Commons photograph); six are `no` from their Register descriptions; the rest `?`.
