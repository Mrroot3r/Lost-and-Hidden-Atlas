## What this batch is
The first places in Scranton, Lackawanna County (the Atlas had nothing within 15 km), taken as leads from one Old World Exploration video (record: `source/videos/20230923_qW7QsXkqqKQ.md`) and checked one by one. 16 places, all standing but one ruin: the courthouse, City Hall, the Albright Memorial Library, the old Central High School (Lackawanna College), the Masonic Temple (Scranton Cultural Center), the Lackawanna Railroad station (now a hotel), the Hotel Jermyn (apartments), the Watres Armory, the county prison, Scranton Prep (the International Correspondence Schools building), the Cathedral of Saint Peter, Elm Park Methodist, Covenant (First) Presbyterian, St. Matthew's Lutheran (the old Second Presbyterian), the Scranton Iron Furnaces and the Lake Scranton dam. Sources: SAH Archipedia (Buildings of Pennsylvania, entries PA-02-LK1 to LK48), the City of Scranton's 2016 landmark-buildings guide (a PDF with history, materials and a coordinate for each building), Wikipedia read as raw text, and the pages of the library system, the Anthracite Heritage Museum, PHMC, the cathedral and Elm Park church. Pins are the city guide's coordinates and all sat on their buildings on the leaf-off aerial. Not checked: hours for the courthouse, City Hall, the station hotel's lobby and the Cultural Center's tours (its tour page answered 404); service times for Covenant Presbyterian and St. Matthew's (no page of their own found); the Lake Scranton gatehouse; red brick for the cathedral, armory, St. Matthew's, the iron furnaces and the dam. Nothing was seen on the ground.

Its STYLE and MATERIALS lines follow each entry's history.
