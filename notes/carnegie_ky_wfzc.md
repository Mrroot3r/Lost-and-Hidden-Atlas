## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Kentucky (21 places): 20 standing buildings, among them eight in Louisville (the Crescent Hill, Eastern Colored, Highland, Parkland, Portland, Shelby Park and Western branches) and two college buildings (Centre College's Old Carnegie and Kentucky Wesleyan's), plus the site of the torn-down Paducah library. Sources: Wikipedia's state list and articles, National Register nominations, and the libraries', cities', museums' and colleges' own pages. Hours only from the operators' own pages.
