## What this batch is
Seventeen places in West Brookfield, Brookfield, North Brookfield and Warren (Worcester County), where the Atlas had nothing within 10 km of West Brookfield (region cmass drawn at random by choose.py). West Brookfield: the Lucy Stone Home Site (farmhouse ruins, The Trustees), the Jedediah Foster Homesite (foundations, Quaboag Historical Society park), the Quaboag Plantation markers on Foster Hill (Ayres' fortified house besieged in 1675; private land, view only), the Fort Gilbert marker, the Old Indian Cemetery, the Salem Cross Inn (White Homestead, c. 1707/1740), the 1760 Hitchcock Tavern, and the 1847 and 1884 railroad depots (Quaboag Historical Society museum). Brookfield: Brookfield Cemetery, Brookfield Common, the Merrick Public Library (Banister Memorial Hall) and Elm Hill Farm. North Brookfield: Camp Atwater and the Town House. Warren: the Crossman lenticular pony-truss bridge, the public library and the 1875 First Congregational (now Emmanuel Orthodox) church. Sources: Wikipedia with wiki_raw.py (Register list rows for Worcester County), HMdb nearby lists round five points, and page.py on the West Brookfield Historical Commission's site (westbrookfield.org, which reads cleanly and covers almost every West Brookfield site), The Trustees, the two libraries' own pages, the Salem Cross Inn and two articles.
