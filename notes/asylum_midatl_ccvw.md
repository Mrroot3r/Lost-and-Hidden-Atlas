## What this batch is

Sixteen historic state hospitals and asylums from New Jersey to West Virginia, for the themed pass on asylums: Trenton, the Greystone site and Skillman Village (New Jersey); the Delaware State Hospital at Farnhurst; Spring Grove, Springfield's Warfield Complex, Crownsville, Sheppard Pratt and Rosewood (Maryland); the reconstructed Public Hospital of 1773, Western State (the Blackburn Inn), Central State and its unmarked cemetery, Southwestern State's Henderson Building and the Virginia State Colony at Madison Heights (Virginia); and the Spencer site and Lakin (West Virginia). Sources: Wikipedia and its Kirkbride list, the Park Service's Register records and forms, SAH Archipedia, the Virginia Department of Historic Resources, the hospitals' and counties' own pages, Colonial Williamsburg, Clio, e-WV, HMdb, Cardinal News, the New Jersey Historic Trust, Preservation New Jersey, Patch and local papers. Not checked: which building at Trenton is the 1848 one (the pin is on the campus); whether the Greystone gas plant still stands; where the Spencer rubble lies (SAH says rubble, Clio says a Walmart; the pin is on the Walmart); the condition of Rosewood's remaining state-owned buildings; brick colour for most places (only Springfield, red, and the Henderson Building, dark, are from sources); park hours at Skillman; whether the Public Hospital is open now (its page gave Thursday hours and also said closed).
