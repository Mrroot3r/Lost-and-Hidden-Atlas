# Pictures batch eznw (scheduled run, November 21, 2026)

What this batch is: a pictures job (PROMPTS.md job 8). It chose a photograph, or recorded none, for 30 places already on the map that had none. No new places, no pins, no text about places. The file is tools/pics_eznw.py.

How it went: 30 places looked at in six sheets of five. 13 got a picture and 17 got none. Every picture's address, credit and licence came from Wikimedia Commons through the tool. Nothing was typed by hand.

Pictures chosen (13): Whitin Machine Works (an addition with its tower, 1980, by name); Blair Covered Bridge; Pompeys Pillar; Smith-Appleby House; Santarella; Shepaug railroad tunnel south portal; Indian Head Lighthouse; Ruby Church (St. John's); Merrick Public Library; Old Greene County Gaol; South Granville Congregational Church; St. George's Anglican Church, Brigus; Cottage Furnace.

No picture (17): Becket Quarry, Minisink Battleground Park (only a sign in the candidates), the South Road house site, Minnewaska hotels, James House Museum, Hotel Balmoral, Bourne (Oregon), Noyes House Museum, St. Thomas' Amenia Union, Acadian-Scottish burial ground, Repose of the Fallen, Bethabara God's Acre, Keir Memorial Museum, Madrid Stone Bridge, Ricker Basin, Jordan Aqueduct, Long Pond road cellar. Most had nothing on Commons near the pin, or none that named the place.

Where I hesitated:
- Whitin Machine Works: the candidates were all by name only. I chose the 1980 view of the Addition's frontage and tower because it shows the works from the street; the photo is older than the present state of the mill. The place is an area pin, so the view is of part of the works.
- Pompeys Pillar: a 2012 view of the pillar in grass, by name only. It is the pillar itself; the candidate with a building at its right was not chosen.
- Santarella: the 2008 view (14 m from the pin) was chosen over the 2023 view with the sign, which is also right.
- Steep Rock tunnel: a view of the south portal from the trail, not the house-like front of a building; it is the tunnel as it stands.
- St. Thomas' Amenia Union: one by-name photo of a Gothic church in the same town, taken 211 m from the pin; I could not confirm it is this church, so none.
- Keir Memorial Museum: the one candidate shows Princetown United Church, another building; none.
- Bethabara God's Acre: the candidates were the historic park and the church across the road; none.
- Jordan Aqueduct: the candidates named "Jordan" and "aqueduct" but were in Israel; none.
- Bourne, Oregon: candidates were Bourne in Lincolnshire, England, and a mineral specimen labelled Bourne, Oregon; none.

Not checked: nothing on the ground. Only what Commons showed near each pin, and the by-name candidates the tool listed.
