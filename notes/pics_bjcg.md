# pics_bjcg: pictures for places that had none

35 places were looked at over seven rounds.

- Got a picture: 18
- Answered "none" by hand: 17
- Recorded as none by the tool itself (nothing on Commons, or nothing new): 20

Where I hesitated:

- cambridge-historic-district: took the Cambridge Hotel on Main Street as the clearest village view; the area has no single building.
- callicoon-downtown: took the Erie Depot, the centre of the hamlet.
- hoags-corners-anti-rent: took the old tavern building beside the pin (8 m); the file is named for the road, not the tavern.
- mountain-park: only old postcards and a road view; took the pavilion postcard.
- powerhouse-park-port-henry: took the old stereo view of the furnace ruins at Port Henry.
- old-washington-maysville: the survey photograph of the Thomas Marshall House, 58 m from the pin, stands for the town.
- blackstone-canal-lincoln: the Kelly House (402 m away) is named in the place's title; the canal itself was not shown.
- quabbin-enfield: a view over the reservoir from the hill named for the lost town of Enfield.
- slocan-mercantile-sandon: a brick building 37 m from the pin; could be the store or a neighbour.
- charlestown-naval-airfield: a former runway 700 m away, as named by the file.
- fundy-saltwater-pool, scaroon-manor, mud-lock-5-liverpool: nothing showed the place.

Not checked: the pictures were judged only from the contact sheets.
