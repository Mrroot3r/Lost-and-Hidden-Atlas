# Pictures batch pics_yejz

30 places already on the map were looked at for a photograph (scheduled run, 21 November 2026). 13 got a picture, 17 got none. The choices are in tools/pics_yejz.py; every address, credit and licence was taken from Wikimedia Commons by the tool.

Chosen: Wyoming Frontier Prison (Old Pen, Rawlins), Tupperville School Museum, Fayette County Precinct No. 2 Courthouse, Canajoharie village (Church Street), Parting Ways cemetery (Plymouth), Melanson Settlement (meadow view of the site), Barter Theatre (Abingdon), St Andrew's Episcopal Church (Walden), Zapp Building (Fayetteville), Green Mountain Seminary (Waterbury Center), St. Paul's Episcopal Church (Troy), St. Peter Dynevor Old Stone Church (East Selkirk), Cornucopia Jailhouse.

Hesitations:
- wyoming-frontier-prison: the by-name candidate "Rawlins Jail" looked like the same stone block; the chosen view is the one 37 m from the pin with the cone-roofed towers.
- canajoharie-historic-district: an area pin; the picture is a village street 337 m from the pin, taken as the village centre. Looser than the others.
- parting-ways-plymouth: by-name only; the cemetery enclosure with its sign matches the description.
- melanson-settlement: the picture is an open meadow with hills on the upland terrace, no structure remains; taken as the site as it is.
- green-mountain-seminary: the picture is 100 m from the pin; the caption names the seminary building in Waterbury.
- st-pauls-episcopal-troy: several by-name candidates of the same church; chosen the frontal view captioned 54-58 Third Street.
- zapp-building-fayetteville: the chosen picture is 82 m from the pin, the corner building with the 1900 parapet.
- st-augustines-church-rustico: none. The by-name candidates show church fronts that differ (one tower or two), and captions place the church in both North and South Rustico. Not settled, so none.
- white-creek-historic-district: none. Only single houses and a church inside the district; no view of the hamlet as a whole.
- bentonsport-iowa: none. Only one brick house in the district.
- cady-tavern-moravia: none. The candidates showed St Matthew's Church and other houses on South Main Street, not the tavern.
- charlie-major-trail-mills, west-troy-weighlock, remington-ilion, heywood-wakefield, fort-bayard-nm: none. Each candidate was a different thing (a trail bridge, canal walls, a stables building, a chair sculpture, a statue).
- irondale-iron-works: none. The only candidate is a vacant lot marked as the site of the jail.
- Nothing on Commons near the pin, or none named the place: stewarts-dam-amsterdam, pioneer-oil-museum-bolivar, menands-manor, cherry-rock-park-bridge, hand-hale-elizabethtown, plotter-kill-aqueduct, coverdale-united-church.

Not checked: nothing was skipped; every look succeeded on the first try. No Wikimedia errors. The smoke test on dist/web passed (phone size).

Tips for the next session: the look command's by-name candidates are often several photographs of the same church under different captions; where their captions disagree on the village, none was chosen.
