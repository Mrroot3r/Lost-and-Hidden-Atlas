## What this batch is
Thirteen standing downtown Kansas City buildings, all named in one Old World Exploration video (record: source/videos/20240316_aiU_PTo5jsM.md): the New York Life, Scarritt, Old New England, Fidelity (909 Walnut), Power and Light and Bell Telephone (Oak Tower) buildings, the Kansas City Athletic Club tower (Mark Twain Tower), the Grand Avenue Temple and its office building, the Hotel Muehlebach, the Aladdin Hotel, the Midland Theatre, the Central Library in the old First National Bank, and the Liberty Memorial. The video was only a list of names; the facts come from Wikipedia read as raw text, the Register list for downtown Kansas City (positions), Clio entries (Scarritt, Grand Avenue Temple, Aladdin), Flatland KC and KCTV news pages, and the National WWI Museum's and Kansas City Public Library's own pages (hours). The Register forms for the Scarritt, Old New England and Grand Avenue Temple buildings could not be opened (the federal copies are placeholders and the state copies have moved). Not checked: any interior except the library and the memorial; who uses the Grand Avenue Temple since its 2021 closing; whether the Scarritt hotel conversion happened; hours for the Midland Theatre and the Muehlebach (their pages refused). Red brick is "?" for most: no source or picture opened says what colour the brick is. The Mark Twain Tower pin is the register point, which may sit across Eleventh Street from the building, so it is marked "site".
