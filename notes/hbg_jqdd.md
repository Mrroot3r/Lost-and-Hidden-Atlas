## What this batch is
Eight places from the buildings named in one video about Harrisburg (the record is `source/videos/20231207_tGfLzwnuoC8.md`; 28 names in all). Added: the Harrisburg State Hospital campus, the Rockville Bridge, the Marysville Bridge piers at the Dauphin Narrows with their Statue of Liberty replica, the Pennsylvania State Arsenal on Herr Street, the Mulberry Street Bridge, the Cathedral of Saint Patrick, the Pennsylvania Railroad station (Harrisburg Transportation Center) and the 1931 YMCA on North Front Street. The Capitol was already on the Atlas. Sources: Wikipedia read as raw text, SAH Archipedia's Harrisburg entries (PA-02-DA5, DA8, DA10, DA19, DA24), FortWiki for the Arsenal, the Department of General Services' August 2024 news page for the hospital's future, the cathedral parish's own page for Mass times and parking, and Amtrak's Great American Stations page for the station's hours.
