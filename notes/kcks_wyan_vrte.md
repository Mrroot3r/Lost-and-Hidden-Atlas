## What this batch is
Fourteen places in Kansas City, Kansas (Wyandotte County), where the Atlas had only Quindaro and a few places on the Missouri side: Sauer Castle, Grinter Place (open Wednesday to Saturday afternoons in season), the Argentine Carnegie Library and railroad YMCA, the Rosedale memorial arch, the Scottish Rite Temple, Memorial Hall, the county courthouse, the Granada Theater, the Schleifer-McAlpine and Davis (Castle Rock) houses, the Wyandot burying ground, Fire Station No. 9 and St. John the Divine church. Sources: the Kansas Historical Society's register search for Wyandotte County (page.py; its summaries are one paragraph and often say nothing of what survives), Wikipedia articles (wiki_raw.py), the Society's Grinter Place page, Cinema Treasures. Pins: Wikipedia, register list or address lookup, each looked at on the Kansas 2024 aerial; three were moved (Grinter, Castle Rock, the YMCA). Not checked: any interior, hours other than Grinter's, who owns the Granada, the casino hours in the Scottish Rite Temple, which building on the courthouse block is the courthouse (prec site). The National Register forms were not read (NPGallery was not tried). Brick: yes for the YMCA (source), the Schleifer-McAlpine house and St. John (photographs), no for Castle Rock (limestone) and the burying ground, the rest not checked.

## Tips
- The Kansas Historical Society register search takes county code WY for Wyandotte; six pages of ten.
- The Wikipedia and register coordinates for Grinter Place and Castle Rock fell 70 to 270 m off the building; an ArcGIS address lookup was right for all three tried (Kansas address lookups through geocode.arcgis.com need no key).
- The Society's Grinter Place page lists hours as a bare line; an event notice gave the season dates.
