## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Missouri (30 places): 29 standing buildings, among them the St. Louis Central Library, six St. Louis Carnegie branches, the St. Joseph branches and Park College's library, and the site of the torn-down Maryville library. Sources: Wikipedia's state list and articles, National Register nominations from Missouri State Parks, SAH Archipedia, St. Louis City Talk, The Clio, local papers and the libraries', cities' and museums' own pages. Places near Kansas City (St. Joseph, Excelsior Springs, Parkville) are in the kcmo region; the rest are in mo. Hours only from the operators' own pages.

## What was not checked

- Excelsior Springs, Mexico, Fulton, Shelbina and the old Divoll branch are pinned 'area': the corners or addresses sources give were not certain on the aerial.
- Old Divoll branch: two addresses on Farrar Street, and one account calls it privately held while another says it is a school board centre.
- Joplin, Nevada, Soulard and Mexico: private buyers and owners are named in the sources and left out on purpose.
- Brick is 'yes' only for Albany; most other sources do not give the brick's colour. No pictures were opened for the walls.
- Carondelet branch: the National Register listing is from St. Louis City Talk, not the Register itself.

## Leads not finished

- Aurora, Brookfield and Monroe City (on Wikipedia's list): not researched.
- William Jewell College (Liberty): its Carnegie building was not settled.
- Carthage: the address and whether the Carnegie building still stands were not clear from sources read.
