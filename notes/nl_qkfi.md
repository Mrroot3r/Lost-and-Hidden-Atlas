## What this batch is
Twenty-five places on the Baccalieu Trail and round Conception Bay, the second batch in the province (none within 40 km before). Harbour Grace: the 1830 Court House (on the National Trust's endangered list in 2016 after the court closed), the Ridley Hall ruins (burned 2003), St. Paul's Anglican (stone, 1835), the former Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception (closed 2014, sold for a brewery and hotel; whether that opened is not confirmed), the Conception Bay Museum in the 1870 Customs House, and the 1927 airstrip Amelia Earhart left from. Carbonear: Rorke's Stone House (now the Stone Jug restaurant), the Rorke Store museum, the railway station museum, the 1905 post office, St. James Anglican. Also the Mosquito School House (Bristol's Hope, 1818–1828), the Victoria hydro station (1904), Western Bay station, and in Heart's Content the Cable Station, the 1901 cast-iron lighthouse, Heyfield Memorial United Church and SUF Lodge No. 1. Brigus: Hawthorne Cottage, the Stone Barn (now the John N. Leamon Museum), St. George's (now a heritage church museum) and Kent Cottage; Cupids Cove Plantation; the Cable Building in Bay Roberts; Bell Island No. 2 Mine. Sources: the Canadian Register statement for each (historicplaces.ca with `page.py --all -n 60000`, ids from the `{{HPC row}}` templates of "List of historic places on the Avalon Peninsula"); the Heritage Foundation of NL pages (heritagenl.ca/?p=…), which gave both newer facts and better pin points; Wikipedia through `wiki_raw.py`; seethesites.ca, Parks Canada (Hawthorne hours page), bellislandminetour.com, conceptionbaymuseum.com; CBC and GCatholic for the cathedral; Museums NL and Historic Places Days for who runs the small museums. **Not checked:** anything on the ground; service times for St. Paul's, St. James and Heyfield (no parish pages found); hours for the Rorke Store, Carbonear station, the old post office, the Stone Barn, St. George's and the Cable Building museum (only directory listings, so `hours='?'`; the Stone Jug's hours are from its own Historic Places Days listing); the present use of the Court House; who owns the Ridley Hall ruins, the airstrip land and the Heart's Content lighthouse point; whether the Victoria Heritage Park opens. Pins that mark only an area or could not be confirmed on the aerial (the Esri aerial is coarse here): Ridley Hall (Heritage Foundation point on grass), St. Paul's (Heritage Foundation point on Cochrane Street; the church not picked out), the Stone Barn, Bell Island No. 2 Mine (street point in the mine yard), the airstrip (middle of the strip, prec area). Pins moved: the Court House (Register point was 4 km west), Heyfield United (Register point 2 km off in woods), St. Paul's, SUF Lodge, the Stone Barn, Mosquito School House, Western Bay station, Ridley Hall (all to the Heritage Foundation's map points), the cathedral (37 m onto the building with `pix.py`). One `th=`: a gravel clearing at the lighthouse seen on the aerial. `--lidar` not run (no 1 m LiDAR in Canada).

## Tips
- heritagenl.ca (Heritage Foundation of NL) has a page for each provincial and municipal designation at `https://heritagenl.ca/?p=<n>` (a web search "heritagenl.ca <name>" finds the number). It is newer than the Register (it alone said Ridley Office was demolished in May 2026) and its "Google map" link carries a `q=lat,lon` that was on or beside the building every time; grep the cached page in `research/pages/` for `q=[-0-9.]*,[-0-9.]*`.
- Register list coordinates on the Avalon Peninsula were off by up to 4 km (Harbour Grace Court House) and 2 km (Heyfield United, in woods). Prefer the Heritage Foundation point, then the ArcGIS PointAddress.
- OpenStreetMap is thin in Conception Bay North: `nom.py` found 5 of 26 names; `parking.py` found almost no lots.
- The Parks Canada hours page (`/visit/heu-hou`) is reported DRAWN BY SCRIPT by `page.py`, but the short text it returns does hold the season and hours.
- seethesites.ca gives no daily hours out of season ("Closed for the season"); `plan-your-visit/` has next season's dates.
- heritage.nf.ca reset the connection on every request this session; the web search result text was empty for most queries, so hours could not be read from search snippets.
