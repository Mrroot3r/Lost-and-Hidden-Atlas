## What this batch is
Nine standing buildings in downtown and South Omaha, taken from the names in one Old World Exploration video (the record is source/videos/20221211_KW1k7K10hiA.md; the video was a list of names, never a source): the 1912 Douglas County Courthouse, the 1917 First National Bank Building, the Blackstone Hotel (now the Kimpton Cottonwood), Omaha Central High School, the Hill Hotel (Kensington Tower), the Riviera/Astro theatre (The Rose), the Livestock Exchange Building at the old Union Stockyards, the J. L. Brandeis and Sons store, and the 1887 Anheuser-Busch beer depot office (Wikipedia calls it the last Krug brewery building; the Register form names only Anheuser-Busch, so it is 'mixed'). Facts come from Wikipedia (wiki_raw.py), National Register forms read as text from NPGallery (First National Bank, Central High, Hill Hotel, the beer depot), the National Park Service "Travel Omaha" place pages (courthouse, Brandeis, Livestock Exchange, Astro), the HMdb marker and SAH Archipedia for the depot, and a Lodging Magazine report on the Blackstone. Pins are ArcGIS address points; three that fell in the street were moved onto the building seen on the aerial. Not checked: anything on the ground; hours of the courthouse, the hotel and The Rose (all hours='?'); interiors except as the Register forms and the hotel report describe them; parking (downtown street and pay parking only, no lot named); the year the Blackstone reopened as a hotel (the report page's dates were unclear); red brick for five places ('?').
