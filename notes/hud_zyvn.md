## What this batch is
Twenty-one places in Kingston and the hamlet of Hurley, Ulster County (region `hud`, drawn at random by `choose.py --state ny`, which offered Livingston, Philmont and Hurley; start.py showed nothing on the Atlas in the Stockade or in Old Hurley). Nearly all standing. Uptown Kingston: the Stockade District (area pin), the Senate House, the Old Dutch Church, the 1818 courthouse, the Persen House at the Four Corners (closed for the 2026 season), the Fred J. Johnston House, the Frog Alley ruins (the one ruin; it was an unnamed OpenStreetMap lead), the Van Steenburgh house. Midtown and the Rondout: City Hall, the Rondout–West Strand district, Rondout Light (boat only; the Maritime Museum is its th= point), the 1921 suspension bridge, the Trolley Museum, the 1870 poured-concrete Ponckhockie chapel, the Fitch bluestone office in Wilbur, the City Almshouse (now Landmark Place apartments). Hurley: the historic district, the Reformed church, the Van Deusen house, the DuMond "Spy" house and the Heritage Society museum. Sources read in the session with `wiki_raw.py` and `page.py`: Wikipedia, the parks.ny.gov Senate House page, the Ulster County Clerk's Persen House page, olddutchchurch.org, tmny.org, hrmm.org, kingston-ny.gov, fohk.org and its 2016 Stockade walking-tour PDF, hurleyheritagesociety.org (museum page, Main Street history, walking-tour PDF), two NPGallery Register forms, the Governor's 2022 release on Landmark Place and a 2022 Hudson Valley One article. Not checked: nothing on the ground; the courthouse's present use and whether it can be entered; whether Frog Alley's walls are still as the 2016 brochure describes and whether the little park is open; the Fitch office's owner and use; service times for the Hurley and Ponckhockie churches (no page of their own found); the Hurley church's date (1853 or 1854 in the society's two texts) and the Van Deusen house's (c. 1744 in the brochure, 1723 on the state marker seen in a photograph), both conf='mixed'. Pins are geocoder PointAddress or OpenStreetMap points, each looked at on the aerial; Senate House, Van Deusen and Ponckhockie are prec='site' because the point is beside the building, and the Stockade and Rondout pins are area pins. Pictures: 18 of 21 places have one (Wikipedia or a chosen Commons photo in `pics_rrch.py`); none fitted the Persen House, Frog Alley or the DuMond house.
