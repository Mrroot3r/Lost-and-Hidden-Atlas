## What this batch is
Fourteen places in and near Dubuque, Iowa, which had one (the Shot Tower), taken from the buildings named in one video of old postcards and photographs of the town (the record is source/videos/20240217_3bNLR_2UCGk.md); the video was a tip only and nothing in the entries comes from it. Added: the Julien Dubuque Monument in the Mines of Spain, the Town Clock, the Carnegie-Stout Public Library, St. Luke's United Methodist Church (Tiffany windows), the Dubuque Brewing and Malting Company (its southern half torn down in 2024; the northern part stands), City Hall (1857), the County Courthouse, the County Jail (Egyptian Revival, a National Historic Landmark), the Five Flags Theater (built as the Majestic), St. Raphael's Cathedral, the Hotel Julien Dubuque, New Melleray Abbey near Peosta, Wartburg Theological Seminary and St. Mary's Church (now Steeple Square). Sources: Wikipedia read as raw text, the Dubuque County Register list rows, Encyclopedia Dubuque (encyclopediadubuque.org) for the brewery, Steeple Square and Wartburg, and SAH Archipedia for the brewery. Hours: the library's own page; New Melleray's church hours and the Iowa state park hours are as web search results quoted the abbey's and the DNR's pages, which would not open. Not checked: service times for St. Luke's and St. Raphael's (their pages would not open; directory listings were not used), office hours for City Hall and the courthouse, box office times at Five Flags, how much of the brewery stands after November 2024 (the aerial still shows its northern block), and red brick for nine places (left '?'; only the courthouse's red brick is from a source). Every pin was looked at on the aerial; the brewery and St. Mary's pins were moved onto the buildings, St. Mary's to 'site' as the church roof is not certain on the aerial.
