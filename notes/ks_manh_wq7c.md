## What this batch is
Sixteen places in Manhattan, Kansas (Riley County), worked from the names in one Old World Exploration video (record: source/videos/20250725_E2snoFf5Q6M.md); the Atlas had only the Carnegie library and the Goodnow House there. Downtown: the Riley County Courthouse, the Union National Bank building, the Wareham Opera House and Wareham Hotel, the Marshall Theater and the Community House; churches: Seven Dolors (open, Mass times from the Diocese of Salina's page), First Congregational (its 1859 stone block) and the former First Christian Church (county-owned, threatened with demolition; standing on the 2024 state aerial, its status after April 2024 not checked, conf='mixed'); the 1901 Union Pacific depot; and Kansas State University's Holtz, Anderson, Fairchild, Calvin and Nichols halls and World War I Memorial Stadium. Sources: National Register forms read as text from NPGallery (05001249 courthouse, 95001054 Seven Dolors, 06001051 Community House, 08001068 First Congregational, 06001240 Downtown Manhattan Historic District, which describes the bank, the Wareham buildings and the Marshall Theater one by one), Kansas State University's own building pages (k-state.edu/maps/buildings/<letter>/), Wikipedia, SAH Archipedia, Clio, KMAN and the Preservation Alliance's 2021 newsletter. Pins: address lookups and OpenStreetMap, all looked at on the 2024 Kansas aerial; Anderson Hall moved about 50 m onto the building; First Congregational is prec='site'. Not checked: anything on the ground, interiors, campus and downtown parking rules, who runs the Community House now, service times at First Congregational (its page gives none), and brick for the depot and four campus buildings ('?').

## Tips
- Kansas State University's building pages follow k-state.edu/maps/buildings/<letter>/ (F, C, N, A; Holtz Hall is HZ); each has a short dated building history and reads well with page.py --all.
- The Downtown Manhattan Historic District form (NPGallery 06001240_text, 86 pages) has a numbered entry per building with date, architect and what was altered; search it for "<number>. <name> Status".
- ksoutdoors.gov and ksoutdoors.com (Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks) answered HTTP 403 to page.py and the web fetch tool, and web.archive.org could not be fetched.
- SharonPapierdreams has uploaded Register photographs of most downtown Manhattan buildings to Commons, named by address, which photo_find.py finds by name.
