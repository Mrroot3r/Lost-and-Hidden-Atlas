## What this batch is
Fifteen standing buildings in and round Atlanta, taken from the names in one video of old architecture (Old World Exploration, "Old World Atlanta, Georgia"; the record of every name and its outcome is source/videos/20230701_j5c5IBAGQbo.md). The video was used only as a list of names. Added downtown: the Candler Building, the Flatiron (English-American) Building, the Hurt Building, the Henry W. Grady statue, the Atlanta Constitution Building (under renovation) and Georgia Hall, the first Grady Hospital; in Midtown: the Fox Theatre, the Georgian Terrace Hotel, the Atlanta Biltmore and Tech Tower; also the Basilica of the Sacred Heart, Fountain Hall (Atlanta University's Stone Hall), the Atlanta Federal Penitentiary, Agnes Scott Hall in Decatur and Oglethorpe University's Gothic campus with the Crypt of Civilization in Brookhaven. Facts came from Wikipedia (wiki_raw.py), the National Register forms on NPGallery (Biltmore, Grady Hospital, Stone Hall), and the Basilica's, Fox Theatre's, Oglethorpe University's and Invest Atlanta's own pages (page.py). Not checked: parking for every place (all are on city streets or campuses), interiors except where a source describes them, and the current use of the Biltmore. The Fox Theatre's tours page answered 406, so only its ticket-window times are given. Mass times for the Basilica are from its own page. Pins were looked at on the aerial; the Flatiron and Biltmore pins were moved to OpenStreetMap's building points. No LiDAR sheet was made: every place is a standing building in town.
