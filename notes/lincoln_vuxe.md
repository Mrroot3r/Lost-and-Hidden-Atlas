## What this batch is
Eleven standing places in Lincoln, Nebraska, taken from the names in one Old World Exploration video about Lincoln (record: source/videos/20230507_AuBI-u1hbCU.md; the video was a list of names only, never a source): Old City Hall and Grand Manse (the first and second federal buildings on Government Square), Architecture Hall (the Old University Library), the Temple Building and the Coliseum on the university's city campus, Old Main at Nebraska Wesleyan, the Burr Block, the Federal Trust Building, the Treasury Building columns in Pioneers Park, First-Plymouth Congregational Church and the 1930 Veterans Administration hospital on South 70th Street. Sources: National Register records and forms through form.py (69000132, 75001098, 75001097, 02000409, 79001448, 12000785, 93000538), SAH Archipedia, the UNL archives' historic-buildings pages, Wikipedia, the City of Lincoln's Pioneers Park page (web fetch) and First-Plymouth's own site for service times. Pins are Register-list points and ArcGIS address points, all on their buildings on the aerial; the columns are an OpenStreetMap point. Not checked: present use of the Burr Block and the old VA buildings (a 2015 plan opened the VA campus to redevelopment), the Temple Building's materials, parking everywhere, and whether any building but the church lets visitors in. The Pioneers Park columns have two dates for the gift (1916 in the Register form, 1907 on the city's page), both given.
