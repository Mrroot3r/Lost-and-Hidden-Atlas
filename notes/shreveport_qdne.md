## What this batch is
Ten places in downtown Shreveport and one in its South Highlands, from the names in one video tip (the record is source/videos/20250523_FoLUB2-iuTs.md; the video is not a source). Added: Oakland Cemetery with the 1873 yellow fever mass grave and its 2023 memorial, the Municipal Memorial Auditorium (Louisiana Hayride), First Methodist Church, the Old Commercial National Bank Building (1910), the old Municipal Building of 1924 at 724 McNeil Street, the downtown YMCA (1925), Holy Trinity Catholic Church (1896), the Cathedral of St. John Berchmans, the 1910 U.S. Post Office and Courthouse (now the closed-for-renovation main library) and the Dodd College President's Home (private house, view only). Sources: Wikipedia through wiki_raw.py, Register nomination forms through NPGallery (cemetery, bank, municipal building, Dodd house), the Downtown Development Authority, LSU Shreveport, Shreveport Common, Shreve Memorial Library, the Diocese of Shreveport's parish pages and the cathedral's and First Methodist's own pages. Pins are address points from the ArcGIS geocoder, all looked at on the aerial; the cathedral's was on Jordan Street and was moved onto the church. Not checked: the cemetery's opening hours (no page of the city or the preservation society was found), the auditorium's museum and tour hours (its own site answered 403), anything on the ground, and the insides of the bank, the YMCA, the post office and the auditorium. No parking points: the lots parking.py offered near the cemetery and the auditorium were a cemetery path and a building on the aerial, so the notes say street parking.

## Tips
- No Shreveport source names an Elks home, an Arcade Hotel, a 1921 natatorium or the Peerless Supply Company: web searches turned up only namesakes in other states.
- downtownshreveport.com (the Downtown Development Authority) has short histories of lost and standing buildings, with dates, and reads well with page.py.
- The Diocese of Shreveport's parish pages (dioshpt.org/church/...) give address and Mass times when the parish's own site answers 403 (Holy Trinity).
