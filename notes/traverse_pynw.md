## What this batch is
Ten places in downtown Traverse City from one walk-through video of the town's old buildings (Old World Exploration, "Old World Traverse City Michigan"; the record is source/videos/20251017_ti6FAoC4260.md). The video names 31 buildings; 10 are added: the Northern Michigan Asylum's Building 50 at Grand Traverse Commons (with its steam tunnels, on the guided tour), the Perry Hannah House, the City Opera House, the Grand Traverse County Courthouse, the Park Place Hotel, the Hannah & Lay Mercantile Building, the Traverse City State Bank clocktower building, the old Fire Station No. 1 on Cass Street, Central United Methodist Church and Oakwood Cemetery. Sources read in full or with filters: SAH Archipedia GT1 to GT12, Wikipedia, The Ticker news reports, American Courthouses, and the pages of Grand Traverse County, the City of Traverse City, The Village at Grand Traverse Commons, the City Opera House and Central United Methodist Church. Not checked: any place on the ground; the inside of the Hannah & Lay, clocktower, fire station and church buildings; red brick for five places (left '?'); hotel and opera-house hours (none on their pages). The Building 50 pin was moved from the Wikipedia point, which fell in woods, onto the south wing as OpenStreetMap maps it. The other pins are street-address points that sat on the buildings on the aerial.
