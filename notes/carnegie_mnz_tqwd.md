## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Minnesota, public towns M to Z (32 places): 25 standing buildings and 7 sites of torn-down ones, from Wikipedia's state list, Placeography's Minnesota Carnegie Libraries Tour pages, National Register forms, local papers and the operators' own sites. Hours only from the operators' pages.

## What was not checked

- No pictures were opened; wall colours come from Placeography's text, so many are `brick='?'`.
- Mankato's art centre lists hours only "until February 18th", so hours are '?'.
- Spring Valley: Placeography says the building is City Hall, Wikipedia says it is still the library at another address. Both versions given.
- Thief River Falls: sources disagree on who uses it (visitor bureau, county, city, library); the visitor bureau's page was followed.
- Zumbrota was for sale in 2021; its use now was not found.
- Pins set to 'area': Mankato (blurred aerial), Montevideo (trees), Preston (geocoded to the street crossing), and every torn-down site.

## Leads not finished

- Red Wing (Carnegie-Lawther Free Library, 1903 to 1968, at 3rd and Broadway): the geocoder could not place the corner, and whether the 1969 library stands on the site was not found.
- Virginia (1907 to 1953): only a personal postcard site gives the site (Chestnut Street West and 9th Avenue).
- Worthington (1905 to 1966): the National Register form for the War Memorial Building puts it across 4th Avenue, but the cross street is garbled.

## Tips for the next session

- Placeography (placeography.net) has a page for nearly every Minnesota Carnegie library, standing or razed, with addresses, architects and wall materials.
