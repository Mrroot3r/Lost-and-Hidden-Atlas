## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Idaho, Utah and Nevada (30 places): 22 standing buildings and 8 sites of torn-down ones. Idaho 7 (Boise and Wallace were already on the Atlas), Utah 22, Nevada 1 (Reno, its only grant). Sources: Wikipedia's three state lists and articles, National Register nominations (Utah's 1984 thematic nomination covers all 23 of its Carnegie libraries), the Idaho Heritage Trust, HMdb markers, Reno Historical, the Nevada State Historic Preservation Office and the operators' own pages. Hours only from the operators' pages.

## What was not checked

- Wikipedia's list rows were read through a page summariser, so spacing and coordinates may be slightly normalised.
- Manti and Panguitch: what the buildings are used for now is not settled (Panguitch: a 2016 page says a gift shop, a 2021 account says the library, and the library's own page gives another address). Both are 'view'.
- Provo: present use rests on one undated page (vacant, offered as commercial space).
- Eureka: who uses the Memorial Building now was not found.
- Beaver and Springville: no hours on a page of their own ('?').
- Tooele: the museum's own page contradicts itself on whether Tuesdays are year-round.
- Addresses that disagree between sources (both given in the place text): Beaver 55 or 50 W Center, Manti 2 or 12 S Main, Richmond 38 or 6 W Main, Springville 175 or 45 S Main, Lehi 51 or 55 N Center, Pocatello 113 or 105 S Garfield.
- Mountain Home pin is from an interpolated address ('area'); Moscow, Garland, Mount Pleasant and Panguitch pins sit beside the building ('site').
- No pictures were opened for the walls; brick comes from the sources' words.

## Leads not finished

- Boise (already on the Atlas): a 2021 law had the state buy it for the University of Idaho law school; whether the sale closed was not found.
- Weiser, Intermountain Institute Carnegie library (1919, reinforced concrete): listed in the campus's 1979 nomination; whether it stands today and its spot on the campus were not found. The campus is already on the Atlas (Hooker Hall).
- Preston, Idaho (dark red brick, opened 1917, demolished June 11, 2004): on the north side of the public square; no street address found.
- Parowan, Utah: demolished; no location or dates found.
- Ogden: what stands on the site now was not found; the marker is there.
