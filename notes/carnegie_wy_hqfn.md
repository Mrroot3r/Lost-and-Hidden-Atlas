## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Wyoming (15 places): 10 standing buildings and 5 sites of torn-down ones, from Wikipedia's state list, Linda R. Waggener's 2019 University of Wyoming thesis on the state's Carnegie libraries, Cowboy State Daily, the Wyoming State Historic Preservation Office, HMdb markers and the operators' own pages. Wyoming had 16 grants and no academic ones. Hours only from the operators' pages.

## What was not checked

- The thesis was read through a page summariser; facts that rest on it alone (several architects and opening days) are worth a second look.
- Buffalo: one 2018 newspaper lists it among the demolished; every other source says it stands. Summer hours of the museum were not found.
- Green River: empty and unsafe in 2024; the owner is not stated (the county is implied).
- Thermopolis: pinned on the present library, which a 2021 account says the old building stands behind.
- Wheatland and Green River pins are from interpolated addresses ('area').
- No pictures were opened for the walls; brick comes from the sources' words.

## Leads not finished

- Cody (1914 grant, opened 1916, torn down 1965): the site was not found; a new library stood on the same lot until the library moved in 2008.
- Douglas (300 Walnut Street, torn down 1966): whether the present library stands on the same lot was not found.
- Cheyenne: what was built on the site after 1971 is uncertain (one radio station page says a savings and loan).

## Tips for the next session

- Waggener's thesis (Wyoming State Library digital collections) covers every Wyoming Carnegie library and is the quickest way in.
