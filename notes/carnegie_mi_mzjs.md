## What this batch is

Michigan's Carnegie libraries, part of the owner's pass to put every Carnegie- and Rockefeller-funded building on the map. 54 places: the public libraries on Wikipedia's state list that could be placed from pages read in this session, including Detroit's main library and eight of its branches (four of those branches gone, one empty). Each says whether the Carnegie building stands, what it is now, and how to see it. Hours are only from the operator's own page; where none was found the place says hours='?'.
