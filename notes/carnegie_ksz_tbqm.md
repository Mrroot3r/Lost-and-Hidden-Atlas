## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Kansas, towns M to Z and the college libraries (16 places): 12 town libraries still standing, the site of Salina's torn-down library, the College of Emporia's Anderson Memorial Library, Washburn's Carnegie building in Topeka, and the three columns saved from Fairmount College's burned Morrison Library at Wichita State. The sources are Wikipedia's state list, the 1987 group National Register nomination and single nominations on npgallery, and the libraries', cities', museums' and colleges' own pages. Hours come only from the operators' own pages. Wichita's city library and Baker University's Case Library were already on the map.

## What was not checked

- Walls come from the National Register nominations, not from pictures. Peabody's brick is grey in the nomination and brown in a Wikipedia caption.
- Oswego's library page shows its days and times in separate blocks, so the pairing could not be trusted; hours are '?'. Hours were not found for Osborne, Ottawa, Parsons, Peabody and Yates Center either.
- Winfield: what the old building is used for now was not found.
- Osborne has three addresses (307 West Main, 112 South Sixth, Third and Main); the pin is on the building in the park at Third and Main, as 'area'.
- Washburn's building was not picked out on the aerial; the pin is the list's coordinate, as 'area'.
- The Morrison Library columns' exact spot by the 17th Street entrance was not found; the pin is at the corner.
- Newton: Wikipedia's list gives a $160,000 grant; the nomination's account is $15,000.

## Leads not finished

- McPherson College (Beeghly Hall, library 1907 to 1972): standing, but where on the campus was not found.
- Bethany College, Lindsborg (1908, torn down): demolition date and site not found.
- Midland College, Atchison: a 1912 text casts doubt on whether it was ever built.
- Stockton (124 North Cedar): the group nomination calls it altered; whether the present library is the Carnegie building was not settled.
- McPherson, Olathe, Osawatomie, Plainville, Russell and Washington (all torn down): sites not found. Olathe's opened in 1914 and was torn down in the mid-1960s.
