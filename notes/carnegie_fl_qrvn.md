## What this batch is
Carnegie libraries, Florida (carnegie_fl_qrvn): 14 places, 9 standing, 5 gone. Public libraries at Bradenton, Jacksonville, Palmetto, St. Petersburg (Mirror Lake), Tampa and West Tampa; sites of those at Bartow, Clearwater, Gainesville and Ocala; academic ones at FAMU (Tallahassee), Stetson (Sampson Hall, DeLand) and Rollins (Carnegie Hall, Winter Park), and the site of Fessenden Academy's Carnegie Hall near Ocala. No Florida Carnegie library was on the Atlas before; no other place is within 600 m of any pin.

## What was not checked
- Pins were looked at on the aerial. Bartow, Gainesville, Ocala and Fessenden are area pins; Ocala's is Wikipedia's point (the geocoder could not match the Ocklawaha Avenue address); Bartow's geocode gave two East Stanford Street points 1.4 km apart (the one nearer downtown was used).
- Hours not found for Mirror Lake and West Tampa (the county page gives none); FAMU's Black Archives page gives hours but not which of its buildings is open.
- Brick: Tampa yellow and brown, FAMU white, Mirror Lake buff under stucco, Jacksonville limestone (all 'no'); the rest '?'.
- Disagreements given both ways: Jacksonville's grant ($50,000 or $55,000), Tampa's grant (1901, or 1912 raised in 1913), West Tampa (built 1913 or opened 1914), Mirror Lake's grant (1913 or approved 1914), FAMU's listing (1978 or 1996), Fessenden's handover (1951 or 1952).
- Tampa's use now: offices of the county library since 2016, but the same article also names a city department; a 2024 museum idea.
