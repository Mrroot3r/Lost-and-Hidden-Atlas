## What this batch is

Fifteen Carnegie libraries in eastern and southern New York outside New York City, the second batch of the pass the owner asked for (buildings Andrew Carnegie and the Rockefellers paid for), from Wikipedia's "List of Carnegie libraries in New York". With batch `carnegie_ny_tpsk` and the six the Atlas already had, this covers every Carnegie library in the state outside New York City except Hamburg and Wells College (leads below).

Added: Catskill, Chatham, Johnstown, Kingston (former city library, now the high school's Carnegie Learning Center), Saugerties, Port Jervis, Theresa, Nyack, Mount Vernon, New Rochelle (former library, private), Patchogue (moved in 2012, now the teen centre), Rockville Centre (former library), Schenectady (Webster House, a Union College dormitory), Alfred (Jordan Hall, Alfred University offices) and the site of Ossining's, torn down in the 1960s.

Sources: Wikipedia as raw text; the libraries' own pages (hours, histories), the colleges' pages, local papers and historical pages read with the web fetch tool. Hours are from each library's own page; directories were not used. Pins are ArcGIS geocoder or OpenStreetMap points, each looked at on the leaf-off aerial.

Not checked: Mount Vernon's hours (its page gives two tables without saying which is which); who uses the Rockville Centre building now; the New Rochelle street number (662 or 622 Main Street); where exactly Ossining's "Museum in the Streets" marker stands (its HMdb page refused the fetch), so that pin is the present library's address. Red brick is "yes" for Chatham, Saugerties and Patchogue, by their Wikimedia Commons pictures; "no" for Nyack (rough stone front), Kingston (narrow brown brick), Webster House (light yellowish-grey brick), and Johnstown and Port Jervis (buff brick in their Commons pictures); and "?" elsewhere.

Pictures: found on Wikimedia Commons for Chatham, Johnstown, Saugerties, Port Jervis, Patchogue and Webster House (Kingston and Nyack show their Wikipedia articles' pictures); none fitted Catskill, Theresa, Mount Vernon, New Rochelle, Rockville Centre or Jordan Hall. The finder ran without the Commons login, at the slow pace.
