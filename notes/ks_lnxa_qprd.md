## What this batch is
Five places: three buildings in Old Town Lenexa (the 1910 limestone Krumm Building, the 1923 brick Farmers State Bank building later Ryckert's Market, and the 1904 E. H. Haskin Home) and two monuments (the 1907 Santa Fe Trail marker in the Olathe courthouse plaza, and the 1963 Memorial to the Six Million at the Jewish Community Center in Overland Park). This is well under the 15 to 20 asked for. Four earlier batches had already taken the Johnson County Register listings, and what is left there is mostly private houses with a one-line summary, scans with no text layer, or buildings too new to count (the Museum of Deaf History building opened in 2001, so it was left out). Facts come from the Lenexa Historical Society's own pages on each Old Town building and the Clio driving tour (theclio.com/tour/1012), read with page.py. Not checked: whether the interiors can be visited, tenants' hours, who owns the Haskin Home now, and the exact spot of the Olathe marker and the memorial. Pins were looked at on the Kansas state aerial; the Ryckert's corner building could not be picked out with certainty (prec area). None of the five has a Commons photograph that fits (photo_find recorded none for each).
