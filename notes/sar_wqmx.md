## What this batch is
Sixteen places in western and northern Saratoga County, drawn at random by `choose.py --claim --state ny` (region `sar`); the Atlas had nothing in Charlton, Galway, Providence, Greenfield or Rock City Falls. Ruins and traces: the Hawkwood mansion foundations, windmill base and lone fireplace in the Town of Ballston's Anchor Diamond Park; the Hoyt lime kiln and quarry at the State Museum's Lester Park; the Granger glass factory site (Mount Pleasant, 1840s) in Lake Desolation State Forest; George West's paper-mill hamlet at Rock City Falls; the Ballston Terminal trolley bed and Pioneer Mill dam in Boice Family Park. Standing: the George West House (The Mansion of Saratoga), Charlton hamlet with the Freehold church and the historical society's museum in the 1804 St. Paul's church, the West Charlton "Scotch Church", Calvary Episcopal at Burnt Hills, the Packer farm and Barkersville store, the Copeland Carriage Shop and the Barker General Store at Beecher Hollow, the Batchellerville church (moved ahead of the reservoir), Smith's feed store at Elnora, the Jonesville Store and Grooms Tavern with the Grange hall (the last two were lead rows in `notes/sar_pgzb.md`, now deleted). Sources: Wikipedia as raw text; HMdb marker pages (nearby lists round Galway, Rock City Falls, Corinth and Charlton gave most of the ruins); Register forms with text for Calvary, the Copeland Carriage Shop, the Packer farm and Jonesville Cemetery; Saratoga PLAN, NYS DEC (state forest page and the 2002 draft unit management plan), Friends of the Kayaderosseras, the Town of Milton, the Charlton Historical Society, Lakes to Locks Passage, the Town of Clifton Park, The Mansion of Saratoga, the Saratoga Business Journal, Atlas Obscura and nnywaterfalls.com. Every pin was looked at on the aerial and the LiDAR sheet; George West House, Jonesville Store, Grooms Tavern and Hawkwood were moved with `pix.py`, Packer farm moved to the Barkersville marker. Red brick: four set from Wikipedia photographs (Jonesville Store and West Charlton church no; Barker store some, a brick basement corner; Smith's store some, a brick chimney); twelve left at '?'.
Not checked: anything on the ground. Pins that mark only an area, not the thing: Hawkwood (on LiDAR hollows that may be the house; the HMdb positions of the Hawkwood markers disagree with the welcome panel), the glass factory (marker by the DEC lot; furnaces not located), Rock City Falls (hamlet marker), Boice park (park point; trolley bed and dam not located), Packer farm (road triangle), Copeland Carriage Shop (beside the covered bridge point). What stands of each Rock City Falls mill and who owns them; Lester Park's hours (the museum page did not open); present use and owners of the Barker store, Smith's store (the Wikipedia photograph, undated, shows a "Main Street Grille" sign) and the West house; service times of the four churches; Grooms Tavern's hours; whether the Boice Family Park lot on Creekside Drive is a lot (the aerial shows the road); Wikipedia's Barker store Underground Railroad claim has no source behind it.
