## What this batch is

The Carnegie libraries of Iowa in towns M to Z: 44 places, from Wikipedia's state list, the Carnegie Libraries in Iowa Project and Wikipedia's articles, with hours from the libraries' own sites where they gave them. One is gone: Newton, torn down in 1994. Sioux City's main library was already on the map and was left alone.

## What was not checked

- Wall colour: almost no page opened gives it. Brick is `no` only where a page says stone or stucco (Marshalltown, Mason City, Monticello, Ottumwa, Waterloo East); everything else is `?`. `tools/brick.py` could settle the rest from pictures.
- Hours: libraries whose own sites would not load (Malvern, Mount Ayr, Osceola, Oskaloosa, Woodbine) or gave none (Manchester, Tipton, Villisca, West Liberty) have `hours='?'`. Maquoketa's site lists times framed as when staff can be reached, so it is `?` too. Museum hours (Montezuma, New Hampton, Perry, Sheldon) were not found.
- Waterloo East Side: Wikipedia's list says 715 Mulberry Street and its article 626; the pin is an `area` pin on 715.
- Storm Lake and Tama: what the buildings are used for today was not settled.
- Parking: no page gave any.

## Leads not finished

- Sanborn: the project says the Carnegie building stood on West 2nd Street and still stands; 407 Main Street is the 2007 library. Find the old building.
- Stuart (111 NE Front Street): the project marks it gone; nothing says when.
- Waverly: Wikipedia's list says city offices at 100 2nd Street SW, but Waverly City Hall is on 1st Avenue NE and the project's record for it looks garbled.
- Spencer: razed 1970; the site was not found.
