## What this batch is
Twenty standing buildings in downtown Providence and on College Hill, taken from the names in one walk-through video of old Providence photographs (the record is `source/videos/20240309_i0RZv9-m84Q.md`; the video was only the tip). Added: the Arcade, the Providence Athenaeum, the Old Stone Bank, the Joseph Brown House (old Providence Bank), the Market House, the First Baptist Meeting House, Beneficent ("Round Top") and Central Congregational churches, the Cathedral of Saints Peter and Paul, City Hall, the County Courthouse, the John Hay Library, the Providence Public Library, the unfinished Masonic Temple (now a hotel), the Biltmore, the Federal Building, Loew's State Theatre (PPAC), the Majestic Theatre (Trinity Rep), the Turk's Head Building and the 1898 Union Station. Facts come from Wikipedia read as raw text and the National Register list for Providence. Service times and hours were read on the owners' own pages (Athenaeum, First Baptist, Beneficent, Central Congregational, the cathedral's parish page, City of Providence). Pins are address-lookup PointAddress results or Wikipedia points; all were looked at on the aerial, and the Federal Building pin was moved about 54 m onto the building. Not checked: anything on the ground; hours for the Arcade, the John Hay Library, the Public Library (its pages answered 403), PPAC and Trinity Rep (pages 404/403), the hotels and the Union Station food hall; parking (city streets and garages, no lot named); red brick for nine places with no source or picture that settled it.
