## What this batch is
Eighteen places in southern Orange County, New York (region `midh`, drawn at random by `choose.py --claim --state ny`, which offered Warwick; the Atlas had three places within 15 km of Warwick and nothing at all between Tuxedo and Monroe). Iron country first: the Southfield Furnace ruin, the Sterling Furnace (Lakeville Ironworks, where the 1778 Hudson chain was made) and the Sterling and Lake mine plant ruins in Sterling Forest State Park, the Sterling Mountain fire tower, Clove Furnace at Arden (the Orange County Historical Society's home), and the Pine Swamp Mine with its miners' village foundations in Harriman State Park. Standing places: Tuxedo station (Bruce Price, 1885), the Smith Clove Quaker meeting house and the Rushmore Memorial Library in Highland Mills, Museum Village in Monroe, the Hathorn stone house, and in Warwick village the Shingle House, Baird's Tavern and the Old School Baptist Meeting House; the Sugar Loaf hamlet district, the Yelverton Inn and the First Presbyterian Church in Chester, and the Seward Memorial in Florida. Sources: Wikipedia raw text, NYS Parks pages for both parks, the NY-NJ Trail Conference park and hike pages (secure.nynjtc.org reads; nynjtc.org/hike/print answers 404), the Orange County Historical Society, whsny.org (Warwick society), Museum Village, HMdb marker pages and newspapers, all through `page.py`. Not checked: anything on the ground; Baird's Tavern's opening hours (no page of its own found); when the Woodbury Historical Society opens the Rushmore library; the date of this year's Smith Clove worship; parking at Clove Furnace and in Sugar Loaf; who owns the Yelverton Inn now; whether the Sterling fire tower is open (the Trail Conference says only when manned). Clove Furnace's closing year (1877 or 1885) and the Sterling Furnace's building date (1751 or 1770) are given both ways. Pins that mark a general area: Sugar Loaf (the district marker). Brick was settled from Commons pictures for the Southfield Furnace (stone) and the Shingle House (red-brick chimney); eight places that show their Wikipedia picture were left '?' where no source said.

## Tips
- The Trail Conference's hike pages answer 404 at `nynjtc.org/hike/print/<n>` but read in full at `secure.nynjtc.org/hike/print/<n>`; each gives a GPS point for the trailhead and describes the ruins passed.
- HMdb nearby lists round Warwick, Monroe and Sterling Forest (5 miles each) gave the Sterling Furnace tablet's position and most of the Warwick, Chester and Florida markers in three requests.
- The Warwick historical society's site is `whsny.org` (the older `warwickhistoricalsociety.org` does not connect); its properties page describes all twelve buildings and its home page gives the office hours.
- `parks.ny.gov/documents/recreation/trails/SterlingForest*.pdf` (the trail guide chapters that search results list) answer 404.
