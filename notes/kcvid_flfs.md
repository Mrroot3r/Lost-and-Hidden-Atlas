## Tips
- `videos.py list` cannot tell a picture-only video from one that walks through named buildings. The 7-minute "Old World Kansas City" was a montage with no names; a very short running time against the channel's usual 20 to 60 minutes is a hint.
- `note.py` will not make a note for a batch with no data file; a video record with no places needs its note written by hand.
