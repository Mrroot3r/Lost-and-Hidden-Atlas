## What this batch is
Twenty-four places in the north of Otsego County, New York (region `mohw`, drawn at random by `choose.py`, which offered Cooperstown, Fly Creek and Fort Plain). Before this the Atlas had nothing within 20 km of Cooperstown. Nearly all are standing buildings. Cooperstown: the Otsego County Courthouse (1880, red brick), the 1935–36 post office with its Janowsky sculpture, The Smithy (1786, called the oldest building in the village), the Tunnicliff Inn (1802, brick), First Presbyterian (1805), Christ Church (brick, 1807; Cooper made it Gothic in 1840) with the Cooper family graves, and two places marked only by markers: the Otsego Hall site and Clinton's Dam at Council Rock. Round the lake: Hyde Hall and the 1825 Hyde Hall covered bridge in Glimmerglass State Park, Fenimore Farm & Country Village (its 1918 stone barns and moved buildings) and Kingfisher Tower (private, seen from the lake). Fly Creek: the 1856 water-powered cider mill, the 1838 Methodist church and the 1899 Grange hall (historical society museum). Also the Middlefield District No. 1 School, the Hartwick Seminary church (1839), St. Mary's at Springfield Center, the Baker Octagon Barn (private) and, in Cherry Valley, the museum, the cemetery with its 1877 massacre monument, First Presbyterian (1873), Roselawn (the limestone mansion) and The Tepee on US 20. Sources: Wikipedia through `wiki_raw.py`, HMdb marker pages (nearby lists round Cooperstown, Cherry Valley, Richfield Springs and Hartwick gave the positions and most of the texts), and through `page.py` the Hyde Hall and NY State Parks pages, Mohawk Valley Museums, the Episcopal Diocese of Albany, the Middlefield Historical Association, nyroute20.com, allotsego.com, theclio.com and Mohawk Valley Living. No National Register form was read. Not checked: anything on the ground; interiors of almost every building; hours for the courthouse, post office, churches, the Smithy, the Middlefield school and the Fly Creek museum's open-house dates; the Cherry Valley Museum's hours come from nyroute20.com, not its own page; owners of the Cherry Valley cemetery, Roselawn and the Hartwick Seminary church. Pins moved: Hyde Hall (Wikipedia's point was on the beach lot), the post office and St. Mary's (OpenStreetMap points). Pins that are markers, not the building: Hartwick Seminary church (area), Clinton's Dam, Otsego Hall. The Baker Octagon Barn and the Fly Creek Grange pins are `area`: the building could not be told apart on the aerial. Red brick was seen on Wikipedia pictures for the courthouse and post office (yes), Hyde Hall, Kingfisher Tower and the Fenimore Farm barns (no); nine places are left `?`.
