## What this batch is
Seven places in Overland Park, Merriam, Shawnee and Spring Hill, Johnson County, Kansas: the Strang Carriage House, Strang Depot and Strang Line Car Barn (the surviving pieces of Overland Park's interurban founder), the 1927 Aztec Theater in Shawnee, the Campbell Home in Merriam, the Boy Scouts' Statue of Liberty replica at Shawnee Mission North High School, and Spring Hill's South Main Street district. This is fewer than the 15 to 20 asked for: two earlier batches (ks_dvmh, ks_hqwz) and ks_jcwr had already taken nearly every Johnson County Register listing worth a pin, and what is left on the Register list is mostly private houses with a one-line summary. Facts come from the Clio driving tour of Olathe, Overland Park and Shawnee, Wikipedia read as raw text, the Overland Park Historical Society's visit page, the City of Spring Hill's pages and the Kansas Historical Society's register search. Not checked: anything on the ground; opening times for the carriage house and car barn (none found); whether the Discover O.P. museum is the depot (the society's page gives no street address); owners of the Campbell Home and the statue; which Spring Hill buildings are oldest. The Spring Hill listing date is from the Kansas register search (State Register, December 9, 2000); the Register nomination was not read. Pins: the carriage house pin sits in a small park and the aerial cannot pick out the building, so it is within about 60 m by Commons photograph distances. Pictures: the carriage house got one; the Aztec, Campbell Home, depot and Spring Hill got none (Commons had only other places' pictures). Red brick is unchecked ('?') except the two limestone buildings ('no'). HMdb.org answered HTTP 403 from here, so the marker list for the county could not be used.
