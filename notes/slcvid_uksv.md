## What this batch is
Fourteen places in Salt Lake City (and Saltair, by Magna), taken from the buildings named in one walk-through video of the city's old buildings; the video was only a tip, and its record is source/videos/20220831_RsChr2pfY8k.md. Added: the Salt Lake Tabernacle and Temple, the Cathedral of the Madeleine, First Presbyterian Church, the City and County Building, the Boston and Newhouse Buildings, Hotel Utah (Joseph Smith Memorial Building), the Tribune and Walker Bank buildings, the Masonic Temple, the Union Pacific and Rio Grande depots, West High School's 1922 building, and the Saltair resort site with its pilings. Facts are from Wikipedia (read raw with wiki_raw.py), SAH Archipedia, and the owners' own pages (the cathedral, First Presbyterian, Temple Square, Preservation Utah's Masonic Temple tour page, the city's licensing office page) and a KSL report on the Rio Grande depot after the 2020 earthquake. Not checked: anything on the ground; interiors; parking for every place; hours for Hotel Utah and the Union Pacific depot hotel (the hotel's page refused, Temple Square's hours page now opens its home page with no times); where the Saltair pilings are best seen from public ground; the Tribune Building's and Boston and Newhouse pins sit on a roof the aerial cannot name (prec site); First Presbyterian's pin is the Wikipedia coordinate. Red brick on the Tribune Building is from a Commons photograph.
