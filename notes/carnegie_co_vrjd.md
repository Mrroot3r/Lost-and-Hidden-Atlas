## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Colorado (34 places): 28 standing buildings (nine Denver branches among them, counting the Central library) and 6 sites of torn-down ones, including the University of Denver's academic library. From Wikipedia's state list and articles, National Register nominations, SAH Archipedia, History Colorado, Denver Public Library's history pages and the operators' own pages. Colorado Springs' main Carnegie library was already on the map. Hours only from the operators' pages.

## What was not checked

- Several notes in Wikipedia's list are out of date: Delta's library moved out in 2023 (now sheriff's offices), Manitou Springs' came back in February 2025, Florence closed as a library in 2003.
- Warren branch, Denver: the library history gives 3354 and 2825 High Street; pinned at 3354 as 'area'.
- Elyria branch, Denver: pinned at 47th Avenue and High Street ('area'); Wikipedia gives 1901 East 47th Avenue.
- Pueblo: a photograph shows a stone McClelland Public Library dated 1902; whether that was the Carnegie building was not settled.
- Hours not found for Brush, Idaho Springs, Leadville's museum, Trinidad; Littleton's restaurant hours not looked up.
- Emhoolah branch was closed for building repairs through November 30, 2026 when read.
- The Sterling nomination names a private owner; left out.
