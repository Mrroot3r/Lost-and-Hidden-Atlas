## What this batch is
Four places in Kansas City, Missouri, taken from the leftover names in the earlier note mo_kcl_xtvn: the Mutual Ice Company Building in Westport (now the Westport Ice House), the Louis Curtiss Studio Building, the Union Confederate Monument in Union Cemetery, and the site of the Red Crown Tavern (the 1933 Bonnie and Clyde shootout). The ground was meant to be Lexington, Cass County and the rest of the Kansas City area; the Register lists for Lafayette, Cass, Ray, Jackson and Johnson counties and the Kansas City building lists were compared with the place lists and almost nothing was missing, so the batch is small on purpose. Sources: Wikipedia read with wiki_raw.py, the National Cemetery Administration page for the monument, the Platte County Landmark newspaper, Tessere's and Flatland KC's pages for the ice plant, and web search summaries (the Register entry for the ice plant, which gave Rudolf Markgraf as architect and its former names, and the Curtiss studio's address). The Register forms for all of these are not digitized on NPGallery and could not be read. Not checked: interiors, the ice house businesses' hours, whether the Curtiss studio's front is glass (one search result says a glass curtain wall; Wikipedia's article says nothing), the Red Crown marker's text and exact spot (HMdb and the marker-project page would not open), and whether a visitor may walk to it across the company lot. The pin for the Curtiss studio is from an OpenStreetMap address lookup; Wikipedia's coordinate is about 400 m south. The Red Crown pin is the Wikipedia coordinate and marks an area, not a structure.
