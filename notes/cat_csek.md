## What this batch is
Seventeen places in Sullivan County, New York (region `cat`, drawn at random by `choose.py --claim`, which offered Windsor, Narrowsburg and Bovina Center; the Atlas had only the Hankins stone arch within 25 km of Narrowsburg). The Upper Delaware: Roebling's Delaware Aqueduct with its toll house and towpath trail, Minisink Battleground Park (1879 monument, Hospital Rock), the Tusten stone arch bridge (1896, on Boy Scout land, open along the NPS Tusten Mountain trailhead) and the 1856 Ten Mile River Baptist Church, the 1894 Arlington Hotel in Narrowsburg (arts alliance galleries), the c. 1850 Cochecton Erie freight house (moved and re-erected), Callicoon's 1899 depot, Western Hotel and Main Street (one district entry), and St. Joseph's Seraphic Seminary (now a Job Corps center, view only). Inland: the D&H Canal Linear Park in Mamakating (locks, dry dock, the Boothroyd house), the Kenoza Lake stone arch bridge, the Van Tran Flat, Beaverkill and Halls Mills covered bridges, the 1821 Bloomingburg Dutch church, the Rivoli Theatre in South Fallsburg, and the Ohave Shalom (Woodridge) and Agudas Achim (Livingston Manor) synagogues. Sources: Wikipedia as raw text (`wiki_raw.py`, Register list with `--rows`), the Callicoon district form (NPGallery 16000390, the only one of nine forms tried that has text), and with `page.py` the NPS Upper Delaware hours page and places pages, Sullivan County's park pages and 2024/2025 brochures, HMdb markers (nearby lists round Narrowsburg, Callicoon, Tusten and Cochecton), the Town of Cochecton, the NY State Covered Bridge Society, gribblenation.org, the River Reporter, the Pike County Courier, Spectrum News and the Times Hudson Valley. Not checked: nothing on the ground; the DEC Beaverkill campground's rules and fees; service times for the two synagogues and the Ten Mile River church; the Rivoli's schedule; the Cochecton station's opening; whether the Bloomingburg church still opens by appointment; the Arlington's hours come from a 2026 newspaper notice, not the alliance's own site (403). Pins that are not on the thing itself: the Cochecton station (area pin on Route 97, building not picked out), Agudas Achim (area pin on Rock Avenue; Wikipedia's point fell on a house), the Ten Mile River church (pin in the clearing taken to be its churchyard). Sources disagree on Kenoza's date (1873, 1880, 1882), the Van Tran bridge's length, whether Halls Mills is open to walkers, and Minisink's season; both versions are given.
