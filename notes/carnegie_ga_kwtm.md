## What this batch is

26 places: 24 standing, 2 gone. Public libraries at Albany, Americus, Atlanta (main library site, the Carnegie Education Pavilion made from its front, Anne Wallace Branch, Auburn Avenue Branch site), Barnesville, Boston, Cordele, Cuthbert, Dawson, Dublin, Eatonton, Fitzgerald, Lavonia, Montezuma, Moultrie, Newnan, Pelham, Rome, Savannah (Bull Street, East Henry Street) and Valdosta; academic ones at Georgia Tech, the old State Normal School in Athens (UGA) and Mercer. No Georgia Carnegie library was on the Atlas before.

Sources: Wikipedia's state list and articles, Register records and nominations, the libraries', museums' and colleges' own pages, the University of North Florida's historic architecture photographs, Vanishing Georgia, BlackPast, HMdb, Columbus State's archives.

## What was not checked

- Pins were looked at on the aerial. Anne Wallace, Dawson and Lavonia are street-interpolated points (area).
- Anne Wallace Branch: a bank branch when listed in 2003; its use now was not checked.
- Hours not found on an operator page for Boston, Cordele, Eatonton, Fitzgerald, Lavonia, Pelham and both Savannah libraries. Valdosta's museum page was last updated in 2005.
- Brick colour known only for Albany, Lavonia, Athens (yellow) and Moultrie, Anne Wallace (red).
- Dates disagree for Cordele (1904 or 1915), Cuthbert (grant $7,000 or $12,000; dedicated 1918?), Boston (1912 or 1914) and the Auburn Avenue branch (closed 1949 or 1959); both are given.

## Leads not finished

- Columbus: the list says razed; Wikipedia's library-system article says the building is now offices. Sold in 1947 to the Muscogee Manufacturing Company; stood on Mott's Green at the north end of Broadway. Settle whether it stands.
- Atlanta South Branch: a row in the list with no address or source.
- Agnes Scott College (Decatur): built 1910, later a student centre, said demolished 1986; where it stood not found.
- Atlanta University: a Carnegie library by 1905 (razed, says the list); where it stood not found.
