## What this batch is
Fourteen standing buildings in downtown Albany, named in one Old World Exploration video (record: source/videos/20240419__cumgD2Ht04.md) and each then looked up: City Hall, Court of Appeals Hall (the old State Hall), the County Courthouse, the old Albany Academy, the Executive Mansion, the Smith State Office Building, the State Education Building, SUNY Plaza (the D&H building), the Old Post Office, the Albany Trust Company and City Savings Bank buildings, the Kenmore and DeWitt Clinton hotels, and Union Station. Four of them (City Hall, the Academy, SUNY Plaza, Union Station) replace thin lm- landmark pins with was=. Sources: Wikipedia read as raw text (wiki_raw.py), the Historical Society of the New York Courts, Historic Albany Foundation, the Kenmore Ballroom's page and WAMC (page.py), and the Empire State Plaza tours page (tours of the Executive Mansion suspended). Not read: the State Education Department's tour page (503), the Court of Appeals' visitor pages (403) and the City of Albany's City Hall page (connection reset); the hours for those three are as web search results quote them, and each access note says so. Not checked: interiors of most buildings, the DeWitt Clinton hotel's lobby hours, and red brick for eight places (left '?'). Pins: all on their buildings on the aerial; four Wikipedia coordinates were moved 20 to 51 m onto the building.
