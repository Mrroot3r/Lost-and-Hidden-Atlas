## What this batch is
Fourteen places in Johnson County, Kansas, where the earlier passes had used up the National Register lists: the old downtown of Edgerton (the 1906 bank now a library, the 1904 Grange Hall, Assumption Catholic Church, the 1913 Methodist church), De Soto (the Clear Creek Grange Hall, the 1908 Masonic building, the 1879 stone Presbyterian church, the 1919 high school now city hall), Old Town Lenexa (the 1917 grain elevator, Salem Lutheran Church, the Strang Line waiting station) and Olathe (First Christian Church, Second Baptist Church, the Strang Line car barn). All come from the Kansas Historical Society's 2009 Johnson County survey report (`jocosurveyreport2009.pdf`, `pdftotext -layout`, its "Recommendations for further research" list and the town chapters), which several earlier batches also used; its "recommended for further research" list still had these unused. Second sources were read only for the Edgerton library (Johnson County Library's own pages, which give the hours) and the Strang waiting station (Lenexa Historical Society pages). Everything else rests on the survey alone and says so. Not checked: interiors, who owns or uses the Edgerton Grange Hall, Clear Creek Grange Hall, De Soto Masonic building and Lenexa elevator today, service times (no congregation's own page was read), and the red brick of the Methodist church and the elevator. Pins are address lookups checked on the state aerial; the waiting station pin was placed by eye beside the caboose. Only De Soto and Edgerton pictures: Commons had none for any of the 14.
