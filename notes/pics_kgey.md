# Pictures batch kgey

A pictures run (job 8): 34 places came up; 23 got a picture, 7 were answered "none" by me, 4 were recorded as none by the tool itself (nothing on Commons, including Kaubisch Memorial Library, Osceola and Owensville; a Lansingburgh church was an earlier none the tool kept).

Where I hesitated:
- Lakewood Public Library: the only picture of the Carnegie building is a 1916 historic photograph (tif); chose it over a distant modern view of the replacement library.
- Ann Arbor Carnegie facade: chose the photo of the saved facade (candidate 5), not the 1938 photo of the whole library, because the pin is the surviving facade.
- Village minier de Bourlamaque: an area pin; chose a clear view of a log house at the pin over street views.
- Pierceton and Salem, Oberlin, Linton, Delafield: several equally good views; took the clearest front view. Delafield's is taken through a windshield but shows the hatchery building.
- Scottsburg: only a close-up of the historical marker, so none.
- Charlotte, Franklin, Plano, Washington Court House, Noblesville, Portland, Milan, Berkshire Block: candidates showed other buildings or other places, so none.
