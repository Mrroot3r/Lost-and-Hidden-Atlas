## What this batch is
Every Carnegie library in Arizona (Phoenix, Prescott, Tucson, Yuma), New Mexico (Las Vegas, Raton, Roswell) and Hawaii (the Hawaii State Library in Honolulu): 8 places, 7 standing and 1 gone (Raton). Wikipedia's lists give no academic Carnegie libraries in these three states. Built from Wikipedia's lists and articles, National Register forms (Phoenix, Prescott, the Library Park district in Las Vegas), SAH Archipedia, the libraries' own pages, the Sharlot Hall Museum and the New Mexico State Library. None was on the Atlas before.

## What was not checked
- Pins were looked at on the aerial. Las Vegas is OpenStreetMap's library point; Raton is the middle of Ripley Park, because nobody says where in the park the library stood.
- Phoenix's brick is left unsettled: Wikipedia says red brick, SAH Archipedia says slightly reddish buff brick.
- Brick not checked for Yuma, Raton, Roswell or Honolulu. No picture was opened.
- Tucson's Children's Museum hours were not read from the museum's own page. Phoenix exhibits are by appointment only (SAH Archipedia), so Phoenix is marked look from the street.
- Roswell: the newest source is Wikipedia, which says it was privately owned offices auctioned in 2014. Its list calls it vacant. Whether anyone uses it now was not found.
- Some dates differ between sources and both versions are given: Prescott (grant 1899 or 1889, cost $8,242 or $8,252, closed 1974 or 1975), Phoenix (closed 1952 or 1953), Tucson (closed 1990 or 1991), Yuma (renovated 2008 or 2009), Las Vegas (grant 1902 or 1903).
