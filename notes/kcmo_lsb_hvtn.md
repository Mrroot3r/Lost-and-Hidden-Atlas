## What this batch is
Four places, far fewer than the 15 to 20 aimed for: the Chicago & Alton House (a vacant 1878 hotel) and the Dillingham-Lewis Museum in Blue Springs, the Todd M. George Sr. House in Lee's Summit, and the Grandview Residential Historic District. The Atlas had 46 places within 22 km of Lee's Summit and 9 within 6 km at the start; this batch makes it 50 and 13. Two earlier sessions (kcmo_jcse and kcmo_lsr_pwzk) had already taken nearly every Register listing here, so what was left were houses and districts with little written about them. Sources: a KSHB news report, Clio, Waymarking (quoting the Grandview nomination), a paranormal group's write-up for the Dillingham house history (the only account found, so weak), Wikipedia as raw text, and the Lee's Summit parks page. National Register forms were not readable (NPGallery placeholders, Missouri State Parks copies 404). Not checked: anything on the ground, the Dillingham-Lewis Museum's hours (its society's site would not open), parking. Both Blue Springs pins fall on the same block and could not be told apart on the aerial, so both are `area` pins. The Todd George pin lands on a building among offices and apartments; its picture is a Craftsman house 26 m away from a neighbouring district and may not be this house. O'Bannon Homestead, which I had written up, was already on the Atlas from kcmo_lsr_pwzk and was dropped. The Bailey Family Farm was dropped because its Register point falls inside a new subdivision on the aerial.
