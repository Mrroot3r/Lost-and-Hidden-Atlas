## What this batch is
Eighteen places on the Greene County Mountaintop and in Pine Hill, Ulster County (region `cat`, drawn at random by `choose.py --claim --state ny`, which offered Long Eddy, Onteora Park and Tannersville; the Atlas had the Haines Falls hotel sites and the tanneries but nothing in Tannersville, Hunter village, Windham, Ashland, Lexington, Prattsville village or Pine Hill). Haines Falls: the 1913 Ulster and Delaware depot (Mountain Top Historical Society, open), Layman's Monument on the Escarpment Trail (1900 or 1901: the sources disagree, `conf='mixed'`) and the Kiersted tannery site in Kaaterskill Clove (HMdb marker says a few foundations remain; pin is the marker, `prec='area'`). Hunter: the fire tower (hike from the Spruceton Trail lot), the 1909–1914 synagogue with the IOOF hall in its text. Tannersville: Main Street district, All Souls (Onteora), St. John the Evangelist. Prattsville: the Zadock Pratt Museum (hours from the museum's own home page), the 1785 Laraway Inn (private), the Reformed Dutch Church. Ashland's Trinity Church, Windham's Mitchell Hollow Union Chapel, the Lexington House. Pine Hill: the hamlet district (pin on the Ulster House Hotel), both stone arch bridges, and the old schoolhouse museum. Sources: Wikipedia raw text and the Greene County Register list, HMdb markers, mths.org, zadockprattmuseum.org, DEC's Hunter-West Kill and Kaaterskill Wild Forest pages, Catskill Mountaineer, Hudson Valley One. NPGallery gave placeholders for all 18 Greene County forms tried. Not checked: anything on the ground; service times for every church and the synagogue; whether the Prattsville church is repaired after Irene; the Shandaken museum's hours (only a 2018 news article); the St. John the Evangelist and Mitchell Hollow pins (Wikipedia coordinates in trees; the building could not be picked out); the Trinity Ashland pin was moved onto the building at the HMdb marker, not confirmed as the church.

## Tips
