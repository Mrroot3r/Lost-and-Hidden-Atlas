## What this batch is
Fourteen places in south and east Jackson County and Cass County, Missouri: Fort Osage at Sibley, the Rice-Tremonti House at Raytown, the Truman Farm Home at Grandview, Unity Village, St. Paul's Episcopal Church and the downtown district at Lee's Summit, Missouri Town 1855, the Lone Jack museum and battlefield, the Blue Springs pastors' home, and the Harrisonville and Pleasant Hill town centres with their depot and church. The Atlas had seven places within 20 km of Lee's Summit before. Sources: the National Register list and Wikipedia articles read as raw text (`wiki_raw.py`), the Lone Jack Historical Society and NPS pages through `page.py`, and county, chamber, parish and visitor pages as web search results quote them. No Register form was read (NPGallery holds only a placeholder, the Missouri State Parks PDFs answer 404). Nothing was checked on the ground. Hours for Fort Osage, Missouri Town and the St. Paul's services come from search-result text, not from a page read in full; Rice-Tremonti, Lone Jack and Pleasant Hill have no hours. Three Wikipedia coordinates were wrong on the aerial and were moved (Truman Farm Home sat on a shopping centre, Missouri Town 2 km off in woods, Pleasant Hill district on houses). The Lee's Summit district and the Christian church pins mark the area only. Pictures: five found on Commons, the rest recorded as none.
