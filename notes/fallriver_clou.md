## What this batch is
Fifteen standing places in Fall River, where the Atlas had nothing: the Academy Building (Borden Block), Central Congregational Church (The Historic Abbey), the Church of the Ascension (now Church of the Holy Spirit), the 1886 B.M.C. Durfee High School (now a probate and family court), St. Anne's Church and Shrine, the Cathedral of St. Mary of the Assumption, St. Patrick's Church, Oak Grove Cemetery, the Fall River Waterworks (pinned on its 1875 water tower), the Durfee Mills, the Metacomet Mill, the former Truesdale Hospital, the Jesus Marie Convent, St. Joseph's Orphanage and the Fall River Public Library. The names came from one walk-through video of the city (the record is source/videos/20250502_jdfCD1GGyIw.md); the video was used only as a list of names. Facts come from Wikipedia read as raw text (the articles, the city's National Register list and the list of mills), the Diocese of Fall River's page for the cathedral, and the library's own hours page. Not checked: church service times (no parish page of its own gave them; the directories that do were not used), the cemetery's opening hours (the city's page gives none), interiors, and which building on Anawan Street is the 1847 Metacomet Mill (its pin is an area pin on the Register point for the American Printing Company group). The Church of the Ascension's Wikipedia and Register coordinate lies about 320 m south of its address, 160 Rock Street; the pin is on the address point and marked site. Red brick was settled from Wikipedia photographs for the Academy Building and Central Congregational (yes) and Truesdale Hospital (no: pale yellow brick), and from sources that say granite or marble for the stone churches, the school, the mills and the waterworks; the convent, the orphanage and the cemetery are left "?". Nothing was checked on the ground.
