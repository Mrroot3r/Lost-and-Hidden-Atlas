## What this batch is
An "hours" session (PROMPTS.md type 7): no new places and no pins. New York had no church left that this session could do (its two waiting churches, Smith Clove and the Old School Baptist Meeting House in Warwick, open only once a year or for rentals), so the list came from `tools/hours.py list --kind worship` across every state: 42 churches were waiting, 30 were looked up.

Hours recorded (14), each from the page of whoever runs the place, read in the session, with the street address or town on the page checked against the place:
- Christ Church, Washington Parish, DC: washingtonparish.org (620 G Street SE).
- St. Michael and All Angels, Anniston AL: stmichaelsanniston.org (1000 West 18th Street).
- St. George's Cathedral, Kingston ON: stgeorgescathedral.ca (Kingston postcodes, the cathedral's own site).
- Mission Nombre de Dios, St. Augustine FL: missionandshrine.org/location-and-hours/ (101 San Marco Avenue).
- St. Peter's Lutheran, Dell Rapids SD: lcdr-sd.org (701 Orleans Ave N, the address Wikipedia gives for the 1902 church; the congregation was renamed Lutheran Church of Dell Rapids).
- St. Augustine, Isle Brevelle LA: Diocese of Alexandria parish page (2262 Highway 484, Natchez).
- St. Anne, Spanish Lake LA: Diocese of Alexandria page for St. Francis of Assisi parish, which lists St. Anne's at 4310 Hwy 485, Robeline.
- Historic St. Mary's Mission, Stevensville MT: saintmarysmission.org tours page (315 Charlo Street; guided tours Tue to Sat, mid April to mid October).
- St. Benedict's, Nebraska City NE: the parish site stben-nc.solutiosoftware.com (411 5th Rue).
- Appoquinimink Friends Meetinghouse, Odessa DE: Friends General Conference meeting page (622 Main Street). It gives the Sundays but no clock time.
- Renner Lutheran Sanctuary SD: rennerlutheran.com (Renner; Wikipedia says the 1878 building is still in use).
- Immaculate Conception, Downieville CA: St. Canice parish, Nevada City, which lists the Downieville Mass.
- St. Luke's Episcopal, Granville OH: stlukesgranville.org (its calendar and Granville events; no street address printed on the home page).
- St. Paul's Episcopal, Port Townsend WA: stpaulspt.org.

Not checked: nothing was seen on the ground and no telephone call was made.
