## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Kansas, towns A to L (30 places): 27 standing buildings and 3 sites of torn-down ones (Great Bend, Halstead, Hays). The sources are Wikipedia's state list, the Kansas group nomination of Carnegie libraries and the single National Register nominations on npgallery, Kansas Historical Society pages, and the libraries' and towns' own sites. Hours come only from the operators' own pages.

## What was not checked

- Hours were not found on the operators' own pages for Cherryvale (two pages give different times), Columbus, Concordia, Dodge City, Downs, Fort Scott, Goodland, Independence, Kingman and Lyndon, so these are `hours='?'`.
- Canton: the library gives 203 North Main Street and the National Register gives 300, so the pin is 'area'.
- Great Bend, Halstead and Hays are gone; their pins are on the sites as the sources give them, at street level.
- Emporia: the Kansas Historical Society's Emporia Carnegie entry is the College of Emporia's Anderson Memorial Library, an academic building. Whether the downtown public library building is listed on its own was not settled.
- Walls come only from pictures or sources opened; the others are `brick='?'`.
