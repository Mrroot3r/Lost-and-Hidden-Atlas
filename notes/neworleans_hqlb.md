## What this batch is
Sixteen standing buildings in the French Quarter, the Central Business District and the Lower Garden District of New Orleans, from the names in one video tip (the record is source/videos/20240913_6HokPHEPoWY.md; the video is not a source). Added: St. Louis Cathedral, the Cabildo and the Presbytère on Jackson Square, the Old Ursuline Convent, the U.S. Custom House, Gallier Hall, Confederate Memorial Hall, the Hibernia Bank Building, Hotel Monteleone, the Roosevelt (Hotel Grunewald), the Saenger and Orpheum theatres, the facing Irish and German churches of St. Alphonsus and St. Mary's Assumption, St. Patrick's Church and the Jackson Brewery. Facts from Wikipedia read as raw text; hours from the Louisiana State Museum's pages (Cabildo, Presbytère), the Confederate Memorial Hall's page, Old St. Patrick's Mass-times page, the Seelos Shrine's page (St. Mary's Assumption) and the Shops at Jax Brewery's page, all read with the web fetch tool or page.py. Not checked: hours for the cathedral (its site answers 403), the Old Ursuline Convent (its site's host would not resolve), St. Alphonsus, the two hotels and the two theatres (recorded as `?`); parking for every place; whether the Custom House's Marble Hall is open to the public; red brick for most places (left `?`, except the Confederate Memorial Hall, from a Commons photograph, and three stone, marble or stuccoed buildings from their articles). Pins: OpenStreetMap points, all looked at on the aerial; St. Mary's Assumption moved about 50 m onto its OpenStreetMap building, and the Hibernia Bank Building placed by an address lookup on a tower in shadow (marks the site).
