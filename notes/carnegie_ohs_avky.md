## What this batch is

Thirty-eight Carnegie buildings in central and southern Ohio, the last Ohio batch of the pass the owner asked for to put the buildings Andrew Carnegie and the Rockefellers paid for on the map:
- Cincinnati's nine Carnegie branches (West End's site is gone) and Norwood.
- Miami University's Alumni Hall, Middletown and Lebanon.
- Dayton's East branch and West site, Germantown, Miamisburg, Greenville and Xenia.
- Wilberforce and Cedarville.
- Wilmington, Washington Court House, London and Ripley.
- Chillicothe, Coshocton, Delaware, Gallipolis, the Marietta library on the Capitolium mound and Marietta College's Irvine building.
- Middleport, Mount Sterling, Pickerington, Pomeroy and Portsmouth.
- Marysville (gone), Otterbein's Clippinger Hall and Cambridge.

Northeast Ohio is in `carnegie_ohne_tjfb` and northwest Ohio in `carnegie_ohnw_wjcj`.

## Not checked

- **Pomeroy:** its present use (law offices) and its number on East Second Street come only from Wikipedia's list. The pin is marked area.
- **Marysville:** the address of the demolished library comes only from Wikipedia's list, and what is on the spot now was not found.
- **Wilberforce:** the pin is the HMdb marker's point, which falls on a lawn. Which campus building is the library was not settled from the aerial, and sources differ on whether it is museum offices or still a library.
- **Middleport:** the pin falls among houses and trees on the aerial.
- **Cedarville:** the street number comes only from Wikipedia's list.
- **Hours not found:**
  - Avondale: it reopened in November 2026 and its page still showed "closed".
  - Germantown's museum: the city says only that it is open from May to December.
- **Chillicothe:** Monday hours were not confirmed.
- **Lebanon:** hours are as listed for one week in February 2026, because the library's home page would not load.
- **Unconfirmed claims:**
  - Waymarking says Dayton East is on the National Register.
  - Middletown's owner after 2024 was not found.
- **Middleport:** the architect differs between sources (Packard or Kircher).
- **Pictures:** Commons pictures were chosen for Alumni Hall (Miami) and Gallipolis, the places with no Wikipedia picture; Commons had none of Dayton East. Red brick on Alumni Hall and buff walls at Gallipolis were read off those pictures. The other brick values come from the sources named in each entry.

## Tips for the next session

- **Cincinnati:** Eira Tansey's paper on Cincinnati's Carnegie branches (a University of Cincinnati pre-print) gives exact opening dates and costs for all nine branches. The library's own history pages, chpl.org/about/history/<branch>/, give the rest.
- **Lookalike sites:** marysville.lib.nckls.org is Marysville, Kansas, and leblib.org is Lebanon, Indiana.
- **Waymarking** has a page for most Ohio Carnegie buildings, with the architect, but it is a third-party source.
