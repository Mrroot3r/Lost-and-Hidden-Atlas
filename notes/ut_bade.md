## What this batch is
Eighteen places spread across Utah, which had 35: in the south-west Fort Pearce, the Pine Valley Chapel and tithing office, the Jacob Hamblin Home, the St. George Tabernacle, Parowan's Old Rock Church, the old Beaver County Courthouse and the Panguitch historic district; in the middle the Torrey log church, the Spring City district and the Helper museum (Old Helper Hotel); in the east Wolfe Ranch in Arches, the Dewey Bridge remains, the Bank of Vernal and the Josie Bassett Morris ranch at Cub Creek; in the north the Benson Grist Mill, the Corinne Methodist church, the Box Elder Tabernacle and the Beehive House. Facts are from Wikipedia (tools/wiki_raw.py, Register lists with --rows), the pages of whoever runs each place read with tools/page.py (BLM Fort Pearce and Dewey Bridge, NPS Arches and Dinosaur, the church's location pages for Pine Valley, the Hamblin Home and the St. George Tabernacle, Tooele County's mill page, Helper City's museum page), the 2022 Spring City Register amendment (Utah SHPO PDF), Utah Humanities, HMdb, Moab Happenings, KSL and the Deseret News. Pins were looked at on the aerial; Fort Pearce was moved onto the walled ruin (plainly visible), the Parowan church, Torrey log church, Helper museum and Box Elder Tabernacle onto OpenStreetMap points; the Beaver courthouse and Corinne church were moved onto the likeliest building but not confirmed by a picture. The Dewey Bridge "what is left" comes from 2025 Commons photographs, not a written source. Not checked: anything on the ground; hours for the Beaver and Parowan Daughters of Utah Pioneers museums (their directory and the city page would not open), the Benson Grist Mill (county page gives none), the Box Elder Tabernacle's summer tours, the Pine Valley Chapel's summer tour times; park fees at Arches and Dinosaur; Arches timed-entry rules. Pine Valley Chapel's date disagrees (Wikipedia 1868, the church's page 1872–73) and Fort Pearce's builders (BLM: 20 men; Wikipedia: six), both given. Pins that mark an area only: Panguitch and Spring City (districts), Dewey Bridge (towers not picked out on the aerial).

## Tips
- tools/photo_find.py look on six places took about twelve minutes (Commons waits); run it in the background from the start.
- Wikipedia's "Dewey Bridge (Utah)" redirects to Utah State Route 128; the BLM recreation-site page and Commons photographs say more about what is left.
- dupinternational.org (Daughters of Utah Pioneers museum directory) and parowan.org's page for the Old Rock Church would not open; DUP museum hours stay unchecked.
- The church's location pages (churchofjesuschrist.org/learn/locations/<name>) give weekly hours for the Hamblin Home and St. George Tabernacle; the Pine Valley page says only "contact the chapel".
