## What this batch is
Eighteen standing landmarks of central Washington, taken as leads from one walking-tour video of the city's old buildings and each checked against its own Wikipedia article: the Capitol, the Library of Congress's Jefferson Building, the Old Post Office, the Willard and Mayflower hotels, Healy Hall at Georgetown, Memorial Continental Hall, the Pan American Union Building, the Cannon House Office Building, the National Savings and Trust building, the Old Patent Office, the Renwick Gallery, the Eisenhower Executive Office Building, the House of the Temple, the Perry Belmont House, M Street High School, the City Post Office (National Postal Museum) and Union Station. Hours come from the Capitol Visitor Center, the Library of Congress, the Smithsonian American Art Museum (main building and Renwick), the Scottish Rite and Union Station pages. The DAR, Organization of American States and National Postal Museum pages refused to open, and the National Park Service page for the Old Post Office tower could not be found, so those hours are not checked; the two hotels have no hours. Pins are Wikipedia's coordinates and all sit on their buildings on the aerial; Healy Hall's is on a campus building that could not be told from its neighbours, so it is marked as the site. No parking points: every place is in the city centre near a Metro station. Red brick is set only where the article names the material.
