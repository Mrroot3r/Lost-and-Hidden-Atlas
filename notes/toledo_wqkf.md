## What this batch is
Fourteen places in Toledo, Ohio, where the Atlas had nothing within 15 km. Nine come from the names in one Old World Exploration video about the city (the record of every name is `source/videos/20230122_W6cUcDA_7_k.md`; the video is a tip and nothing rests on it): the Nasby Building, the Second National Bank Building (Edge21 Apartments), the Ohio Building, the Blade Building, the Central YMCA, the Secor Hotel, the Old Central Post Office (Jefferson Center), Rosary Cathedral, and the Toledo State Hospital's old cemetery with its state marker (the hospital itself is mostly razed). Five more stand within a few blocks downtown and were added from the Lucas County Register list: the Lucas County Courthouse, the Valentine Theatre, the Pythian Castle, Trinity Episcopal Church and the Historic Church of St. Patrick. Facts are from Wikipedia (read with the raw-text tool), the Clio page and the city library's architecture collection for the Secor, the Ohio History Connection's marker text and a University of Toledo news story for the cemetery; the Blade Building's opening date is from a search result quoting the newspaper's own history page, which would not render, so it is marked mixed. Hours are from the cathedral's own visit page, the parish bulletin of St. Patrick's (July 2026), the Episcopal Church's listing of Trinity, and the Valentine Theatre's visit page. Pins are ArcGIS address points, three moved onto the building (cathedral, Blade, Trinity) from OpenStreetMap or the aerial. Not checked: interiors of every building except where a source describes them, current use of the Nasby, Jefferson Center, Pythian Castle and Blade buildings (all were changing hands or being renovated in the sources), whether the 1960s cladding is still on the Nasby, whether visitors may walk into the State Hospital cemetery (the pin is the marker by Arlington Avenue), downtown parking fees. Red brick: the Secor is red brick from a 2022 photograph; the cathedral (granite), St. Patrick's and the post office (sandstone) are not; the rest are unset.

## Tips
- The Lucas County Register list is one page ("National Register of Historic Places listings in Lucas County, Ohio"); there is no separate Toledo list. Several downtown Toledo Register forms (Secor Hotel, Toledo Traction power station) are placeholders at NPGallery.
- The ArcGIS geocoder gave PointAddress results for every downtown Toledo address tried; three still fell beside the building and needed moving.
- toledoblade.com pages come back as navigation only, to both page.py and the web fetch tool.
- Remarkable Ohio marker pages carry the whole state's marker list first; the marker's own text and its latitude and longitude are at the very end of the page.
