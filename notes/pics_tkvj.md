# Pictures batch pics_tkvj

**What this batch is.** Photographs chosen from Wikimedia Commons for places already on the map that had none (job 8). No places, pins or place text were changed. Only `tools/pics_tkvj.py` and this note are in the pull request.

**Counts.** 25 places looked at by hand: 17 got a picture, 8 got "none". The tool also recorded about 11 more as none by itself (nothing on Commons, or nothing new since an earlier look). Commons login: logged in.

**Got a picture (17):** Marietta, Westerville (Clippinger Hall), Schoharie (Vroman), Cleveland South Branch, Whiting, Napoleon, Crawfordsville, Grinnell, Howard (Carnegie Building), Alexander Majors House, Lebanon OH, Bingham-Waggoner, Detroit Lakes, Sigourney, Mansonville round barn, Gallatin jail museum, Norwich Pharmacal warehouse.

**None (8):** Glenwood IA (only a Minnesota library), Litchfield MN (elevators and trucks), Winchester IN (aerial and an Oregon college), Ames (municipal building and churches), Madison OH (town sign, church, shops), Superior Washington Block (Washington D.C. streets), Metropolis (courthouse only), Petoskey (modern county building only).

**Where I hesitated.**
- Howard Carnegie Building: chose the brick portico building 39 m from the pin (candidate 4); no caption names it as the Carnegie building.
- Sigourney: the picture at the pin shows a small brick library with a pedimented entry; the place text says Classical Revival, and the photo is a plain small building. Chosen because it was 1 m from the pin and names the library.
- Whiting: chosen by name only (no position); a red-brick Richardsonian building fits a 1906 library.
- Vroman House: chose the one at the pin (a distant view across a creek) over the clearer 1785 house photo 33 m away, which may be a neighbouring building.
- Napoleon: by name only; red brick Carnegie look fits.

**Tips.** None for the guide.
