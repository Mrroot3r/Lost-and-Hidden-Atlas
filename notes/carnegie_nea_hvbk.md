## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Nebraska, towns A to L (33 places): 27 standing buildings and 6 sites of torn-down ones, from Wikipedia's state list, the 2018 National Register document "Carnegie Libraries in Nebraska" (its inventory gives year, architect, style and whether each stands), single nominations, the Nebraska Library Commission's history pages and the operators' own sites. Hours only from the operators' pages.

## What was not checked

- Present use comes only from the Library Commission's undated founding-dates table for Aurora (church), Bloomfield (historical society), Columbus (offices), David City (house), Franklin (art gallery), Grand Island (law office) and Lexington (law office).
- Gothenburg: the state list and the 2018 document say it is the library, the Library Commission says privately owned. Set to look from the street.
- Chadron's library moved to a temporary site in March 2026 for a renovation of the Carnegie building; whether it has moved back was not found, so hours are '?'.
- Holdrege's 2025 plan for a new library says the present library building would be removed; it does not say whether that means the 1907 Carnegie part.
- Alma (111 John Street or 1114 2nd Street) and Aurora (812 or 819 12th Street) have two addresses; pins are 'area'.
- Walls were not settled from pictures; most are `brick='?'`.
