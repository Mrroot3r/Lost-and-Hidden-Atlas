## What this batch is
Eleven places that open the Lower Peninsula, which had none of its own besides Detroit, Marshall, Saginaw and the Straits: five Lake Huron and Lake Michigan lights (Old Mackinac Point, Big Sable Point, Sturgeon Point, Old Presque Isle, Pointe aux Barques), the Au Sable light on Lake Superior, the Glen Haven village and the Port Oneida farm district in Sleeping Bear Dunes, the Packard Plant ruin in Detroit, the Ramsdell Theatre in Manistee and the Ypsilanti Water Tower. Facts come from Wikipedia read with wiki_raw.py. Hours, fees and access come from web search results quoting the managers' pages (Mackinac State Historic Parks brochures, Michigan DNR, Alcona Historical Society, NPS, Presque Isle Township, the Ramsdell's ticket policy, the Pointe aux Barques museum), because page.py could not open the Big Sable site, the NPS Au Sable page or others; every one is marked as a web-search quote and none is the 2026 schedule for the off-season sites. Not checked: the colour of the brick at Sturgeon Point, Old Presque Isle and Packard (brick '?'), what survives inside Old Presque Isle, daily times at Au Sable, Glen Haven and Port Oneida, the length of the Big Sable walk. The pins at Glen Haven, Port Oneida and Packard mark an area, not one building. Pins at Pointe aux Barques and Au Sable were moved onto the buildings after the Wikipedia point fell in the water.
