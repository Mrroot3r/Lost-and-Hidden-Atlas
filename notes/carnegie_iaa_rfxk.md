## What this batch is

54 Carnegie library buildings in Iowa: the public libraries of towns A to L and the college buildings (Cornell, Drake, Grinnell, Simpson and Upper Iowa), standing and gone, as part of the owner's pass to put every building Andrew Carnegie paid for on the map. Dubuque's Carnegie-Stout library was already on the map and was left alone. Towns M to Z come in the next batch. Sources: Wikipedia's state list and articles, the Carnegie Libraries in Iowa Project (carnegielibrariesiowa.org), Prairie Rivers of Iowa, Drakeapedia, Grinnell College's library history, the National Park Service, and the libraries' own sites for hours.
