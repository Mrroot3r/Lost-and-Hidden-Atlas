## What this batch is
Seventeen standing buildings named in one walk-through video of old Detroit (Old World Exploration, "Old World Detroit";
the record is source/videos/20220523_pyXMlPDhK-U.md): the Nancy Brown Peace Carillon and the Anna Scripps Whitcomb
Conservatory on Belle Isle; downtown, the Book Tower, the Westin Book Cadillac, the Broderick Tower, the David Whitney
Building, the Dime Building (Chrysler House), the Wayne County Building, the Grand Army of the Republic Building, the
Guardian and Penobscot buildings, the Detroit-Leland Hotel and Old St. Mary's Church in Greektown; north of downtown, Old
Main at Wayne State (the old Central High School), the Detroit Masonic Temple and Cadillac Place (the General Motors
Building); and the Russell A. Alger Jr. House (Grosse Pointe War Memorial) in Grosse Pointe Farms. Facts come from
Wikipedia read one article at a time; pins from the ArcGIS geocoder's address points or OpenStreetMap, each looked at on
the aerial (St. Mary's moved 31 m off a parking lot onto the church). Belle Isle hours and the Recreation Passport rule
from the Michigan DNR's Belle Isle page and the Belle Isle Conservancy's front page; Masonic Temple tours from its own
tour page. Not checked: hours of the three hotels (Book Tower, Book Cadillac, Whitney) and of the War Memorial (left
hours='?'); Mass times at St. Mary's (the parish site answered 403); whether the Guardian Building's lobby is open now;
inside of every office building; red brick for eight places (no source said what they are faced with, or the colour).
Nothing was checked on the ground.
