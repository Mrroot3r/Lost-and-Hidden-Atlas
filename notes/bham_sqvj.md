## What this batch is
Thirteen standing buildings in downtown Birmingham and the Highlands just south of it, all named in a video walk-through of the city's old buildings (the names were a tip only; the record is source/videos/20241108_MR2W5a75L38.md). Added: the Alabama Power Building; three of the four towers on the "Heaviest Corner on Earth" (Woodward, Empire, John Hand); First United Methodist, First Presbyterian, the Cathedral of St. Paul, St. Mary's-on-the-Highlands and Temple Emanu-El; Boutwell (Municipal) Auditorium; the Lyric Theatre; the Thomas Jefferson Tower; and the Tutwiler Hotel (Ridgely Apartments). Sources were read with tools/wiki_raw.py and tools/page.py: SAH Archipedia (very good for Birmingham), the National Register forms on NPGallery (text layers read for First Presbyterian, Empire, Woodward, Ridgely), the Encyclopedia of Alabama, Bham Now, and the churches' own pages for service hours (St. Paul's, First Presbyterian, St. Mary's). Pins come from OpenStreetMap or ArcGIS address points and were all looked at on the aerial; Boutwell is marked site because the point falls between two roofs. Not checked: anything on the ground; parking (only private lots are mapped downtown, so no parking point was given); hours for the hotels, the Lyric's shows, First United Methodist and Temple Emanu-El; when the present St. Mary's church was built; the colour of the brick at St. Paul's, Boutwell, the Thomas Jefferson and Temple Emanu-El. Sources disagree on the Ridgely/Tutwiler's date (1913 or 1914) and height (eight or nine storeys).
