## What this batch is
Fifteen standing buildings in Bay City, Michigan, from one walk-through video of old Bay City buildings (Old World Exploration, "Old World Bay City Michigan"; the record is source/videos/20260424_00xc5Vx6vNY.md). Added: Bay City Hall, the Masonic Temple, the Crapo Building (The Legacy), the Shearer Brothers Block, First Presbyterian Church, Elm Lawn Cemetery, the Pere Marquette depot, the Sage Library, St. Stanislaus Kostka Church, the Swart Block, the Armory (now the county historical museum), the Charles Whitney residence and the Trombley House, all named or shown in the video; and Trinity Episcopal Church and the Bay County Building (which replaced the old courthouse the video names), which were not. Sources: Wikipedia read with wiki_raw.py, SAH Archipedia entries MI-01-BY1 to BY13 read with page.py, the Bay County Historical Society's pages, Route Bay City (Second Wave Media) walking-tour articles, the Shearer Block condominium association, a Michigan Building Trades report on the Crapo Building, the Our Lady of Czestochowa parish pages, the Bay County Library System's Sage page, a state marker's text as printed by Lost in Michigan, and Cinema Treasures for the Washington Theatre. Not checked: nothing on the ground; service times for the three churches (no page of their own could be read); City Hall and county office hours; brick colour for the Crapo, Shearer, Swart, Armory, Whitney and St. Stanislaus buildings ('?'). The Shearer block's year (1884 or 1886), the Crapo's (1890 to 1892) and some Swart dates differ between sources and are given both ways. Downtown parking was not looked up place by place. Pins are address points looked at on the aerial; two were moved (the depot onto its building, St. Stanislaus to OpenStreetMap's church point).
