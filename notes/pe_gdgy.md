## What this batch is
The first places in Kings County, Prince Edward Island (the three earlier PEI batches stop at Orwell and Wood Islands). Twenty-five places: Georgetown's red-and-grey sandstone Kings County Courthouse (1887, W. C. Harris), Holy Trinity Anglican (1842, deconsecrated, town-owned), St. David's United (1837–41) and the Georgetown Historic Inn (1840 Wightman house); the Roma at Three Rivers settlement remains (1732–45) and Brudenell Island's pioneer cemetery; Knox's Dam, the ruined Montague hydro plant (1907–55); Montague's red brick post office (1887–88, now the Garden of the Gulf Museum) and its 1906 station; the Elmira station (1912, end of the line); five lighthouses (East Point with its fog alarm building, Souris, Cape Bear with the Marconi station that heard the Titanic, Panmure Head 1853, St. Peters Harbour) and the Annandale rear range light; St. Mary's, Souris (Harris sandstone, rebuilt inside after 1929), All Saints, Cardigan (1874) and St. Paul's, Sturgeon (1873–88, Harris's first stone church); the Basin Head cannery (1941); the St. Peter's circuit courthouse (1874–75); the St. Alexis Acadian mission graves at Rollo Bay (1804); Souris Town Hall (1905 sandstone post office) and the Matthew and McLean store; and the vacant Little Sands church (1898). Every place has its Canadian Register statement, read with `page.py --all -n 60000` (ids from the `{{HPC row}}` templates of the Wikipedia "List of historic places in Kings County, Prince Edward Island", already in `research/wikiraw/`). Hours: Tourism PEI attraction pages (dates only, except East Point's 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.), the PEI Lighthouse Society's pages (Cape Bear, East Point; they disagree with Tourism PEI on the season, both given), the Diocese of Charlottetown's February 2026 Mass table (its day columns run together in the text layer, so only Sturgeon's one weekend Mass reads cleanly; Souris and Cardigan are given as far as they read), the Georgetown Historic Inn's page. Brick from the Register text, and for Garden of the Gulf (red brick), Holy Trinity (none seen) and Elmira (red brick chimney) from the Register's own photographs. **Not checked:** anything on the ground; owners of Brudenell Island, Knox's Dam, the St. Alexis ground, the Annandale and St. Peters Harbour lights (all view-only); whether the Kings County Courthouse, the Montague station welcome centre (Tourism PEI page 403) or Souris Town Hall's library open, and their times; St. David's and the St. Peter's Courthouse programme. **Pins that mark only an area or an uncertain building:** St. Paul's, Sturgeon (no church found at the Register/address point on Route 17A; prec area), St. Alexis Mission (area), Brudenell Island (island centre), Garden of the Gulf Museum (falls on Main Street at the bridge end), Montague station (falls on the marina green), St. Peters Harbour lighthouse (dune top; tower not picked out). Holy Trinity, Elmira station and St. Peter's Courthouse were moved onto buildings that are probably, not certainly, the right ones. No 1 m LiDAR in PEI, and the US land map says "not on mapped public land" everywhere.

## Tips
- historicplaces.ca answered all 31 statements in one loop with an 8-second pause and three tries per page; none reset this time. Cutting the saved text at 4,500 characters lost the endings (owner, present use): keep the whole statement, or cut after "Source:" instead.
- The Diocese of Charlottetown's Mass PDF loses its column boundaries in `page.py`'s text layer: a parish row reads as a run of times with no days. Only a parish with a single time (Sturgeon) can be given with confidence.
- `look.py` at 50 to 70 m half-width gives DOWNLOAD FAILED (dark red) for PEI; 100 m works and is enough to pick out a lighthouse by its shadow.
- In rural PEI the Register list point, the ArcGIS PointAddress and often the OpenStreetMap point are the same spot and fell on roads, lots or woods for six of 25 places (Sturgeon, Elmira, Souris church and lighthouse, Panmure, All Saints); look at every one.
- tourismpei.com's Montague Welcome Centre page answered 403 while its other attraction pages read fine.
