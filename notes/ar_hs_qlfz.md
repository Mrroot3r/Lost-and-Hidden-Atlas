## What this batch is
Fifteen standing buildings in Hot Springs, Arkansas, taken as leads from the names in one Old World Exploration video (the record is `source/videos/20260918_rSBHEmJVHyc.md`; the video was a tip only, nothing in the entries comes from it). Four on Bathhouse Row and beside it (the Hale, Maurice and Ozark bathhouses, the Ohio Club), the 1924 Arlington Hotel, the Medical Arts Building, the empty 1933 Army and Navy Hospital, the Garland County Courthouse, four churches (First Presbyterian, St. Luke's Episcopal, Central Methodist, Visitors Chapel AME), the 1914 high school (now lofts), the Missouri Pacific depot (now the bus station) and the 1901 Old Post Office. Facts come from Wikipedia (`wiki_raw.py`, the Garland County Register list), the National Park Service's Hot Springs pages (`page.py`) and the Encyclopedia of Arkansas (Ohio Club). Pins come from OpenStreetMap buildings and address lookups, each looked at on the aerial; three were moved onto their buildings, and the Old Post Office is an area pin. Not checked: anything on the ground; most hours (only the Ohio Club's kitchen and St. Luke's Sunday services, the latter from a search result quoting the parish page); whether the Medical Arts hotel conversion is finished; the Old Post Office's present use; who owns the Army and Navy Hospital. The Kansas City video (DjVrDC2ikaU) was drawn first and dropped: its transcript names no building.
