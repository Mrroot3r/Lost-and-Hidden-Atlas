## What this batch is
Six places in Independence, Missouri, a second pass after kcmo_ind_wvxq: the 1827 Log Courthouse, the Kritser House, the Truman site's visitor center in the 1928 fire station, the Temple Lot, the Community of Christ Auditorium and First Presbyterian Church. Fewer than the 15 to 20 aimed for: Wikipedia has no article for most of the town's Register houses, the Register forms could not be read, and the other Lee's Summit, Raytown, Grandview, west and east side parts had already been worked. Sources: Clio's Independence Heritage Trail, the City of Independence page for the log courthouse, the National Park Service hours page, Wikipedia as raw text. Not checked: interiors, hours for the fire-station visitor center (the page gives only days), the Auditorium's and the church's visiting times, brick (left '?' where no source says), and the Kritser House's exact spot (the pin marks the area; it sits among trees on the aerial). Pictures: the Log Courthouse and the fire station have none on Commons; the church has a pre-1923 postcard.
