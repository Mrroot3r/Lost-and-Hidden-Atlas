## What this batch is
Fourteen places in Akron, Ohio, where the Atlas had only the Carnegie library within 15 km, from the names in one Old World Exploration video about the city (the record of every name is `source/videos/20260227_VXfG4pjqO-A.md`; the video is a tip and nothing rests on it). Standing: the Summit County Courthouse, the 1899 post office (now the oldest part of the Akron Art Museum), the 1929 federal post office, the First Congregational Church, St. Bernard's Church, the Hower House and Stan Hywet Hall (both house museums), the YMCA and YWCA towers of 1931, Firestone Plant No. 1 with its neighbours, and the B.F. Goodrich complex (Canal Place). Ruins and grounds: the burned shell of old St. Paul's Episcopal Church (1884-85; fire 2018; demolition ordered in 2025 and under appeal), Glendale Cemetery with its 1875 Civil War Memorial Chapel and 1883 bell tower, and the Cascade Locks of the Ohio and Erie Canal with the Mustill Store. Sources: Wikipedia, the Park Service records for each listing, the Firestone Register form (the only Akron form with a text layer), the museums' and the diocese's own pages, Signal Akron, Clio, Akron Life and the University of Akron's Rubber City Revisited. Not checked: whether old St. Paul's still stands after the October 2025 appeal; Stan Hywet's hours (its site is drawn by script) and the Mustill Store's; the Glendale gate hours (its site would not connect); First Congregational's service times; the present use of the Firestone buildings and which Goodrich buildings are in the 2020 district (no form read, `conf='mixed'`). The 1929 federal building's pin is within about 100 m (the aerial did not show which building it is). Nothing was visited on the ground.

## Tips
- The Akron-Summit County library keeps scans of the Register forms at `https://www.akronlibrary.org/images/SpecCol/<reference number>.pdf`, but almost every one tried (13 of 14) is a scan with no text layer; the B.F. Goodrich district form (100005529) has a little text.
- stanhywet.org is drawn by script and its old addresses answer 404; glendalecemeteryakron.org would not connect; cascadelocks.org answers only its front and about pages.
- The Diocese of Cleveland's parish directory (`dioceseofcleveland.org/parishes/directory/<parish>`) gives Mass times in a form `page.py` reads.
