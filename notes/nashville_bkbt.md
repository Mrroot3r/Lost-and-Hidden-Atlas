## What this batch is
Ten standing places in Nashville, Tennessee, taken from the buildings named in one video about the city's old architecture (Old World Exploration, "Old World Nashville"; the record of every name and its outcome is source/videos/20220505_ZGJvi-Gfq1g.md). The video was used only as a list of names. Added: the Parthenon in Centennial Park, Jubilee Hall at Fisk, the Downtown Presbyterian Church (Strickland's Egyptian Revival church), Belmont Mansion, Riverwood, the old Customs House on Broadway, the Arcade, the Scarritt College buildings with Wightman Chapel, Kirkland Hall at Vanderbilt and the Peabody College campus. Sources: Wikipedia read with wiki_raw.py; National Register forms for Jubilee Hall, the Customs House, the Arcade, Scarritt College and Peabody College (NPGallery text layers, read with form.py and page.py); hours from the Parthenon's, Belmont Mansion's, the Downtown Presbyterian Church's and the Scarritt Bennett Center's own pages. Not checked: the Riverwood pin (an address point; the house itself was not found for certain on the aerial, prec area) and whether the house can be seen from Welcome Lane; which Peabody building is the old Industrial Arts Building; the Arcade's shop hours; visitor parking at Vanderbilt and Peabody; every interior except where a source describes it. Red brick is '?' for seven places: the sources say brick without its colour, and no picture was looked at. Riverwood has no free picture on Commons.
