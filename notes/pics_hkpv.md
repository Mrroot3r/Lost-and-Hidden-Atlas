# Pictures batch pics_hkpv (2026-11-22)

What this batch is: a pictures job (PROMPTS.md job 8). No new places and no pins. It gives a photograph to places already on the map that had none, by looking at Wikimedia Commons candidates from `tools/photo_find.py next 5`. The only file is `source/tools/pics_hkpv.py`.

Counts:
- Places that got a picture (chosen by looking): 5. Clintons Ditch (Cohoes), Licking County Courthouse (Newark, OH), Tunstall-McSween Store (Lincoln, NM), Holland Congregational Church (VT), Manoir Mauvide-Genest (Quebec).
- Places I said none for after looking: 5. Fort Elfsborg site, D&H Canal linear park (Mamakating), First Baptist Church (Ballston Spa), Kellogg's Dam (Amsterdam), Tremper House site (Phoenicia).
- Places the tool recorded as none by itself (Commons had nothing near the pin, under its names or in a category of its own): 10 (Black River Canal locks, Conanicut battery, Gilbertsville cemetery, Glastenbury, Irwin Brothers store, Lime Rock kilns, Long Pond Road cellar, Old Yankton bridge, Scott ice house, Van Vleeck house).
- Places looked at in the first two rounds: 10. Five places from the third round (Glenaladale House, Keystone Bridge, Hotel Kaaterskill, Idaho City, First National Bank of Huntsville) had sheets made but were not looked at, so they were left unrecorded and still wait.

Stopped early: the job's 40-minute limit ran out during the third round, so the job is about a third of its target (about 30 places had a look).

Where I hesitated:
- Clintons Ditch: the chosen picture (Harmony Mills, a gnome, and a canal) has a garden gnome in the foreground. The mill and the canal walk are the subject; the gnome is a figurine, not a person. Taken 38 m from the pin.
- Fort Elfsborg: the earth fort is gone. The shoreline view was 900 m off and showed no fort, so none.
- Tunstall Store: a colour exterior was chosen over the HABS survey perspective (candidate 3), which shows the same building. Either would fit.
- Holland Congregational Church: candidate 4 chosen (a full side view with the steeple) over candidate 5 (the church in a field, 56 m from the pin).
- Manoir Mauvide-Genest: candidate 2 (wide view, chapel on the gable) chosen over candidate 1, a close frontal view at 3 m from the pin.
- Kellogg's Dam: an 1809 French drawing of an oil mill interior was rejected as a drawing, and the Penang and Romanian mills as other places.
