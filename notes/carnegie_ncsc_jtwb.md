## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of North Carolina and South Carolina (carnegie_ncsc_jtwb): 29 places. North Carolina 15 (12 standing, 3 gone): public libraries at Charlotte (site), Durham, Greensboro (main library site; Carnegie Negro Library at Bennett College), Hendersonville, Hickory (Elliott-Carnegie), Murphy, Winston-Salem and Rutherford College (site); college ones at Johnson C. Smith, Davidson, Guilford, Livingstone, UNC Greensboro (Forney Building) and UNC Chapel Hill (Hill Hall). South Carolina 14, all standing: Anderson, Beaufort, Camden, Charleston (Library Society), Darlington, Gaffney, Honea Path, Kingstree, Latta, Marion, Sumter, Union, Converse College and Winthrop (Rutledge Building). No Carnegie library in either state was on the Atlas before. Sources: Wikipedia's two state lists and articles; Plummer Alston Jones Jr.'s survey in North Carolina Libraries (2024); NC State's architects database; the Hickory and Sumter Register forms; Open Durham; the South Carolina Picture Project; SAH Archipedia; HMdb; county, town, college and university pages.

## What was not checked

- No pin was looked at on the aerial. Hill Hall's OpenStreetMap address reads oddly; Hendersonville is a street corner; JCSU is the university's address; Latta is street-interpolated (area); Murphy's point is beside the courthouse.
- Hours were read only for Murphy (the county's page gives summer hours; the town's page gives others). All other libraries, museums and centers have hours '?'.
- Brick colour known only for Gaffney (red). Hickory's 1979 wing is dark red; the old part's colour was not stated.
- Present use not confirmed lately for Durham (offices in a 2007 photo), Winston-Salem (a church since 1954; its name not found), Gaffney (county offices after 1972) and Darlington (museum, future unsettled).
- Sources disagree (both given): Hickory grant ($13,250 or $14,000) and opening day; JCSU's last year as library (1967, 1969 or 1978); Winthrop (1961 or 1969); Camden and Marion opening years; Gaffney addition (1937 or 1938); Winston-Salem address (211 West 3rd or 411 North Cherry); Greensboro grant.

## Leads not finished

- Andrews, NC: pulled down in 1979 and replaced by the present library; whether on the same lot not found.
- Greenwood, SC (open 1917 to 1958, pulled down in the 1960s), Benedict College in Columbia and Furman University in Greenville: all gone; where they stood not found.
- Spartanburg, SC: opened 1906, pulled down about 1956; one photo site puts the Kennedy Free Library on Magnolia Street, not enough to pin.
