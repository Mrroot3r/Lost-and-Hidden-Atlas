# Pictures batch mdqz (scheduled pictures run, 22 November 2026)

What this is: a pictures-only pull request. It adds tools/pics_mdqz.py and no new places, pins or text about places. The first 30 places listed as waiting by `photo_find.py list --n 30` were looked at, five at a time, from their candidate sheets.

Result: 10 places got a picture and 20 got none.

Pictures chosen (10):
- boise-county-courthouse-idaho-city: candidate 1, the brick building with a front porch (Ken Lund, CC BY-SA 2.0). The Idaho World building in other candidates is a different building and was not chosen.
- first-methodist-cohoes: candidate 2, the brick church at 121 Remsen Street, named for the Vineyard Community Church (Kenneth C. Zirkel, CC BY 4.0).
- benedictine-heights-hospital: by name only (Kat17pho, CC BY-SA 4.0). Hesitated: the file's caption says "under construction" while the picture shows an empty, boarded hospital. The name, the town and the five-storey brick front match the description, so it was taken.
- babcock-smith-house-westerly: candidate 2, the house itself (Magicpiano, CC BY-SA 4.0). Candidate 1 is the carriage house and was not chosen.
- leitch-collieries: candidate 1, a stone ruin with arched openings, taken 245 m from the pin (Wilson Hui, CC BY 2.0). Hesitated: the distance is large, though the file is named for the colliery and the stone building matches the sandstone power house in the description.
- walters-blacksmith-shop: by name, the main entrance on Kempt Street (Marc-Lautenbacher, CC BY-SA 4.0). Hesitated: a by-name photo, but the caption and the description agree.
- cohoes-city-hall: candidate 2, the whole building from the street (Kenneth C. Zirkel, CC BY 4.0). The entrance close-up was not chosen, as it is a detail.
- audrey-headframe-jerome: by name, Audrey Shaft Headframe Park (SidewaysSarah, CC BY 2.0). Hesitated: the headframe in the picture looks like a steel lattice, and the file's description calls it the largest wooden headframe in Arizona. The place is the same headframe, so it was taken, but a person may wish to check it.
- st-johns-cohoes-library: candidate 2, the stone church from the street (Kenneth C. Zirkel, CC BY 4.0).
- trout-creek-school-32: candidate 1, the one-room school at 8 m (Daniel Case, CC BY-SA 3.0).

Places with none (20), and why:
- elder-mill-covered-bridge: only a historical marker sign was found.
- ishpeming-municipal-building: a cornice corner against sky; the others were mineral specimens. Not confirmed as City Hall.
- beaver-kill: Lincoln Park with the Empire State Plaza, not the ravine.
- maxon-mills-wassaic, boght-road-dutch-houses-colonie, international-paper-office-corinth, empire-brickyard, uigg-baptist-church, empire-lock-29, east-line-meeting-house, triangle-no-1-petrolia, lidar-bradt-hollow-cellar, lidar-nickerson-hill-house-site: no free Commons photograph within the search radius names the place.
- lumber-district: Interstate 787 and 90 views only.
- suburban-park-unionville: car-show photos of Plymouth Suburban cars only.
- lester-park-lime-kiln: stromatolite rock only.
- quappelle-town-hall: a 19th-century watercolour only.
- st-marys-waterford: by name; the one photo is a cropped view, partly hidden by a tree and wires. Not confirmed.
- farmington-canal-lock-13-hamden: close-ups of wall remnants in woodland; the lock form does not show clearly.
- muldoon-training-barns-belfast: the nearest candidate (23 m) is a white clapboard building labelled as the Bare Knuckle Boxing Hall of Fame. It may share the barns, but this could not be confirmed, so it was left out. A person may wish to check whether that building is the training barns.

Not done: nothing was skipped because of a network error; the 429 on Wikimedia Commons at the start cleared after a few minutes.

Tips for the next pictures session: many of the none results are places with no Commons photograph within 150 m or 400 m, so the next batch will also have many none; this is expected.
