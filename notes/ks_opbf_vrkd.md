## What this batch is
Four places in Overland Park and Stilwell, Johnson County, Kansas: Blackfeather Farm (National Register form read as text from NPGallery, plus Clio), the Graham Rogers House (a local history article; the Kansas register summary only for the style), the Overland Park State Bank building (Clio) and the Sapling Grove campsite park (National Park Service page). The batch is small on purpose: Johnson County's 46 register listings, the Wikipedia categories for Olathe, Shawnee, Lenexa and Overland Park and the Clio walking tour had already been used by five earlier batches, and what was left had no source I could open. Not checked on the ground: interiors, owners now, the park's hours. The Blackfeather pin is the street address and falls among the farm's outbuildings, not on the house. HMdb pages and Living New Deal answered 403, so marker texts were taken from Clio and search-result summaries only where said.
