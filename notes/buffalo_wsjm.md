## What this batch is
Nineteen standing places in downtown Buffalo and its near neighbourhoods, all named in one walk-through video (Old World Exploration, "Old World Buffalo", 2023; the record is source/videos/20230913_Q6Igtr4uY68.md). The video was used only as a list of names. Added: the Connecticut Street (74th Regiment) Armory, Blessed Trinity Church, the Electric Tower, Old Erie County Hall, the Old Post Office (SUNY Erie), City Hall, the Ellicott Square, Guaranty, Liberty and Buffalo Savings Bank buildings, Hotel Lafayette, Shea's Buffalo Theatre, St. Joseph Cathedral, St. Paul's Episcopal Cathedral, Westminster Presbyterian Church, Lafayette High School, Fosdick-Masten Park High School (City Honors), and the McKinley and Soldiers and Sailors monuments. Facts come from Wikipedia articles read with wiki_raw.py and the Register list rows; hours from the owners' own pages (diocese page for St. Joseph, spcbuffalo.org, wpcbuffalo.org, blessedtrinitybuffalo.org, sheas.org, explorebuffalo.org). Not checked: the Guaranty visitor centre's hours (the pages that give them refused or were 404), the Hotel Lafayette's own page, City Hall tour days (the tour page says noon but names no days; the encyclopedia says weekdays), whether the armory, County Hall, Ellicott Square court or Old Post Office court can be entered, and parking (downtown lots are private; no th= points given). Red brick from sources or the article photograph; five left as "?". Pins: each looked at on the aerial; the Buffalo Savings Bank pin was moved onto the dome and Shea's moved from a parking lot to its address point.
