## What this batch is

Fifteen state hospitals, asylums and schools for the "feeble-minded" and epileptic in Illinois (nine) and Indiana (six), for the themed pass on historic state hospitals: Jacksonville, Anna, Kankakee, Elgin, Manteno, Chicago State (Dunning, pinned on Read Dunning Memorial Park), Watertown (East Moline), Lincoln and Dixon in Illinois; Logansport, Richmond, Fort Wayne State School (site, now Northside Park), Muscatatuck, the Central State Hospital grounds (a second entry beside the Indiana Medical History Museum, with apart=) and Madison in Indiana. Peoria State Hospital and the Indiana Medical History Museum were already on the map. Sources: Wikipedia, the Park Service record for the Kankakee district, Asylum Projects, Kirkbride Buildings, the Indiana Archives and the state hospitals' own FSSA pages, the Indiana Historical Bureau marker, the Kankakee County Museum, the Allen County genealogy pages and the History Center, the Indiana National Guard and DVIDS for Muscatatuck, and news (WAND, NPR Illinois, Lincoln Daily News, Shaw Local, AP). Not checked: red brick on every place (all '?' but Dunning, no source said and no close picture was opened); which original buildings stand at Watertown, Dixon, Lincoln, Jacksonville and Madison (sources silent; the aerial shows old buildings); the Kirkbride footprints at Jacksonville, Anna and Elgin; Manteno's administration building on the aerial; the Logansport administration building is picked by its look; museum hours (Logansport, Richmond, Muscatatuck are by appointment, so `ask`).
