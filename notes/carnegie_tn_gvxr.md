## What this batch is

18 places: 14 standing, 4 gone. Public libraries at Brownsville, Etowah, Greeneville, Harriman, Jackson, Nashville (main site, North, East, Negro Branch site), Knoxville (Free Colored branch site) and the Mountain Branch veterans' home at Johnson City; academic ones at Fisk, Knoxville College (site), Lincoln Memorial, Peabody (Vanderbilt), University of Tennessee, Tusculum and Washington College. Chattanooga's Old Library Building was already on the Atlas.

Sources: Wikipedia's state list (from a 2002 thesis) and articles, Register records and nominations, the Nashville Public Library's pages, the museums', libraries' and colleges' own pages, the Park Service's Mountain Branch page, UT's Volopedia, BlackPast, Waymarking, Clio.

## What was not checked

- Pins were looked at on the aerial.
- Greeneville: present use unknown (2002 list: vacant); its 1974 listing is probably the Greeneville Historic District, whose form text does not mention it.
- Harriman: still a library per the 2002 list; no page of its own found for hours.
- Mountain Branch library (Building 17): not found on the map; pin is the campus. Visitor rules for the VA grounds not found.
- Knoxville Free Colored branch: the Nelson Avenue and East Vine Street corner is not on today's map; area pin.
- Washington College's Carnegie-Temple Hall: present use not confirmed (offices in 2002; school later an arts school).
- LMU and Tusculum hours: not on a page of the operator found; Clio lists Tusculum's hours.
- Brick colour unknown for most; Jackson is buff with darker orange brick, left as not checked.
