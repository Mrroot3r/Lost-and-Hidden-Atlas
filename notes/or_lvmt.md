## What this batch is
Ten places in nine Oregon counties, claimed with `choose.py --thinnest` (Oregon had 47): Willamette Falls Locks (Clackamas), Aurora Colony (Marion), Yaquina Bay Lighthouse (Lincoln), Coquille River Lighthouse (Coos), Fort Klamath site (Klamath), Fort Rock Homestead Village (Lake), Philip Foster Farm (Clackamas), Thomas Kay Woolen Mill in Salem (Marion), Flavel House in Astoria (Clatsop), Reid School in Bend (Deschutes). Facts from Wikipedia (wiki_raw.py) and the pages of the Old Aurora Colony Museum, the Willamette Heritage Center, the Philip Foster Farm and the Willamette Falls Locks Commission. Not checked: parking for all; interiors; whether the locks have reopened (Wikipedia expected 2026). Hours for the Yaquina Bay, Fort Klamath, Flavel House and Deschutes museums are `?` (only search-result quotes and aggregators; the Flavel and Klamath County pages gave 403/404/an archive address); Fort Rock's hours come from a search result quoting the society's 2026 page, which would not open. Brick is `?` except the Salem mill (Wikipedia says brick; the Commons photos show red walls). The Willamette Falls pin is `area`: the aerial shows the paper mill and works, and the chambers could not be told apart. Picture: one Commons photo chosen for the Salem mill.

## Tips
- Oregon museum sites often block page.py (Flavel House's clatsophistoricalsociety.org gives 403, Klamath County's page 404). A web search quoting the official page is the only route; say so in the access note.
- Wikipedia infobox coordinates for the Willamette Falls Locks fall on the Blue Heron paper mill beside it; use OpenStreetMap's point and `area`.
