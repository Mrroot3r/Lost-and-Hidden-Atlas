# Pictures batch pics_wjvk (20 November 2026)
tidied: 2026-11-26

This batch is a pictures run (job 8): photographs for 30 places already on the map that had none. No new places, no pins, no text. The tool took each address, credit and licence from Wikimedia Commons; the session only chose, by looking at each sheet.

Result: 10 places got a picture, 20 got none. The file is tools/pics_wjvk.py.

Places given a picture: Bannerman's Castle (Pollepel Island), Beebe Windmill (Bridgehampton), Carter-Archer House (Havre de Grace), Dodge County Courthouse (Mantorville), Gleeson Jail (Gleeson), Lake View Grange (Westport town hall), Othello Tunnels (Hope), St. Luke's Episcopal Church (Beacon), West Point Lighthouse (PEI), Wile Carding Mill (Bridgewater).

Places given none: Dobler Brewery, Thomas Hughes Library, Lidar Saranac River farmstead, Fort Plain, Grinnell Library, Fairholm, Fort Greene (Point Judith), Onesquethaw Valley, Yanney mill site, Coxsackie Light, Lidar Bradt Hollow, Fort Mims, Fort George (Lake George), Horton Grove, William Moore House (Cohoes), Wildwood Cemetery, Peoples Academy (Morrisville), O'Dell House (Annapolis Royal), Lidar Terry Mountain, Onesquethaw Reformed Church. Most had nothing on Commons within the search radius; the rest showed the wrong thing.

Where I hesitated:
- Lake View Grange (Westport): the picture is by name only (the file's own description names the hall as the Lake View Grange and the town hall). It matches the description (white hall, arched window). Taken as right.
- Othello Tunnels: the chosen view is from outside at the tunnel mouth over a footbridge, with two small figures in the distance; the subject is the tunnel. Taken as right.
- Carter-Archer House: by name only; a stone house seen from the front across the lawn. The description calls it a stone house, so taken as right.
- Bannerman's Castle: by name only, but taken from the river at 24 m from the pin, with the castle ruin as the subject. Taken as right.
- Fairholm (Charlottetown): a brick house with a plaque in the foreground was the only candidate that named the place. The house is small in the frame, so it was left as none.
- O'Dell House (Annapolis Royal): the only candidate naming the street shows the 1800s street; the building still stands, so it was left as none.
- Beebe Windmill: two candidates from the same file did not render (grey frames), so they could not be judged; the clear view of the mill was chosen.

Not checked: whether the chosen pictures are still on Commons at the same address after the next copy; the check ran once, on the day.

Tips for the next session: the Wikimedia look-ups sometimes ran longer than two minutes in one call. Splitting the look into batches of two or three worked. Rendering failures show as grey thumbnails; a grey thumbnail cannot be judged, so take the next clear candidate.
