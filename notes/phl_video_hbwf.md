## What this batch is
Eighteen standing buildings in Philadelphia, taken as leads from the names in one walk-through video of old photographs (job 9; the record of every name and what came of it is `source/videos/20230718_9Sbo04xgRxY.md`). Center City: the Academy of Music, the Bellevue-Stratford, the Land Title Building, the Union League, City Hall, the U.S. Custom House, the Masonic Temple, the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts, the Reading Terminal headhouse and market, the Wanamaker Building, the Witherspoon Building, the Cathedral Basilica of Saints Peter and Paul, First Baptist Church at 17th and Sansom and the Drake Hotel; beyond it the Fairmount Water Works, the Baptist Temple (Temple Performing Arts Center), the Metropolitan Opera House on North Broad Street and College Hall at Penn. Facts come from the Wikipedia articles, read as raw text; hours from the managing bodies' pages (PAFA, Reading Terminal Market, Fairmount Water Works, the Archdiocese's parish page for the cathedral). City Hall's tour times are as web search results quoted the visitor center's pages, which answered 403. Not checked: parking (Center City meters and garages, said so), interiors of private buildings, the Masonic Temple's tour days (its hours page answered 404), who now worships in the old First Baptist Church (Wikipedia says Liberti Church, OpenStreetMap says Resurrection Philadelphia: `conf='mixed'`). Red brick left `?` for twelve places: the articles say "brick" without a colour. Pins are OpenStreetMap building points, all on their buildings on the leaf-off aerial; the Metropolitan Opera House was moved about 50 m onto the theatre. 78 names in the video are "not checked"; many are demolished hotels, banks and Centennial Exposition halls.
