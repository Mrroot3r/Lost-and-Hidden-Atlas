## What this batch is
Nineteen standing buildings in New Haven, from a "videos" session: the names came from one Old World Exploration video (record: source/videos/20251107_NqHcwWIYeSQ.md, 86 names; the captions ran to 45:01 of a 46-minute video, so the whole video was read). The video was used only as a list of names. Added: the three churches on the Green (Center, Trinity, United), City Hall, the county and federal courthouses, the Ives library, the Hotel Taft, Christ Church, St. Mary Church, the old Plymouth Congregational Church, Union Station, the Quinnipiac Brewery, the Yale Peabody Museum, Harkness Tower, Sterling Memorial Library, Woolsey Hall, the Yale Bowl and the Soldiers' and Sailors' Monument on East Rock. Facts are from Wikipedia (read raw), the 1970 Register nomination for the New Haven Green Historic District, the National Park Service's Amistad page, Yale's New Haven Building Archive, and the owners' own pages for hours (Center Church, Trinity, Christ Church, the public library, the Peabody, the Yale Visitor Center). Pins are address lookups (house-number matches) or Wikipedia points, all looked at on the 2023 leaf-off aerial; Plymouth Church and Harkness Tower are site-level (the point fell on the street beside them). NOT checked: United Church's hours (its site would not open; a search result gave Sunday 10 a.m.), St. Mary's Mass times (the parish site is drawn by script and the old St. Mary's domain now carries spam), Union Station's opening hours, Woolsey Hall's concert times, the East Rock summit road hours (the city's site refuses requests; the hours given are Wikipedia's and marked not confirmed, conf='mixed'). Red brick was set to yes for Center Church, United Church and the Ives library from Commons pictures looked at; most others are stone or marble from the sources, and the rest are '?'. Interiors were not checked beyond what the sources describe.
