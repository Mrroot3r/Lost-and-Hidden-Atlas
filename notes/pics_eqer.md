## Where I hesitated
- old-champlain-weighlock-waterford: the photo is an old Erie/Champlain lock near Albany, 674 m from the pin, named by its file. Kept because its description fits a Champlain Canal lock at Waterford. The same photo was rejected for old-champlain-locks-5-6-waterford (2 km off), so the two places are not shown with one picture.
- little-falls-aqueduct-ruins: an 1860s stereo card, "Aqueduct in winter, Little Falls, N.Y.", chosen. The image is hard to read. The Little Falls stereo cards labelled Passaic Falls, New Jersey were left out.
- lock-e8-rotterdam: a steel truss bridge with a lock, 55 m from the pin. It fits the truss of the movable dam, but I could not confirm the dam itself in the picture.
- westerlo-hamlet: a white church with a tower, 340 m from the pin, chosen over the plaque signs, which are close-ups and not used.
- white-creek-historic-district: the Methodist (Baptist) church, 64 m from the pin, chosen as the clearest wide view of the district rather than a view of the whole hamlet.
- huff-indian-village: a stone marker shelter on the prairie, 23 m from the pin, chosen. It is the site marker, not the earthworks, which cannot be seen.
- frontier-town: a 1995 photograph of the park's exterior, when it was still open. It is the only clear exterior that names the place.
- ford-assembly-plant-arabi, crowsnest-museum-coleman-high, west-concord-union-station, leards-range-light-victoria: name matches; positions 28 m or closer, or name-only.
- newmarket-stone-mills: a wide street view with two stone mills, 68 m from the pin; not a close-up.
