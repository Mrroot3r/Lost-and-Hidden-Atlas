## What this batch is
Two registered districts that earlier batches had left out: the Commercial Community Historic District in Lexington and the Dougherty-Prospect Heights Historic District in Liberty, both from Wikipedia's articles read as raw text. The National Register forms are not on line (NPGallery holds only a placeholder for each), so no building inside either district was checked, hours and owners are not given, and both pins mark a district roughly (the Liberty pin was moved from a house onto the street beside it). Brick is "?" for both. Wikimedia Commons had no picture of either district (the Lexington candidates showed the courthouse and the museum, which are separate listings), so none was set.
The ground itself is nearly used up: the Kansas City session was aimed at Missouri country round Kansas City, and by the National Register lists for Cass, Clay, Lafayette and Ray counties every listing with a usable source is already on the Atlas (names were compared with every place list). What is left is address-restricted houses and districts with no readable form. A search of the Atlas by town found Harrisonville, Pleasant Hill, Belton, Peculiar, Raymore, Odessa and Higginsville already covered. The target of 15 to 20 could not be met honestly here.
