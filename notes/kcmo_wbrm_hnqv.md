## What this batch is
Nineteen standing buildings in Kansas City, Missouri, none of which the Atlas had: fifteen West Bottoms warehouses and factories (the Bolt, Nut and Screw works, the Studebaker/Stowe, Deere, Parlin-Orendorff, Keystone Implement, Adams, Lowe Brothers, Advance-Rumely, Avery/Holsum, English-Morse "Ship", Ridenour-Baker, Seavey and Flarsheim, Peppard Seed, Hogue and Abernathy buildings), plus the Cold Storage and Richards and Conover buildings in the River Market, St. Mary's Episcopal Church downtown and the Peppard House on the West Side. The ground was chosen because the Smithville, Plattsburg and Odessa areas, and Lee's Summit, Raytown and Grandview, had already been worked by earlier batches and their National Register lists had almost no gaps left.
Facts come from Clio's West Bottoms Walking Tour pages and its Richards and Conover page, Wikipedia (raw text) for Cold Storage and St. Mary's, and a 2018 Flatland KC article for the Peppard House; the National Register forms could not be read (NPGallery has a placeholder, the Missouri PDFs 404 and web.archive.org is blocked to the fetch tool). Pins are Esri geocoder street addresses looked at on the aerial; two did not geocode (Bolt, Nut and Screw landed on a gas station, Advance-Rumely returned the Studebaker point) and were placed by hand with prec='area'. Not checked: hours of every shop, restaurant, distillery and haunted house (all hours='?'), interiors, and who owns the vacant buildings (Ridenour-Baker, Seavey and Flarsheim). Red brick is from a Commons picture for the Bolt works, Lowe, Seavey, Richards and Conover, St. Mary's and the Peppard House, and from Clio's "red brick" for Abernathy. The Crane Company Building picture used for the Bolt, Nut and Screw entry was chosen because Clio names Crane Bros. as an early tenant of that site; it was not confirmed to be the same building.

## Tips
- Clio's tour pages (theclio.com/tour/3025/<n>, n = 1 to 31) open with tools/page.py --all and give year, builder, later tenants and current use for every West Bottoms building: a fast way to a batch.
- The Esri geocoder gives each West Bottoms address a point on the right block but sometimes the wrong one (a gas station; the same point for two addresses). Look at every pin.
- Of 18 places sent to photo_find, Commons had a likely picture for seven. The Commons photographer Mwkruse photographed most Kansas City Register listings; his files are named for the building.
- Many National Register districts and buildings in "Kansas City other" are plain apartment blocks; the list is a poor guide to what is worth a visit.
