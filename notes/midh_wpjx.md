## What this batch is
Fifteen places in western Orange County, New York, the first pass there (region `midh`, drawn at random by `choose.py --state ny`, which offered Middletown; the Atlas had nothing within 15 km of Port Jervis or of Middletown itself). Port Jervis: Fort Decker (1793 stone house, historical society museum), the 1892 Erie depot, the 1854 Erie turntable (pin moved onto the pit seen on the LiDAR), the 1920s post office, and Laurel Grove Cemetery with the Tri-States and Witness monuments. Deerpark: the D&H Canal Park at Cuddebackville with the Roebling Neversink aqueduct abutments, the 1863 Huguenot Schoolhouse with the 1934 Grange hall, and the Gumaer (Pioneer Knoll) Cemetery of 1713. Middletown: the 1892 O&W station (being restored), the 1930 Paramount Theatre, Grace Episcopal, Christ Church (Universalist, Tiffany window), First Congregational (Isaac Perry, 1872) and Vaux and Olmsted's Hillside Cemetery. Slate Hill: the 1792 Primitive Baptist meeting house with box pews. Sources: Wikipedia through `wiki_raw.py` (and `--rows` for the county Register list), HMdb markers, minisink.org, the Pomeroy Foundation, pickocny.com, Trains, Railfan, the Chronicle, the Pike County Courier, the parish and theatre pages, through `page.py`. Not checked: anything on the ground; hours for the canal park, the Neversink Valley Museum (said to be closed by the county, undated), the Huguenot museum, the post office lobby, both cemeteries and three churches (Christ Church, First Congregational, Brookfield: no page of their own found); whether TOYX's turntable exhibit opens in 2026; the Huguenot pin, which falls in a cluster of small buildings and could not be put on the schoolhouse; the Gumaer pin, on a wooded knoll by a pond where no graves show. One Commons caption calls the Gumaer ground part of "the Neversink Preserve", not followed up. Red brick: O&W station yes (picture); Fort Decker, Brookfield, Gumaer and the turntable no; the rest not settled.
