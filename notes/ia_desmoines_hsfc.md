## What this batch is
Twelve standing places in Des Moines, Iowa, which had four, taken from the buildings named in one video of old photographs and postcards of the town (the record is source/videos/20221002_HenB3UAHug8.md); the video was a tip only and nothing in the entries comes from it. Added: the Masonic Temple, the 1908 post office (now county offices and the Polk County Heritage Gallery), City Hall (closed to the public since January 2026), the Polk County Courthouse, the Court Avenue Bridge with the City Beautiful river walls, the Scottish Rite Consistory, the Successful Farming / Meredith building, the Equitable and Fleming buildings, Roosevelt High School, the Soldiers' and Sailors' Monument on the Capitol grounds and the State Fairgrounds. Sources: Wikipedia read as raw text, with Wikipedia's Register list for Des Moines; SAH Archipedia for the Successful Farming building; the Iowa Legislature's Capitol grounds tour and the Iowa History Journal for the monument; the Heritage Gallery's and the Iowa State Fair's own pages. Not checked: hours for the courthouse and the Heritage Gallery (the gallery's page gives none), parking downtown, interiors, and who owns the Successful Farming building now (Dotdash Meredith kept it in 2022; nothing later was found). Red brick was settled from Wikipedia's pictures for the Equitable building and Roosevelt High School and from a street photograph for the Successful Farming building; the Fleming building's picture is black and white and was left '?'. Nobody has stood at any of these.

## Tips
- Wikipedia's "National Register of Historic Places listings in Polk County, Iowa" holds only the rows outside the city; Des Moines has its own list ("... in Des Moines, Iowa", 193 rows), and `wiki_raw.py --rows` reads it.
- Several Des Moines Register buildings (the post office, City Hall) have no article of their own: they are sections of "Civic Center Historic District (Des Moines, Iowa)", which is what the plain titles redirect to.
