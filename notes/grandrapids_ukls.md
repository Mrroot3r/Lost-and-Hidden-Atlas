## What this batch is
Nine standing buildings in downtown Grand Rapids and Heritage Hill from one walk-through video of old Grand Rapids buildings (Old World Exploration, "Old World Grand Rapids"; the record is source/videos/20241101_iVyr5gTGF2U.md). Added: St. Mark's Episcopal Church (1847-48, the city's oldest church building), the Michigan Trust Company Building (1891-92), the Grand Rapids Trust Building at 77 Monroe Center (1926), the Pantlind Hotel inside the Amway Grand Plaza (1913-15), the Old Federal Building (Woodbridge N. Ferris Building, 1908-11), the Morton Hotel (1923), Central High School (now Innovation Central, 1910-11), the 1915 YMCA building (now the Fitzgerald condominiums) and the Cathedral of Saint Andrew. The video names 34 things: 8 are gone (city hall and courthouse, both 1969; Union Station; the 1879 federal building; the Hotel Cody; the police headquarters; the Masonic Home), 5 were not found standing and 10 were not checked. Sources: Wikipedia through wiki_raw.py; SAH Archipedia (Buildings of Michigan, 2012) entries MI-01-KT4, KT9, KT10, KT12, KT13 and KT23 through page.py; Fox 17 (2014) on the Morton; Rapid Growth Media (2008) and a 1926 county history on the YMCA; service times from St. Mark's and the cathedral's own pages. Not checked: insides, the Amway Grand Plaza's own pages (its home page gave no text and /about was 404), brick for the Pantlind, Morton, Central and the YMCA, parking (downtown ramps and meters, not named). The Pantlind's dates differ (1913 opening in Wikipedia, 1913-1915 in Buildings of Michigan): conf='mixed'.
