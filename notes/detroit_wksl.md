## What this batch is
Three places in Detroit from one walk-through video of Detroit buildings that were torn down (Old World Exploration, "Demolished Detroit"; the record is source/videos/20261106_rt2tQMGQuhw.md). The video names 53 buildings; 49 are gone, Pennsylvania Station is in New York, and three are added: the Cathedral Church of St. Paul (the one standing building it names), the Merrill Fountain (it stood in front of the old opera house and is now in Palmer Park) and the Hurlbut Memorial Gate of Water Works Park (the video shows the park's lost tower). Sources: Wikipedia through wiki_raw.py, HistoricDetroit.org, the cathedral's visit page, a WXYZ report of August 2026 on the fountain, and a Waymarking page for the fountain's position; all pins were looked at on the aerial. Not checked: Palmer Park's hours (the city's page answered 403), street parking at the gate and the cathedral, and whether fencing for the fountain's planned restoration and move (work hoped for in 2027) is up yet. The fountain's dates differ between sources (dedicated 1901 or built 1904; moved 1925 or 1926), so it is marked mixed. The National Theatre's facade was taken down in 2024 and stored for reuse, so it is recorded as gone.
