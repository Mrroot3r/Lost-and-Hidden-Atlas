## What this batch is

Fifteen historic state hospitals and asylums in Pennsylvania (Harrisburg was already on the map): the Kirkbride-plan hospitals at Danville and Warren (standing, active) and Dixmont (gone, the hillside regraded); Kirkbride's own 1859 building in West Philadelphia and Friends Hospital at Frankford (both National Historic Landmarks, standing); Norristown (active, part of the grounds handed over for redevelopment); the training schools at Pennhurst (part standing, a ticketed attraction), Polk (closed 2023, standing) and Laurelton (empty, private, gated); the county asylums that the state took over, Embreeville (demolished, becoming a township park), Retreat (later a prison, vacant), Mayview and Byberry (both gone under new building); Allentown (mostly demolished 2020–21); and Farview, the hospital for the criminally insane, now SCI Waymart. Sources: Wikipedia's articles, the Park Service records for Reed Hall, Kirkbride's Hospital and Friends Hospital (Register forms read for the last two), SAH Archipedia for Danville, Warren and Polk, the Living New Deal, the Department of Corrections, West Bradford Township, and news reports; Asylum Projects only for dates nothing else gave, named in the text where used. Not checked: which of Norristown's 1878–1880 buildings survive; which buildings at Retreat and Farview are from the hospital era; the brick colour at most sites (left '?'); the exact spot of Pennhurst's administration building; Embreeville's demolition year (Asylum Projects says 2022; the 2021–23 aerial shows it under way); Mayview's demolition date (Patch 2012, Wikipedia 2016). No Pennsylvania state hospital in this batch has a Register listing of its own except the three named.

## Tips

- Pennsylvania's state hospitals are almost never on the National Register, so `form.py` has little to read; SAH Archipedia's building pages (sah-archipedia.org/node/…, or the archipediavm.upress.virginia.edu/buildings/PA-… copy) open with the web fetch tool and give architects and materials.
- Wikipedia's API category listing answered with an empty page from here; "Pennsylvania State Hospitals" (the article) lists every institution instead.
- Yahoo News copies of Pennsylvania local papers (Times Leader, Daily Item, Republican & Herald) open with the web fetch tool and give recent status.
