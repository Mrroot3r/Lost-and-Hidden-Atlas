## What this batch is
Twelve standing buildings in Sioux City, Iowa, taken from the buildings named in one walking-tour video about the town (the record is source/videos/20240823_Fx7XzYMzeF0.md); the video was a tip only and nothing in the entries comes from it. Added: the Cathedral of the Epiphany, the Badgerow Building, the Battery Building (Simmons Hardware warehouse, now a casino hotel), City Hall (the 1897 federal building, rebuilt behind its old stone walls in 1995 to 1997), Central High School (the Castle on the Hill), the Morningside College Historic District (pinned on Lewis Hall), the Martin Hotel, the Masonic Temple, the Orpheum Theatre, the Carnegie library, the former First Congregational Church and the Milwaukee Road shops (Sioux City Railroad Museum). Sources: Wikipedia as raw text and the Woodbury County Register list rows; the Sioux City Public Museum's history pages (city halls, Central High School) and its 2020 walking-tour leaflets for Pierce, Nebraska and Fourth streets, which also settled which of the video's other names are gone; the Morningside College Register form; the railroad museum's own page (hours) and KWIT news on its 2024 flood and May 2025 reopening; the Orpheum's ticket page. Not checked: Mass and service times at the cathedral and the former First Congregational Church (the parish and diocese sites, on ecatholic.com, answered 403), City Hall's office hours (the city's page answered 404), the casino hotel's lobby hours, the Masonic Temple's present owner and use, and parking at every downtown place. The Masonic Temple pin is on the street-address point, on a large building; the Register point is 145 m east among smaller ones, and no picture settled which is the temple, so it is marked as the site. Red brick was settled from the article photographs for the cathedral, the Battery Building and the Carnegie library; the rest are "?". Pictures: City Hall and Morningside had no usable Commons photograph and were recorded as none.
