# Pictures batch yphg (22 November 2026)

What this batch is: a pictures job. No new places and no pins. It looked for a photograph for places already on the map that had none, using tools/photo_find.py next 5 and choosing by looking at each sheet. The pictures file is source/tools/pics_yphg.py.

Counts:
- 10 places got a picture (chosen by looking).
- 8 places were given none by me, each with the reason in the file.
- 11 places were recorded as none by the tool itself, because Wikimedia Commons has nothing for them near the pin, under their names or in a category of their own.
- 29 places in all had a look, which is close to the 30 the job asked for. Four rounds were run.

Where I hesitated (each is a judgement from the picture, not a checked fact):
- Calvary Episcopal, Burnt Hills: the name only partly matches, but the file names Burnt Hills, New York, and the white board-and-batten church with its tower fits the description. Chosen.
- Brownville Methodist, Brownville (ne): by name only. The caption says Brownville, Nebraska, and dates the church 1861, where the place says 1859. Chosen as the same church.
- Byram's Ford: a brick monument with plaques, seen wide with the road. It is a marker, not the ford itself. Chosen as the clearest view of the site.
- Picher: the water tower that reads PICHER was chosen over a derelict building on Main Street. Chosen.
- Central Warehouse, Albany: a wide view from Livingston Avenue was chosen over close-ups of windows. Chosen.
- Garden of the Gulf, Montague (PEI): the red brick building on the hill across the river is a judgement. The caption does not name the building. Chosen, but a person should look.
- Seneca Mills Falls: the photograph is geotagged about 4 km from the pin but its name matches exactly and it is in Yates County, New York. Chosen. A person should check it is the falls at this site.
- Square Deal Arch, Endicott: an arch on New York Route 17C, found by name only. Chosen.
- Montgomery Birthplace, New London (PEI): the white house with green trim has "greengables" in its file name, and the picture looks like the birthplace. Chosen, but a person should look.
- Wolseley Court House: the yellow brick building was chosen over a close-up of a plaque in the first candidate. Its identity as the court house is inferred from the plaque photo. Chosen.
- Daniels (Patapsco mill village): a weir named Daniel Dam, 214 m from the pin, was declined. It shows no village building.
- Oglethorpe County Jail, Lexington: the only candidate was a Main Street view with no jail in it. Declined.
- Grand Encampment Museum: the only building candidate was the Grand Encampment Opera House, a different building in the town. Declined.
- Old Spanish chimney, St Augustine: the chimney photographs were a New Orleans attic and an English house. Declined.
- Peebles Island earthworks: no candidate shows earthworks. Declined.
- Saskatoon CPR station: the only station photograph was of the CNR station, which was demolished in the 1960s. Declined.
- Uncle Sam Wilson site, Troy: other buildings only. Declined.

Not checked: the tool's pictures from Commons were not re-verified beyond what the sheets show. The building in a few pictures was not confirmed against the place's own page.

Tips for the next session: the sheets sometimes show only a caption that cuts off the description, so it is worth reading the place's description from the command output before choosing. The tool waits for Commons; a round of five takes several minutes.
