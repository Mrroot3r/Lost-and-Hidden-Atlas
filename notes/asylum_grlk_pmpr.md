## What this batch is

Fifteen state hospitals and asylums of Michigan, Wisconsin and Minnesota for the themed pass, beside the Traverse City and Kalamazoo entries already on the map. Michigan: the Eastern Michigan Asylum site at Pontiac (gone, 2000), the Kay Beard Building at Eloise in Westland (standing, red brick, now a seasonal haunted attraction) and the Newberry State Hospital (red brick Georgian Revival, partly standing inside a state prison). Wisconsin: Mendota with the Wisconsin Memorial Hospital group (the Kirkbride is gone), the Northern Hospital for the Insane at Winnebago (Kirkbride gone, cemetery on Asylum Point), the Wisconsin Home for the Feeble-Minded at Chippewa Falls (one 1897 cottage and a barn; the administration building was razed in 2021), the Southern Wisconsin Home at Union Grove (cream brick on red brick bases), the Central State Hospital for the Criminally Insane at Waupun (red brick Prairie School, now Dodge Correctional) and the Milwaukee County Asylum grounds in Wauwatosa (fieldstone stairs and garden remains in County Grounds Park). Minnesota: the St. Peter Center Building (limestone, museum by appointment), the Fergus Falls Kirkbride (standing, closed), the Rochester State Hospital cemetery and caves in Quarry Hill Park, Anoka (cottages), Willmar (stucco and red tile, now an office campus) and Moose Lake (1938 PWA brick, now a prison). Sources: Wikipedia, the Park Service's Register records and forms (Union Grove, Waupun, Memorial Hospital, St. Peter, Willmar), Wisconsin Historical Society property records, SAH Archipedia, MNopedia, KirkbrideBuildings.com, Asylum Projects, HMdb, the Cultural Landscape Foundation, WUWM, the Quarry Hill Nature Center, Placeography, the Living New Deal, state corrections and health pages, and news (AP, WWJ, New Ulm Journal, Star Tribune, Perham Focus, KVRR, Newberry News, WKFR). Not checked: what has happened at Fergus Falls since 2018; which Newberry and Moose Lake buildings are original; which Chippewa Falls buildings are in use; where the Anoka cottages, the Mendota Memorial Hospital group and the Winnebago Kirkbride stood (those pins are area); Eloise's opening times; park hours at Quarry Hill and County Grounds Park; brick colour at Mendota, Chippewa Falls, Anoka, Willmar and Moose Lake.

## Tips

- Wikipedia's coordinate for a state campus can beat the OpenStreetMap point: at Mendota the OSM hospital point fell in woods and the geocoder sent 816 Troy Drive into a housing street 1.4 km away.
- The Wisconsin Historical Society's property records (wisconsinhistory.org/Records/Property/HI…) give architect, wall material and demolition year building by building, and the web fetch tool reads them.
- The National Register form for the Southern Wisconsin Home (91001394) has a good history of the Chippewa Falls home as well.
- The Perham Focus blocks the web fetch tool (robots) and page.py could not connect.
