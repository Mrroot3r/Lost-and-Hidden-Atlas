# Pictures batch vjsy (2026-11-22, scheduled pictures run)

What this batch is: photographs for places already on the map that had none. No new places, no pins, no text. Only tools/pics_vjsy.py (the tool wrote it) and this note.

Counts:
- 7 places got a picture chosen by looking at the sheet (dorset-quarry, hotel-kaaterskill, batchellerville-presbyterian-church, first-presbyterian-port-gibson, ministers-island-covenhoven, old-whalers-church-sag-harbor, starks-knob).
- 6 places were looked at and none chosen (liberty-street-presbyterian-site-troy, heywood-wakefield, steamboat-warehouse-bismarck, placerville-historic-district-idaho, norwich-dlw-gatemans-tower, millstone-hill-quarries).
- 5 places were recorded as none by the tool itself, because Commons had nothing near the pin, under its names or in a category of its own (coverdale-united-church, hamden-district-1-school, hoyt-house-the-point, indian-fields, lainhart-farm-altamont).
- 2 places were not reached before the time limit and have no record: illinois-central-depot-sioux-falls and silversmith-powerhouse-sandon.

Where the session hesitated:
- dorset-quarry: the chosen picture (candidate 12, Mcvoorhis, CC BY-SA 3.0) is 3 km from the pin and names the Norcross-West quarry on route 30 in Dorset, which the file's description gives as the oldest marble quarry in the USA. It shows a flooded quarry pit. A person should confirm it is the same pit as the Atlas place.
- first-presbyterian-port-gibson: the chosen picture (Calstanhope, CC BY-SA 4.0) is a pink church with a tall spire; its description names First Presbyterian (1860) and the steeple. The gilded hand is not clearly visible at thumbnail size. A person should check the spire against the place.
- batchellerville-presbyterian-church: a pre-1923 postcard of the church before it was moved in 1930-31; the file name only partly matches. Its description fits the place.
- starks-knob: the chosen picture (Henry Bellagnome, CC BY-SA 2.0) is an igneous rock outcrop near the knob, 127 m from the pin, not a view of the summit. Weakest of the seven.
- old-whalers-church-sag-harbor and ministers-island-covenhoven: good matches (exterior views, full name match). Ministers Island candidates 2 to 6 were interiors and were not chosen.
- hotel-kaaterskill: a Detroit Publishing Company postcard (public domain) of the hotel from afar; the file name only partly matches.
- heywood-wakefield: rejected the Big Chair photograph (a Gardner landmark 214 m away, not the works).
- millstone-hill-quarries: rejected a lichen close-up and two aerial survey images, since an aerial is not how someone arriving would see the quarry hill.
- norwich-dlw-gatemans-tower: the only candidate was a train depot in Norwich, England.
