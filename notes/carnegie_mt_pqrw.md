## What this batch is

Carnegie libraries of Montana (16 places): the 15 that stand and the site of the Great Falls library, torn down in 1965 with the present library on the same ground. Sources: Wikipedia's state list and articles, the Montana Historical Society's sign pages (historicmt.org), Montana Public Radio on Kate Hampton's 2019 book, and the libraries', counties', cities' and museums' own pages. Montana had 17 grants and no academic ones. Hours only from the operators' pages.

## What was not checked

- Chinook and Malta: what the buildings are used for today was not found; both are marked 'look from the street'.
- Havre and Bozeman: owned by private people named in the sources; names left out.
- Kalispell: the museum's site is titled Glacier Art Museum but does not say outright that it replaced the Hockaday name.
- Hours not found for Livingston (its page refused automated reading), Havre, Miles City.
- Several grants and dates differ between Wikipedia's list and the state's sign pages; both are given in the text.
- Bozeman, Chinook, Havre and Malta pins are 'area'.

## Leads not finished

- Glasgow (torn down 1965): the site was not found.
- Architects not found for Chinook, Fort Benton, Hamilton and Miles City.
