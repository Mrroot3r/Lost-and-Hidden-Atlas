## What this batch is
An "hours" session (PROMPTS.md type 7): no new places and no pins. New York had one church left waiting (Dundee); the rest came from `tools/hours.py list "<state>" --kind worship` for the states with the most churches still waiting. Twenty-seven were looked up: ten got times or a visiting arrangement from the page of whoever runs them, read in the session; seventeen are recorded `?`.

Hours recorded (address or town on each page checked against the place):
- St. Mark's Anglican, Kaslo: Anglican Diocese of Kootenay page (601 5th Street on the page). Open.
- St. Andrew's United, Kaslo: the St. Andrew's Kaslo Heritage Hall site (services monthly in winter, weekly in summer, dates on its calendar). Open.
- Zion Episcopal, Charles Town: its own site (301 E. Congress Street). Open.
- St. Peter's Catholic, Harpers Ferry: St. James parish, Charles Town, page "Chapel of St. Peter" (Sunday Mass 9:30). Open.
- Old Narragansett Church, Wickford: St. Paul's Wickford "Our Campus" page (docent tours in summer); its "Services" page says August worship is often held there. Open.
- Our Lady, Queen of Heaven, Oreana: a Diocese of Boise story giving Mass the second Saturday monthly at 10 a.m.; the story's date was not checked, so the time may be old. Open.
- Oakland Memorial Chapel, Alcorn State: the university's Religious Life page (Mass Sundays 5 p.m., interdenominational Thursdays 5:30). Not marked open: the page does not say outsiders are welcome and the place note says to ask the university.
- Six Principle Baptist Church, North Kingstown: Stony Lane Church's history page says the congregation built a new worship building in 1984-89; the 10:30 Sunday time is for that building, so the old meeting house is not marked open.
- Wheelock Mission Church, Millerton: Choctaw Nation Cultural Services page offers tours of the academy museum and grounds on request; says nothing of the church's interior. Not marked open.

Not checked: nothing was seen on the ground and no telephone call was made.
