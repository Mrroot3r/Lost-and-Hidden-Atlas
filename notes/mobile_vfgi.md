## What this batch is
Thirteen standing buildings in Mobile, Alabama, all named in a video tour of the city's old buildings (Old World Exploration, January 1, 2026; the record of all 41 names it gave is source/videos/20260101_5HwuXBnLpzk.md). The video was a tip only. Added: Barton Academy, the Battle House Hotel, the Cathedral Basilica of the Immaculate Conception (it was a row in LEADS.md, now deleted), Old City Hall and Southern Market, Government Street Presbyterian Church, the Gulf, Mobile and Ohio terminal, the U.S. Marine Hospital, St. Francis Street Methodist Church (The Steeple), the Van Antwerp Building, St. Vincent de Paul (Prince of Peace), the Spring Hill College Quadrangle, the Convent of the Visitation, and the closed St. Joseph's church on Springhill Avenue. Sources: Wikipedia read as raw text; the National Register records and forms for the Visitation convent and the Spring Hill quadrangle (form.py); the Visitation Monastery's chapel page; Government Street Presbyterian's own page (read with the web fetch tool); the National Trust's 2024 story on Barton Academy; the Encyclopedia of Alabama on the history museum. Pins were checked on the aerial; Barton Academy and St. Vincent de Paul were moved onto the buildings. Not checked: anything on the ground, parking (downtown street parking assumed and said so), red brick for most (left '?'), present use of the Marine Hospital, the owner of St. Joseph's since 2018, Mass times for the cathedral and Prince of Peace, the history museum's hours and the Battle House's.
