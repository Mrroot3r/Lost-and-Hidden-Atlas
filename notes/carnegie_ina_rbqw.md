## What this batch is

Indiana's Carnegie libraries for towns A to K, part of the owner's pass to put every Carnegie- and Rockefeller-funded building on the map. 57 places: public libraries on Wikipedia's state list that could be placed from pages read in this session, plus DePauw University's Carnegie library (Emison Art Center). Six are gone (Albion, Columbus, Elkhart, Cherry Street in Evansville, Fort Wayne, Gary Central) and one site in Indianapolis (Madison Avenue) went under Interstate 70. Hours come only from the operator's own page; where none was found the place says hours='?'.
